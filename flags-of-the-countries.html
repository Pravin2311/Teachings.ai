🌍 Parent Tip: Spark Curiosity About the World

After scratching and revealing a country, open a globe or map and let your child point to it! This builds geography awareness and cultural curiosity.

🎮 How to Play: Flags Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the grey card to reveal a country's flag.
  2. Listen to the name of the country.
  3. Tap Next to discover more flags from around the world.

What your child practices

Flags are a fun way into geography because they are bright and memorable. Children practice noticing colors, shapes and patterns, such as stripes, stars and circles, and connect each flag with a country name.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask your child to describe the flag before it is named: what colors and shapes can they see?
  • Draw and color a favorite flag together, or design a flag for your family.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which flags are included?

Flags include India, China, the United States, Brazil, Japan, Türkiye, Mexico, Canada, Russia, France and others.

What age is the flags game for?

It suits children from about 4 to 9. Younger children enjoy the colors and older ones can try to remember the countries.

How can we learn more about these countries?

Find each country on a map, and visit the country learning pages for facts about its landmarks, food and language.
