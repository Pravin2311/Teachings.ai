Learn Fruits

🍎 Parent Tip: Connect Learning to Healthy Habits

After playing, explore fruits together at home or the grocery store. Ask your child: “Which fruit is red and crunchy?” or “Can you find a banana?” This builds vocabulary, encourages healthy eating, and turns learning into real-life fun for preschool and kindergarten kids.
