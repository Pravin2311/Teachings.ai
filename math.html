➕ Parent Tip: Make Math Part of Daily Life

Reinforce math skills by counting everyday items: “How many spoons are on the table?” or “You ate 2 grapes, now eat 1 more—how many total?” This builds number sense, addition fluency, and problem-solving confidence in preschool and kindergarten kids.
