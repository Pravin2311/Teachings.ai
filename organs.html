🧠 Parent Tip:
Teach Body Awareness & Science Skills

Encourage your child to name the organ, describe its job (e.g., 'The brain helps us think'), and point to where it is in their body. Ask questions like “What does your stomach do?” or “Why do we need lungs?” to deepen understanding. This builds science vocabulary and body confidence!
