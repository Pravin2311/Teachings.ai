🌍 Parent Tip: Spark Curiosity About the World

Turn country learning into a global adventure! After playing, show your child where the country is on a map or globe. Ask, “Have you heard of France?” or “What language do they speak in Japan?” This builds geography awareness, cultural curiosity, and early social studies skills in preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Countries Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the card to uncover a picture of a country.
  2. Listen to the country's name.
  3. Tap Next to travel to another country, from India and Brazil to Japan and Egypt.

What your child practices

This is a first introduction to the idea that the world is made of many countries, each with its own name, landmarks and culture. It builds geography vocabulary and curiosity, which children can then connect to a map or a globe.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Find each country on a map or globe after it is revealed.
  • Ask whether your child knows anyone who lives in, or comes from, that country.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries are included?

The game includes India, China, America, Brazil, Japan, Australia, Canada, Egypt and France, plus Africa, which is a continent made up of many countries.

What age is it for?

It works well from about 4 to 8, as a fun first look at world geography.

Where can we learn more about each country?

Each country has a learning page with facts about its location, landmarks, food, capital and language.
