Learn Animals

🐾 Parent Tip:
Boost Vocabulary & Listening Skills

Encourage your child to name the animal, mimic its sound, and describe where it lives (e.g., farm, jungle, ocean). This builds vocabulary, strengthens memory, and supports early science learning. Ask questions like “What does a lion say?” or “Where do ducks live?” to make learning interactive and fun!
