🐾 Parent Tip:
Boost Vocabulary & Listening Skills

Encourage your child to name the animal, mimic its sound, and describe where it lives (e.g., farm, jungle, ocean). This builds vocabulary, strengthens memory, and supports early science learning. Ask questions like “What does a lion say?” or “Where do ducks live?” to make learning interactive and fun!

🎮 How to Play: Animals Scratch Card

  1. Rub your finger or mouse over the grey card to scratch it away.
  2. When enough is uncovered, the animal appears and you hear its name, then the sound it makes.
  3. Tap Next or Previous to scratch a new animal, from lions and elephants to kangaroos and dolphins.

What your child practices

This game links three things at once: what an animal looks like, what it is called and the sound it makes. Hearing the name right after seeing the picture builds vocabulary, and guessing the animal before it is fully uncovered is great practice for observation and prediction.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Pause before the picture is fully revealed and ask, "Who do you think it is?"
  • After each animal, ask where it lives: on a farm, in the jungle, in the ocean or in the snow.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What age is the animals scratch game for?

It suits children from about 2 to 6. Toddlers enjoy scratching and hearing the sounds, and older children can say the name and spell it before it is spoken.

Does the game play animal sounds?

Yes. After the picture is revealed, it says the animal's name and then plays a recording of the animal's real sound, such as a lion's roar.

How can I extend the activity away from the screen?

Play "guess the animal" by making a sound and letting your child guess, or sort toy animals into farm, wild and sea groups.
