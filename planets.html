🪐 Parent Tip: Spark a Love for Space

After playing, explore the night sky together. Point to the moon and stars and ask, “Can you see the moon tonight?” or “Which planet is the biggest?” This builds curiosity about science, space, and our solar system—perfect for preschool and kindergarten explorers.

🎮 How to Play: Planets Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the grey card to reveal a planet or the Moon.
  2. Listen to its name.
  3. Tap Next to travel to another planet in our solar system.

What your child practices

Children learn the names of the planets and start to notice how different they are, from red Mars to ringed Saturn. It is a gateway to bigger ideas: that Earth is one planet of many, and that all of them travel around the Sun.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Put the planets in order from the Sun after playing, using a song or a rhyme.
  • Look for the Moon in the sky tonight and talk about its shape.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which planets are in the game?

The game includes the whole Solar System, plus Mercury, Earth, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto.

Is Pluto a planet?

Pluto is called a dwarf planet. Scientists changed its category in 2006 because it is much smaller than the eight main planets.

Where can we learn more about each planet?

Each planet has a learning page with facts about its size, weather and moons.
