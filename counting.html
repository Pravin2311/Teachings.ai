🔢 Parent Tip: Turn Counting Into Everyday Fun

Reinforce number skills by counting real objects at home: toys, stairs, fruits, or buttons! Ask “How many apples are in the bowl?” or “Can you count your blocks?” This hands-on practice builds number sense, one-to-one correspondence, and early math confidence in preschool and kindergarten kids.
