🏛️ Community Helpers Lesson Plan

🎯 ObjectiveStudents will name at least three community helper roles and describe how each one helps the community
📊 Grade LevelKindergarten–Grade 1
⏱️ Duration40 minutes
📚 SubjectSocial Studies — Community

📦 Materials

Needed for This Lesson

  • Pictures or simple props representing a few community helpers (firefighter hat, doctor's stethoscope, mail carrier bag, etc. — improvised is fine)
  • Blank paper and crayons
  • Chart paper or whiteboard

🎬 Procedure

Warm-Up (5 minutes)

Ask: "Who are some people who help us in our community?" List student answers on the board without judging completeness — this surfaces what students already know.

I Do: Direct Instruction (10 minutes)

Introduce 3-4 community helper roles (firefighter, doctor, mail carrier, teacher) one at a time, showing a picture or prop for each and explaining specifically how that person helps others — not just their job title, but the actual help they provide ("firefighters keep us safe by putting out fires and rescuing people").

We Do: Guided Practice (15 minutes)

As a class, do a simple role-play: one student wears or holds a prop for a community helper while the class asks "what do you do to help us?" and the student (with teacher support) answers in character. Rotate through a few different roles.

You Do: Independent Practice (7 minutes)

Students draw a picture of a community helper of their choice and, with adult support if needed, write or dictate one sentence describing how that person helps the community.

Wrap-Up (3 minutes)

A few students share their drawing and sentence with the class, building a shared understanding of the range of helper roles covered.

✅ Assessment

Check that the drawn helper is correctly identified and that the accompanying sentence describes a real, specific way that role helps the community, rather than just restating the job title.

🎚️ Differentiation

Support: Provide a sentence starter ("A firefighter helps by ___") for the independent writing task. Extension: Ask advanced students to name a community helper not already discussed in class and explain their role.
