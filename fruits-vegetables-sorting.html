🎮 How to Play: Fruits & Vegetables Sorting

  1. Read each food word.
  2. Drag it into the Fruit group or the Vegetable group.
  3. Tap Submit to check, then Try Again for a new mix.

What your child practices

Children practice sorting foods into groups and learn new food words. Some foods are tricky, like the tomato, which scientists call a fruit but cooks often call a vegetable, which makes a great conversation starter.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Sort real fruits and vegetables from your shopping bag.
  • Explain that fruits usually have seeds inside, and cut one open to check.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is a tomato a fruit or a vegetable?

Botanists call a tomato a fruit because it grows from a flower and has seeds, but in cooking it is usually treated as a vegetable.

What age is this game for?

It is designed for children from about 4 to 7.

How can this encourage healthy eating?

Talking about foods and sorting them makes them familiar, and children are often more willing to try foods they know.
