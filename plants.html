🌱 Learn About Plants

🌿 Parent Tip: Grow Learning Alongside Plants

After playing, plant seeds together—like beans or sunflowers—and watch them grow. Ask your child: “What do plants need to grow?” or “Can you measure how tall it is?” This builds science skills, patience, and a love for nature in preschool and kindergarten kids.

🎮 How to Play: Plants Explorer

  1. Look at the plant on the card and tap to hear its name.
  2. Tap Next and Previous to see more plants, like the sunflower, cactus, lotus and bamboo.
  3. Talk about where each plant grows and what it needs.

What your child practices

Children learn the names of well-known plants and start to notice how different plants are suited to different places, like the cactus in the desert and the lotus in a pond. It is an early step into plant science.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Plant a fast-growing seed, such as a bean, and measure it every few days.
  • Ask "Does this plant need lots of water or very little?"

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which plants are in the game?

The game features the sunflower, rose, cactus, bamboo, fern, lotus and tulip.

How can we turn this into a science activity?

Grow a bean in a clear cup with damp paper towel so you can see the roots and shoot develop.

Where can we read more about plants?

Each plant has a learning page explaining how it grows and where it is found.
