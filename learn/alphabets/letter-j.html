Letter J: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

J is the tenth letter of the English alphabet. It comes after I and before K. J is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is J and the small (lowercase) letter is j.

On this page you will learn the sound J makes, how to write capital J and small j, words that use J, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does J Make?

  • j: made by pushing your lips forward a little and buzzing, with your voice switched on. Examples: jam, jump, jelly, jet.

Practice the sound with pictures on J is for Jug, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital J and Small j

Capital J

  1. Many children start with a short line across the top.
  2. Draw a straight line down from the middle of the top line.
  3. Curve the bottom to the left to make a hook.

Small j

  1. Start at the middle line. Draw a straight line down, going below the bottom line.
  2. Curve the end to the left to make a hook.
  3. Put a dot above it.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

J also appears in the middle or at the end of words: pajamas, enjoy, banjo, object.

Common Mix-Ups

Lowercase j is sometimes written backwards, curving to the right. The hook of j always curves to the left.

Interesting Facts About J

  • J is a consonant.
  • J was the last letter to be added to our alphabet. It used to be a fancy way of writing I.
  • English words almost never end in J. Words that end with a j sound use -ge or -dge instead, as in cage and bridge.

Classroom Activity

Spread jam on crackers together while chanting "J is for jam!" Then line up toy jeeps or cars for a "J parade," calling out other J words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Make a simple jelly or jam sandwich together, talking about how "jelly" and "jam" both start with the J sound. Afterward, trace the letter J on paper, pointing out its curved hook shape.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child confuses lowercase j and i, point out that j has a curved tail that hooks below the line, while i is just a straight line with a dot.
Teachers: J is a great letter for a cooking or snack-time activity — jam, jelly, and juice are all easy, safe classroom foods to pair with the letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is J in the alphabet?

J is the tenth letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after I and before K.

Is J a vowel or a consonant?

J is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter J make?

J makes this sound: j as in jam.

What are some words that start with J?

Words that start with J include jam, jelly, jet, juice, jacket, jungle.

Why don't English words end in J?

It is a spelling pattern of English. The j sound at the end of a word is written -ge (cage, huge) or -dge (badge, bridge).

Summary

J is the tenth letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sound is j. Practice writing capital J and small j, and look for J in words around you. Next, learn the letter K.
