Letter J for Kids

The letter J is the tenth letter of the alphabet. Capital J looks like J and small j looks like j. The letter J makes a “juh” sound.

Words That Start With J

Jug
Jam
Jeep
Jelly
Juice

Learning J words helps children recognize the jumping sound of J in English.

How To Write Letter J

To write capital J, draw a long curve with a small line on top. To write small j, draw a short curve and add a dot.

Fun Facts About Letter J

  • J is one of the last letters added to the alphabet.
  • Many joyful words start with J.

Practice Letter J

Kids can trace J, match J words, and color J pictures.

The Letter J Sound

Letter J makes a "juh" sound, similar to the soft G sound in giraffe. It's made by touching the tongue to the roof of the mouth and releasing with a light buzz.

Classroom Activity

Spread jam on crackers together while chanting "J is for jam!" Then line up toy jeeps or cars for a "J parade," calling out other J words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Make a simple jelly or jam sandwich together, talking about how "jelly" and "jam" both start with the J sound. Afterward, trace the letter J on paper, pointing out its curved hook shape.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child confuses lowercase j and i, point out that j has a curved tail that hooks below the line, while i is just a straight line with a dot.
Teachers: J is a great letter for a cooking or snack-time activity — jam, jelly, and juice are all easy, safe classroom foods to pair with the letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter J make?

Letter J makes a "juh" sound, similar to the soft G sound in giraffe, made by touching the tongue to the roof of the mouth and releasing with a light buzz.

What are some easy words that start with J for preschoolers?

Easy J words for preschoolers include jug, jam, jeep, jelly, and juice — food and vehicle words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter J?

Practice a long curved line ending with a hook, topped by a short horizontal line, for capital J. Lowercase j is a short curve with a hook, plus a dot on top.

Why does J look similar to the letter I?

Lowercase j and i both have a dot on top, which can be confusing at first. J has a curved tail that hooks below the writing line, while i is just a short straight line.

What activities help kids remember the letter J?

Spreading jam on crackers while saying "J is for jam," tracing J in sand or shaving cream, playing with toy jeeps, or coloring pictures of J words while saying each one aloud.
