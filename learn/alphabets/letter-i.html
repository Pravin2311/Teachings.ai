The letter I is the ninth letter of the alphabet. Capital I looks like I and small i looks like i. The letter I makes a short “ih” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter I worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Insect

Letter I helps children learn vowel sounds clearly and confidently.

How To Write Letter I

To write capital I, draw a straight line with small lines on top and bottom. To write small i, draw a short line and add a dot.

Fun Facts About Letter I

  • I is a vowel.
  • I is very easy to recognize.
  • "I" is also a word all by itself — it's how you talk about yourself!
  • The letter I can sound different in different words, like the long "eye" sound in kite and the short "ih" sound in sit.

Practice Letter I

Kids can trace I, color I pictures, and find I words around them.

Classroom Activity

Fill a bin with ice cubes (or pretend ice made from foam) and let children touch and describe them while chanting "I is for ice!" Then draw insects together as a class and name each bug's parts.

Homeschool Activity

Since fewer words start with I, get creative — freeze small toys in ice cubes and let your child watch them melt while saying "I is for ice." Then trace the letter I together, pointing out its simple straight-line shape.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Letter I has fewer everyday example words than most letters, so lean on pictures and hands-on play (like ice or insects) rather than just word lists.
Teachers: Point out that "I" is also a word by itself — it's a fun, memorable fact that also introduces the idea that letters can be words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter I make?

Letter I makes two main sounds: the short i sound heard in sit and insect, and the long i sound heard in kite and island. Preschoolers usually learn the short i sound first.

What are some easy words that start with I for preschoolers?

Easy I words for preschoolers include ice, ink, insect, iron, and island — pairing them with pictures helps since fewer common words start with I than most letters.

How do I teach my child to write the letter I?

Practice one straight vertical line with a small horizontal line on top and bottom for capital I. Lowercase i is just a short vertical line with a dot above it.

Why is I called a vowel?

I is one of the five vowels (a, e, i, o, u). It's also a complete word on its own, used to talk about yourself — a fun, easy fact that helps kids remember it.

What activities help kids remember the letter I?

Tracing I in sand or shaving cream, playing with ice cubes while saying "I is for ice," drawing a picture of an insect, or coloring pictures of I words while saying each one aloud.
