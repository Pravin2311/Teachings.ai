Letter H for Kids

The letter H is the eighth letter of the alphabet. Capital H looks like H and small h looks like h. The letter H makes a gentle “huh” sound.

Words That Start With H

Hat
Hen
House
Horse
Honey

The letter H helps children learn soft breathing sounds in English words.

How To Write Letter H

To write capital H, draw two straight lines and connect them. To write small h, draw a long line and add a curve.

Fun Facts About Letter H

  • H is often silent in some words.
  • H is easy to write using straight lines.

Practice Letter H

Kids can trace H, say H words aloud, and spot H in books.
