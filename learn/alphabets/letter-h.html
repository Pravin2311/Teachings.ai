Letter H: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

H is the eighth letter of the English alphabet. It comes after G and before I. H is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is H and the small (lowercase) letter is h.

On this page you will learn the sounds H makes, how to write capital H and small h, words that use H, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does H Make?

  • h: a soft puff of air, like fogging a mirror. Examples: hat, house, hen, hand.
  • teams with other letters: h joins other letters to make new sounds. Examples: sh (ship), ch (chair), th (thumb), wh (whale).
  • silent h: no sound in a few words. Examples: hour, honest, ghost.

Practice the sound with pictures on H is for Hat, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital H and Small h

Capital H

  1. Draw a straight line down on the left.
  2. Draw a second straight line down on the right.
  3. Draw a line across the middle to join them.

Small h

  1. Draw a tall straight line from the top line down to the bottom line.
  2. Go back up to the middle.
  3. Make a hump over to the right and straight down.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

H also appears in the middle or at the end of words: fish, ship, chair, teeth, elephant.

Common Mix-Ups

Lowercase h can be confused with n. The h has a tall line on the left that reaches the top line; n is short.

Interesting Facts About H

  • H is a consonant.
  • H is very good at teaming up: sh, ch, th and wh all use H to make new sounds.
  • In hour and honest, the H is silent, which is why we say "an hour" instead of "a hour".

Classroom Activity

Set out a pile of hats and let each child try one on while the class chants "H is for hat!" Then have everyone trot around the room like a horse, calling out other H words they can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Hold a hand close to your child's mouth while they say "huh" — they should feel warm breath but no buzzing or vibration, unlike sounds like B or V. Afterward, trace the letter H together on paper.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since H is just breath with no vocal sound, some kids find it easier to learn by whispering H words first before saying them at a normal volume.
Teachers: Letter H pairs well with a "house" theme — hat, house, and horse are all common classroom picture-card words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is H in the alphabet?

H is the eighth letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after G and before I.

Is H a vowel or a consonant?

H is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter H make?

H makes these sounds: h as in hat; teams with other letters as in sh (ship); silent h as in hour.

What are some words that start with H?

Words that start with H include hat, house, horse, hen, hand, heart.

Why do we say "an hour" but "a horse"?

We use "an" before a vowel sound. The h in hour is silent, so the word starts with a vowel sound, but the h in horse is spoken.

Summary

H is the eighth letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are h, teams with other letters, silent h. Practice writing capital H and small h, and look for H in words around you. Next, learn the letter I.
