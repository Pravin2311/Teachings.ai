Letter H Worksheet for Kids (Free Printable PDF)

The letter H is the eighth letter of the alphabet. Capital H looks like H and small h looks like h. The letter H makes a gentle “huh” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter H worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Words That Start With H

Hat
Hen
House
Horse
Honey

The letter H helps children learn soft breathing sounds in English words.

How To Write Letter H

To write capital H, draw two straight lines and connect them. To write small h, draw a long line and add a curve.

Fun Facts About Letter H

  • H is often silent in some words.
  • H is easy to write using straight lines.

Practice Letter H

Kids can trace H, say H words aloud, and spot H in books.

The Letter H Sound

Letter H makes a gentle "huh" sound — just a breath out through an open mouth, with no vocal sound at all. That makes it a little trickier for kids to feel than letters like B or D, since there's no clear mouth shape to copy.

Classroom Activity

Set out a pile of hats and let each child try one on while the class chants "H is for hat!" Then have everyone trot around the room like a horse, calling out other H words they can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Hold a hand close to your child's mouth while they say "huh" — they should feel warm breath but no buzzing or vibration, unlike sounds like B or V. Afterward, trace the letter H together on paper.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since H is just breath with no vocal sound, some kids find it easier to learn by whispering H words first before saying them at a normal volume.
Teachers: Letter H pairs well with a "house" theme — hat, house, and horse are all common classroom picture-card words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter H make?

Letter H makes a gentle "huh" sound, made by breathing out through an open mouth with no vocal sound at all — it's really just breath.

What are some easy words that start with H for preschoolers?

Easy H words for preschoolers include hat, hen, house, horse, and honey — object and animal words a child already knows.

How do I teach my child to write the letter H?

Practice two straight vertical lines, then a short horizontal line connecting them in the middle for capital H. Lowercase h is a tall line with a small curve, or "hump," attached partway down on the right.

Why is the letter H sometimes silent?

H is silent in some English words, like hour and honest, usually because the word originally came from French or Latin — worth mentioning to older preschoolers once the basic H sound is solid.

What activities help kids remember the letter H?

Trying on different hats while saying "H is for hat," tracing H in sand or shaving cream, pretending to be a horse trotting around the room, or coloring pictures of H words while saying each one aloud.
