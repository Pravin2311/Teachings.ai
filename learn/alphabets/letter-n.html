Letter N Worksheet for Kids (Free Printable PDF)

The letter N is the fourteenth letter of the alphabet. Capital N looks like N and small n looks like n. The letter N makes a “nnn” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter N worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Words That Start With N

Nest
Nose
Net
Nurse
Night

The letter N helps children learn nose and tongue sounds clearly.

How To Write Letter N

To write capital N, draw two straight lines and one slant line. To write small n, draw one hump.

Fun Facts About Letter N

  • N is used in many common words.
  • N sounds through the nose.

Practice Letter N

Kids can trace N, color N objects, and read N words.

The Letter N Sound

Letter N makes a "nnn" sound, made by touching the tongue to the roof of the mouth while air flows through the nose instead of the mouth. Try placing a finger on the side of your nose while saying it — you'll feel a light buzz.

Classroom Activity

Have children touch their own noses while chanting "N is for nose!" Then build a pretend bird's nest together using twigs, yarn, or paper strips, naming other N words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Look for a real bird's nest outside, or watch a picture of one, while saying "N is for nest." Afterward, trace the letter N together on paper, pointing out its single connecting diagonal line.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up M and N, have them place a finger on their nose or lips while saying each sound — the mouth position for each is noticeably different.
Teachers: N pairs naturally with a nighttime or bedtime theme — night, nest, and nose are all cozy, familiar words for a calm-down activity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter N make?

Letter N makes a "nnn" sound, made by touching the tongue to the roof of the mouth while air flows through the nose. You can feel the vibration by placing a finger on the side of your nose.

What are some easy words that start with N for preschoolers?

Easy N words for preschoolers include nest, nose, net, nurse, and night — body-part and everyday object words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter N?

Practice two straight vertical lines connected by one diagonal line for capital N. Lowercase n is just a single hump, similar to half of lowercase m.

Why does the letter N sound nasal?

N is a nasal sound, meaning air flows out through the nose instead of the mouth while the tongue blocks airflow through the mouth — M and the "ng" sound in "sing" work the same way.

What activities help kids remember the letter N?

Touching noses while saying "N is for nose," tracing N in sand or shaving cream, building a pretend bird's nest, or coloring pictures of N words while saying each one aloud.
