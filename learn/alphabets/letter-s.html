How To Write Letter S

To write capital S, draw a smooth curved line. To write small s, draw a smaller curved line.

Fun Facts About Letter S

  • S is one of the most used letters.
  • S appears in many common words.

Practice Letter S

Kids can trace S, color S pictures, and find S words in books.

The Letter S Sound

Letter S makes a soft "sss" hissing sound, made by pushing air through the teeth with the tongue close to the roof of the mouth. It's a sound kids can hold out, just like a snake — "ssssnake."

Classroom Activity

Slither around like snakes while hissing "S is for snake!" Then draw or color the sun together, talking about how "sun" and "snake" both start with the same sound.

Homeschool Activity

Fill a bin with sand (or use a sensory tray) and let your child trace the letter S right in it while saying "S is for sand." The texture makes the curved shape easier to feel and remember.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since S is such a common letter, point it out throughout the day in familiar words your child already knows — it reinforces the letter without needing a dedicated activity every time.
Teachers: S's continuous curve is great handwriting practice — have children trace it slowly in the air with a finger before moving to paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter S make?

Letter S makes a soft "sss" hissing sound, made by pushing air through the teeth with the tongue close to the roof of the mouth — a sound kids can hold out, just like a snake.

What are some easy words that start with S for preschoolers?

Easy S words for preschoolers include sun, star, snake, school, and sand — S is one of the most common starting letters in English, so there are plenty of familiar words to choose from.

How do I teach my child to write the letter S?

Letter S is one smooth curved line that changes direction twice, for both capital S and lowercase s. It takes practice since it's a continuous curve rather than straight lines, but it's great for developing pencil control.

Why is S a good letter to teach early?

S is one of the most frequently used letters in English and appears at the start of many familiar words. The hissing sound is also easy for kids to feel and exaggerate, which helps it stick.

What activities help kids remember the letter S?

Hissing like a snake while saying "S is for snake," tracing S in sand, playing in sunshine, or coloring pictures of S words while saying each one aloud.
