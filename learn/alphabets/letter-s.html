Letter S: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

S is the 19th letter of the English alphabet. It comes after R and before T. S is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is S and the small (lowercase) letter is s.

On this page you will learn the sounds S makes, how to write capital S and small s, words that use S, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does S Make?

  • s: a hissing sound, like a snake, made with your teeth close together and no voice. Examples: sun, snake, sock, bus.
  • z sound: s often sounds like z in the middle or at the end of words. Examples: is, has, dogs, rose.
  • sh: when s and h team up. Examples: ship, shoe, fish.

Practice the sound with pictures on S is for Sun, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital S and Small s

Capital S

  1. Start near the top on the right.
  2. Curve up and to the left, then curve down across the middle to the right, like a snake.
  3. Curve back around to the left at the bottom.

Small s

  1. Make the same snake-like curve, but smaller, between the middle line and the bottom line.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

S also appears in the middle or at the end of words: bus, glass, grass, horse, house.

Common Mix-Ups

S is often written backwards by young children. Remember that S starts at the top on the right, curving like the start of C.

Interesting Facts About S

  • S is a consonant.
  • Adding s to the end of a word is the most common way to make it mean more than one: cat becomes cats.
  • Lots of words start with s followed by another consonant, such as stop, snake, spoon and swim.

Classroom Activity

Slither around like snakes while hissing "S is for snake!" Then draw or color the sun together, talking about how "sun" and "snake" both start with the same sound.

Homeschool Activity

Fill a bin with sand (or use a sensory tray) and let your child trace the letter S right in it while saying "S is for sand." The texture makes the curved shape easier to feel and remember.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since S is such a common letter, point it out throughout the day in familiar words your child already knows — it reinforces the letter without needing a dedicated activity every time.
Teachers: S's continuous curve is great handwriting practice — have children trace it slowly in the air with a finger before moving to paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is S in the alphabet?

S is the 19th letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after R and before T.

Is S a vowel or a consonant?

S is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter S make?

S makes these sounds: s as in sun; z sound as in is; sh as in ship.

What are some words that start with S?

Words that start with S include sun, snake, star, sock, seal, spider.

Why does S sometimes sound like Z?

When s comes after a sound made with your voice, as in dogs, or between vowels, as in rose, it often takes on the buzzing z sound.

Summary

S is the 19th letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are s, z sound, sh. Practice writing capital S and small s, and look for S in words around you. Next, learn the letter T.
