The letter Y is the twenty-fifth letter of the alphabet. Capital Y looks like Y and small y looks like y. The letter Y can work as a vowel and a consonant. This page includes a free printable Letter Y worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

The letter Y helps children understand special letter roles in English.

How To Write Letter Y

To write capital Y, draw two slant lines and one straight line. To write small y, draw a curve and a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter Y

  • Y can act as a vowel.
  • Y is used in many modern words.

Practice Letter Y

Kids can trace Y, color Y pictures, and read Y words.

The Letter Y Sound

At the start of a word, Y makes a consonant "yuh" sound, as in yellow and yak. At the end of a word, Y often acts like a vowel instead, making a long "e" sound (as in happy) or a long "i" sound (as in my).

Classroom Activity

Hand out balls of colorful yarn and let children wrap it around their fingers while chanting "Y is for yarn!" Then taste-test yogurt together, calling out other Y words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Look up a picture of a yak together while saying "Y is for yak" — it's an unusual, memorable animal for young kids. Afterward, trace the letter Y on paper, pointing out its two slanted lines meeting a straight one.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Once your child knows Y as a starting consonant, point out how it changes jobs at the end of words like "happy" and "baby" — a fun stretch for kids ready for a challenge.
Teachers: Y pairs well with a "surprising animals" theme — yaks are unfamiliar to most children and make the letter memorable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter Y make?

At the start of a word, Y makes a consonant "yuh" sound, as in yellow and yak. At the end of a word, Y often acts like a vowel, making a long "e" sound (happy) or a long "i" sound (my).

What are some easy words that start with Y for preschoolers?

Easy Y words for preschoolers include yak, yellow, yarn, yogurt, and yacht — animal and food words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter Y?

Practice two slanted lines meeting in the middle, then one straight line continuing down, for capital Y. Lowercase y is a small curve with a tail that dips below the writing line.

Why is Y sometimes called a vowel?

Y is called a "sometimes vowel" because it acts like a consonant at the start of words (yellow, yak) but behaves like a vowel at the end of many words (happy, my, baby).

What activities help kids remember the letter Y?

Wrapping colorful yarn around fingers while saying "Y is for yarn," tracing Y in sand or shaving cream, tasting yogurt, or coloring pictures of Y words while saying each one aloud.
