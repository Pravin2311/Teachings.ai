Letter B for Kids

The letter B is the second letter of the English alphabet. Capital B looks like B and small b looks like b. The letter B makes the sound “buh”.

Words That Start With B

Ball
Bat
Bus
Banana
Butterfly

These words help children recognize the sound and shape of the letter B easily.

Learning the letter B improves vocabulary and early reading skills. Parents and teachers can use B words in games and activities.

How To Write Letter B

To write capital B, draw one straight line and add two curves on the right side. To write small b, draw a long line and add a small curve in the middle.

Fun Facts About Letter B

  • B is the second letter of the alphabet.
  • B comes after A and before C.
  • Many animal and object names start with B.

Practice Letter B

Kids can trace letter B, color letter B, and find words starting with B in books and charts.
