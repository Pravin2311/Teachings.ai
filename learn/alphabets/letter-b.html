The letter B is the second letter of the English alphabet. Capital B looks like B and small b looks like b. The letter B makes the sound “buh”. This page includes a free printable Letter B worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Words That Start With B

These words help children recognize the sound and shape of the letter B easily.

Learning the letter B improves vocabulary and early reading skills. Parents and teachers can use B words in games and activities.

How To Write Letter B

To write capital B, draw one straight line and add two curves on the right side. To write small b, draw a long line and add a small curve in the middle.

Fun Facts About Letter B

  • B is the second letter of the alphabet.
  • B comes after A and before C.
  • Many animal and object names start with B.

Practice Letter B

Kids can trace letter B, color letter B, and find words starting with B in books and charts.

The Letter B Sound

Letter B makes a "buh" sound — press the lips together, then release them with a small puff of air. It's one of the easiest consonant sounds for young children to feel, since they can watch and copy the lip movement in a mirror.

Classroom Activity

Bounce a real ball around the circle while the class chants "B is for ball!" Then have children search the room for other B objects — a book, a box, a backpack — and add them to a class "B is for..." chart.

Homeschool Activity

Try a banana taste-and-trace activity: let your child eat a banana slice, then trace the letter B on paper while saying the word out loud. Tasting and tracing together helps the letter stick in memory.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up B and D, practice them on separate days rather than side by side — comparing mirror-image letters too early can make the confusion worse.
Teachers: Pair movement with the sound. Have children "bounce" in place each time they hear a B word in a story or song.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter B make?

Letter B makes a "buh" sound, made by pressing the lips together and releasing them with a small puff of air. It's one of the easiest consonant sounds for preschoolers to feel and hear.

What are some easy words that start with B for preschoolers?

Easy B words for preschoolers include ball, bat, bus, banana, and butterfly — words tied to toys, food, or animals a child already knows.

How do I teach my child to write the letter B?

Start with tracing a large printed B before moving to pencil and paper. For capital B, practice one straight line down with two curves bulging out to the right; for lowercase b, practice a tall line with one curve at the bottom.

How can I tell letter B and D apart when teaching my child?

B and D are commonly mixed up because they're mirror images. Practicing them on separate days, not side by side, helps — as does connecting b to a word like "ball" that starts with a bounce.

What activities help kids remember the letter B?

Bouncing a ball while saying "B is for ball," tracing B in sand or shaving cream, a banana taste-and-trace activity, or coloring pictures of B words while saying each one aloud.
