Letter Z for Kids

The letter Z is the last letter of the English alphabet. Capital Z looks like Z and small z looks like z. The letter Z makes a “zzz” sound.

Words That Start With Z

Zebra
Zoo
Zip
Zero
Zigzag

The letter Z helps children learn ending sounds and sharp pronunciation.

How To Write Letter Z

To write capital Z, draw three straight lines. To write small z, draw three small straight lines.

Fun Facts About Letter Z

  • Z is the last letter of the alphabet.
  • Z is one of the least used letters.

Practice Letter Z

Kids can trace Z, color Z pictures, and celebrate finishing the alphabet.

The Letter Z Sound

Letter Z makes a buzzing "zzz" sound, made the same way as S but with the vocal cords vibrating — place a finger on the throat while saying it and you'll feel the buzz, just like a bee.

Classroom Activity

Buzz around the room like bees while chanting "Z is for zebra!" Then draw big zigzag lines on paper together, tracing the same three-stroke shape as the letter Z itself.

Homeschool Activity

Look up pictures of zebras together while saying "Z is for zebra," pointing out their black-and-white zigzag stripes. Afterward, trace the letter Z on paper, counting its three straight strokes.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Reaching Z is worth celebrating! Consider a small "we finished the alphabet" moment, like a certificate or reviewing a few favorite letters together.
Teachers: Z's zigzag shape makes a great fine-motor tracing exercise — have children draw zigzags of different sizes before moving to the letter itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter Z make?

Letter Z makes a buzzing "zzz" sound, made the same way as S but with the vocal cords vibrating — you can feel the buzz with a finger on your throat.

What are some easy words that start with Z for preschoolers?

Easy Z words for preschoolers include zebra, zoo, zip, zero, and zigzag — animal and everyday action words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter Z?

Practice one straight line across the top, one diagonal line down, and one straight line across the bottom for capital Z. Lowercase z follows the same three-stroke pattern, just smaller.

What's a good way to celebrate finishing the alphabet at Z?

Since Z is the last letter, many classrooms and families mark it with a small celebration — an "alphabet party," a certificate, or simply reviewing favorite letters from A to Z together.

What activities help kids remember the letter Z?

Buzzing like a bee while saying "Z is for zebra," tracing Z in sand or shaving cream, drawing a zigzag line, or coloring pictures of Z words while saying each one aloud.
