The letter U is the twenty-first letter of the English alphabet. Capital U looks like U and small u looks like u. The letter U makes a soft “uh” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter U worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

The letter U helps children learn vowel sounds and simple word building.

How To Write Letter U

To write capital U, draw two straight lines and connect them at the bottom. To write small u, draw a small curve and a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter U

  • U is a vowel.
  • U is used in many describing words.

Practice Letter U

Kids can trace U, color U pictures, and find U words around them.

The Letter U Sound

Letter U makes two main sounds. The short u sound is heard in words like umbrella and up — this is usually the first sound preschoolers learn. The long u sound, which says its own name, is heard in words like unicorn and uniform.

Classroom Activity

Open umbrellas indoors (or use pictures) while chanting "U is for umbrella!" Then draw pretend unicorns together as a class, naming other U words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Since fewer words start with U, get creative — put on a play uniform or costume together while saying "U is for uniform." Afterward, trace the letter U on paper, pointing out its rounded bottom.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Letter U has fewer everyday example words than most letters, so lean on pictures and props (like an umbrella) rather than just word lists.
Teachers: Point out that U shows up inside lots of familiar words too, like "sun" and "bus" — not just at the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter U make?

Letter U makes two main sounds: the short u sound heard in umbrella and up, and the long u sound heard in unicorn and uniform. Preschoolers usually learn the short u sound first.

What are some easy words that start with U for preschoolers?

Easy U words for preschoolers include umbrella, unicorn, uniform, up, and urn — pairing them with pictures helps since fewer common words start with U than most letters.

How do I teach my child to write the letter U?

Practice two straight vertical lines that curve and connect at the bottom for capital U. Lowercase u follows the same shape, just shorter, with a small curve and a tail on the right side.

Why is U called a vowel?

U is one of the five vowels (a, e, i, o, u). It's also used inside many other words, not just at the start — pointing that out helps kids notice U even when it's less common as a first letter.

What activities help kids remember the letter U?

Opening an umbrella while saying "U is for umbrella," tracing U in sand or shaving cream, drawing a pretend unicorn, or coloring pictures of U words while saying each one aloud.
