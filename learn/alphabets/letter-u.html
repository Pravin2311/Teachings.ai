Letter U: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

U is the 21st letter of the English alphabet. It comes after T and before V. U is a vowel. The capital (uppercase) letter is U and the small (lowercase) letter is u.

How to Write Capital U and Small u

Follow the numbers: start each stroke at its numbered dot and move in the direction of the arrow. The blue top line, dashed middle line and red bottom line match the lines on handwriting paper.

Capital U (1 stroke)

  1. Start at the top on the left. Draw a line down.
  2. Curve around at the bottom.
  3. Go back up to the top on the right.

Small u (1 stroke)

  1. Start at the middle line. Draw a line down, curve around at the bottom and go back up to the middle line.
  2. Draw a short straight line down on the right.

Small u is a short letter: it sits between the dashed middle line and the bottom line, like a, c and o.

Common mix-up: Lowercase u can look like an upside-down n, and like v. U has a round bottom, and lowercase u has a small line on the right.

What Sound Does U Make?

  • short u: the sound at the start of umbrella and in the middle of cup. Examples: umbrella, up, cup, sun.
  • long u: U saying its own name, you. Examples: unicorn, use, music, cute.
  • oo: in some words. Examples: flute, ruler, blue.

Interesting Facts About U

  • U almost always follows Q in English words, as in queen and quick.
  • The letters U and V used to be written as the same letter. They became separate letters only a few hundred years ago.

Activities for Parents and Teachers

In the Classroom

Open umbrellas indoors (or use pictures) while chanting "U is for umbrella!" Then draw pretend unicorns together as a class, naming other U words along the way.

At Home

Since fewer words start with U, get creative — put on a play uniform or costume together while saying "U is for uniform." Afterward, trace the letter U on paper, pointing out its rounded bottom.

Tips

Parents: Letter U has fewer everyday example words than most letters, so lean on pictures and props (like an umbrella) rather than just word lists.
Teachers: Point out that U shows up inside lots of familiar words too, like "sun" and "bus" — not just at the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is U in the alphabet?

U is the 21st letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after T and before V.

Is U a vowel or a consonant?

U is a vowel. The five vowels are A, E, I, O and U. Every English word has at least one vowel sound, and vowels can make short and long sounds.

What sound does the letter U make?

U makes these sounds: short u as in umbrella; long u as in unicorn; oo as in flute.

How do you write a small u?

Start at the middle line. Draw a line down, curve around at the bottom and go back up to the middle line. Draw a short straight line down on the right. Small u is a short letter: it sits between the dashed middle line and the bottom line, like a, c and o.

What are some words that start with U?

Words that start with U include umbrella, unicorn, up, under, uniform, uncle.

Why do we say "a unicorn" but "an umbrella"?

We use "an" before a vowel sound. Unicorn starts with a y sound (you), so we say "a unicorn", while umbrella starts with the vowel sound uh.

Summary

U is the 21st letter of the alphabet and is a vowel. Its main sounds are short u, long u, oo. Capital U takes 1 stroke and small u takes 1. Next, learn the letter V.
