Letter X: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

X is the 24th letter of the English alphabet. It comes after W and before Y. X is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is X and the small (lowercase) letter is x.

On this page you will learn the sounds X makes, how to write capital X and small x, words that use X, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

Letter X at a Glance

Position24th of 26 letters
TypeConsonant
Uppercase and lowercaseX x
Comes betweenW and Y
Main soundsks, z, its name, ex

What Sound Does X Make?

  • ks: at the end of a word, X usually says ks. Examples: box, fox, six, wax.
  • z: at the start of a few words, X says z. Examples: xylophone, xylem.
  • its name, ex: in X-ray, X says its own name. Examples: X-ray.

Practice the sound with pictures on X is for Xylophone, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital X and Small x

Capital X

  1. Draw a slanted line from the top left to the bottom right.
  2. Draw a slanted line from the top right to the bottom left, crossing the first line in the middle.

Small x

  1. Make the same crossing shape, but smaller, between the middle line and the bottom line.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

Words With X

Xylophone
X-ray
Xylem

X also appears in the middle or at the end of words: box, fox, six, taxi, mix.

Common Mix-Ups

X is easy to write because it is just two crossing lines, but children can mix up lowercase x with the plus sign. In x, both lines are slanted.

Interesting Facts About X

  • X is a consonant.
  • Very few English words start with X, which is why X words for kids are usually words that end with X, like box and fox.
  • X is used in math to mean multiply, and on maps to mark a spot.

Printable Practice

Classroom Activity

Draw a big "X marks the spot" on the floor with tape and hide a small treasure nearby for a mini scavenger hunt. Then let children take turns playing a toy xylophone, listening for its X-starting name.

Homeschool Activity

Since X is rare at the start of words, focus on X at the end instead — find pictures of a fox and a box around the house, pointing out the "ks" sound both words share. Afterward, trace the letter X together, two crossing diagonal lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Don't stress about finding lots of "starts with X" words — most kids' phonics programs teach X mainly through ending sounds, like box and fox, since those are far more common.
Teachers: X is a great letter for a simple treasure-hunt theme — "X marks the spot" is a natural, memorable hook.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is X in the alphabet?

X is the 24th letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after W and before Y.

Is X a vowel or a consonant?

X is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter X make?

X makes these sounds: ks as in box; z as in xylophone; its name, ex as in X-ray.

What are some words with X?

Only a few words start with X, such as xylophone, X-ray, xylem. More often X comes at the end: box, fox, six, taxi, mix.

Why are there so few words that start with X?

English has very few native words with X at the start. Most of them, like xylophone, came from Greek, where they start with the z sound.

Summary

X is the 24th letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are ks, z, its name, ex. Practice writing capital X and small x, and look for X in words around you. Next, learn the letter Y.
