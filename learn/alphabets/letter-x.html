The letter X is the twenty-fourth letter of the alphabet. Capital X looks like X and small x looks like x. The letter X makes a “ks” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter X worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

The letter X helps children learn special and rare English sounds.

How To Write Letter X

To write capital X, draw two crossing lines. To write small x, draw two small crossing lines.

Fun Facts About Letter X

  • X is one of the rarest letters.
  • X is often used in science.

Practice Letter X

Kids can trace X, color X pictures, and find X words in charts.

The Letter X Sound

X is unusual: at the start of a word it usually makes a "z" sound, like in xylophone. At the end of a word, it makes a "ks" sound instead, like in box, fox, and six — which is actually how most kids first meet the letter X.

Classroom Activity

Draw a big "X marks the spot" on the floor with tape and hide a small treasure nearby for a mini scavenger hunt. Then let children take turns playing a toy xylophone, listening for its X-starting name.

Homeschool Activity

Since X is rare at the start of words, focus on X at the end instead — find pictures of a fox and a box around the house, pointing out the "ks" sound both words share. Afterward, trace the letter X together, two crossing diagonal lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Don't stress about finding lots of "starts with X" words — most kids' phonics programs teach X mainly through ending sounds, like box and fox, since those are far more common.
Teachers: X is a great letter for a simple treasure-hunt theme — "X marks the spot" is a natural, memorable hook.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter X make?

X is unusual: at the start of a word it usually makes a "z" sound, like in xylophone. At the end of a word, it makes a "ks" sound instead, like in box, fox, and six.

What are some easy words with the letter X for preschoolers?

Since very few words start with X, it helps to also teach words that end in X, like box, fox, and six — these use the more familiar "ks" sound and are much easier for preschoolers to recognize.

How do I teach my child to write the letter X?

Practice two diagonal lines crossing in the middle, like an "X marks the spot." Capital and lowercase X look almost identical, just different sizes.

Why are there so few words that start with X?

X is the least-used letter to start English words because most of its sound value is more naturally spelled other ways. Most phonics programs teach X primarily through words that end in X, like box and fox.

What activities help kids remember the letter X?

Making a big "X marks the spot" treasure hunt, tracing X in sand or shaving cream, playing a toy xylophone, or finding X in words like box and fox around the house.
