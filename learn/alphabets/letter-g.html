The letter G is the seventh letter of the English alphabet. Capital G looks like G and small g looks like g. The letter G can make both “guh” and soft “j” sounds. This page includes a free printable Letter G worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

The letter G helps children understand that one letter can make different sounds in different words.

How To Write Letter G

To write capital G, draw a big curve and add a small line inside. To write small g, draw a circle and a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter G

  • G is the seventh letter of the alphabet.
  • G can sound hard or soft.
  • Many nature words start with G.

Practice Letter G

Kids can trace letter G, match G words, and color G pictures.

The Letter G Sound

Letter G usually makes a hard "guh" sound, as in goat and garden. It can also make a soft "j" sound, as in giraffe and gem, when it's followed by e, i, or y. Preschoolers typically learn the hard G sound first.

Classroom Activity

Wrap an empty box like a pretend gift and pass it around the circle while chanting "G is for gift!" Then ask each child to guess what might be inside before opening it together.

Homeschool Activity

Take a walk to the garden or a park and spot G things along the way — grass, a garden gate, or grapes at the store. Afterward, trace the letter G on paper, drawing the same curved shape as a goat's horn.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Focus on the hard "guh" sound (as in goat) until it's solid before introducing the soft "j" sound (as in giraffe) — mixing the two too early can be confusing.
Teachers: Letter G pairs well with a farm or zoo theme — goats and giraffes are both fun anchors for the two different G sounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter G make?

Letter G usually makes a hard "guh" sound, as in goat and garden. It can also make a soft "j" sound, as in giraffe and gem, depending on the letter that follows it.

What are some easy words that start with G for preschoolers?

Easy G words for preschoolers include goat, grapes, glass, garden, and gift — animal and everyday object words work especially well.

How do I teach my child to write the letter G?

Practice a big curve almost like the letter C, then add a short line pointing inward near the bottom-right for capital G. Lowercase g is a small circle with a hanging tail that curves under the writing line.

Why does G sometimes sound like J?

G makes a soft "j" sound when followed by e, i, or y (giraffe, gem, gym), and a hard "guh" sound before a, o, or u (goat, go, gum) — the same pattern C follows with its own hard and soft sounds.

What activities help kids remember the letter G?

A pretend gift-wrapping activity while saying "G is for gift," tracing G in sand or shaving cream, a trip to the garden to spot G plants, or coloring pictures of G words while saying each one aloud.
