The letter R is the eighteenth letter of the alphabet. Capital R looks like R and small r looks like r. The letter R makes a rolling “rrr” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter R worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Letter R helps children learn strong rolling sounds in speech.

How To Write Letter R

To write capital R, draw one line, one curve, and one slant line. To write small r, draw a short line and a small curve.

Fun Facts About Letter R

  • R is important for clear pronunciation.
  • Many action words start with R.

Practice Letter R

Kids can trace R, color R pictures, and read R words aloud.

The Letter R Sound

Letter R makes a rolling "rrr" sound, made by curling the tongue slightly without touching the roof of the mouth. It's one of the trickier sounds for young children, and it's completely normal for R to still sound a little soft until age 5 or 6.

Classroom Activity

Hop around the room like rabbits while chanting "R is for rabbit!" Then pretend it's raining by wiggling fingers overhead like raindrops, calling out other R words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Look at a rose (real or in a picture) together while saying "R is for rose." Afterward, trace the letter R on paper, pointing out its straight line, curve, and kicking leg.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Don't worry if R still sounds a bit like W at this age (like "wabbit" for "rabbit") — R is one of the last sounds to fully develop and often isn't crisp until age 6 or 7.
Teachers: Model the R sound clearly and often in group settings, but avoid correcting a child repeatedly in front of peers — gentle, natural repetition works better than direct correction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter R make?

Letter R makes a rolling "rrr" sound, made by curling the tongue slightly without touching the roof of the mouth — one of the trickier sounds for young children to master.

What are some easy words that start with R for preschoolers?

Easy R words for preschoolers include rabbit, rose, rain, robot, and road — animal and weather words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter R?

Practice one straight vertical line, a curve at the top, and a diagonal leg kicking out to the bottom-right for capital R. Lowercase r is a short vertical line with a small curve at the top.

Is it normal for young kids to struggle with the R sound?

Yes — R is considered one of the most difficult sounds in English and is often the last sound children master, sometimes not fully clear until age 6 or 7. Saying "wabbit" for "rabbit" is typically normal at this age.

What activities help kids remember the letter R?

Hopping like a rabbit while saying "R is for rabbit," tracing R in sand or shaving cream, splashing in pretend rain, or coloring pictures of R words while saying each one aloud.
