Letter R: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

R is the 18th letter of the English alphabet. It comes after Q and before S. R is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is R and the small (lowercase) letter is r.

On this page you will learn the sounds R makes, how to write capital R and small r, words that use R, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does R Make?

  • r: made by curling your tongue up without touching the roof of your mouth, with your voice switched on. Examples: rabbit, red, rain, car.
  • r-controlled vowels: r changes the sound of the vowel before it. Examples: car, her, bird, corn, fur.

Practice the sound with pictures on R is for Rat, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital R and Small r

Capital R

  1. Start at the top. Draw a straight line down.
  2. Go back to the top. Draw a curve out to the right and back in to the middle.
  3. From the middle, draw a slanted line out to the bottom right.

Small r

  1. Draw a short line from the middle line down to the bottom line.
  2. Go back up and make a small curve over to the right, like the start of n.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

R also appears in the middle or at the end of words: car, star, carrot, zebra, tiger.

Common Mix-Ups

Capital R is like P with an extra leg. Lowercase r is sometimes mixed up with n, but r stops after the small curve.

Interesting Facts About R

  • R is a consonant.
  • The r sound is one of the last sounds many children learn to say clearly, often between ages 5 and 7, and saying w instead of r is common for young children.
  • The letter R comes from an ancient symbol for a head.

Classroom Activity

Hop around the room like rabbits while chanting "R is for rabbit!" Then pretend it's raining by wiggling fingers overhead like raindrops, calling out other R words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Look at a rose (real or in a picture) together while saying "R is for rose." Afterward, trace the letter R on paper, pointing out its straight line, curve, and kicking leg.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Don't worry if R still sounds a bit like W at this age (like "wabbit" for "rabbit") — R is one of the last sounds to fully develop and often isn't crisp until age 6 or 7.
Teachers: Model the R sound clearly and often in group settings, but avoid correcting a child repeatedly in front of peers — gentle, natural repetition works better than direct correction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is R in the alphabet?

R is the 18th letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after Q and before S.

Is R a vowel or a consonant?

R is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter R make?

R makes these sounds: r as in rabbit; r-controlled vowels as in car.

What are some words that start with R?

Words that start with R include rabbit, rainbow, rocket, robot, rose, ring.

My child says "wabbit" instead of "rabbit". Is that normal?

Yes, it is very common in young children because r is one of the harder sounds. If you are worried, especially after age 7, speak to your pediatrician or a speech-language pathologist.

Summary

R is the 18th letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are r, r-controlled vowels. Practice writing capital R and small r, and look for R in words around you. Next, learn the letter S.
