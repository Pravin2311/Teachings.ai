Letter A: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

A is the first letter of the English alphabet. It is the very first letter, and B comes after it. A is a vowel. The capital (uppercase) letter is A and the small (lowercase) letter is a.

On this page you will learn the sounds A makes, how to write capital A and small a, words that use A, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does A Make?

  • short a: the sound at the start of apple and in the middle of cat. Examples: apple, ant, cat, hat.
  • long a: A saying its own name. Examples: acorn, cake, rain, play.
  • ah: an open sound, as in some words. Examples: father, car, spa.

How to Write Capital A and Small a

Capital A

  1. Start at the top. Draw a slanted line down to the left.
  2. Go back to the top. Draw a slanted line down to the right.
  3. Draw a short line across the middle to join the two sides.

Small a

  1. Start just below the middle line. Curve up, around to the left and down, like a small c.
  2. Close the circle back where you started.
  3. Without lifting your pencil, draw a short straight line down on the right side.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

Common Mix-Ups

Lowercase a can look like o or d. Check for the little straight line on the right side, which is short (not tall like d).

Interesting Facts About A

  • A is one of the five vowels: A, E, I, O and U.
  • The capital A comes from an ancient symbol that showed the head of an ox. Turn A upside down and you can still imagine the horns.
  • A is a word all by itself, as in "a cat".

Quick Quiz: Letter A

Answer each question, then tap it to check.

Which letter comes right after A?

B. A is the first letter, so it has no letter before it.

Which word starts with the short a sound: apple, egg or orange?

Apple. Egg starts with E and orange starts with O.

Is A a vowel or a consonant?

A vowel. The vowels are A, E, I, O and U.

Classroom Activity

Ask children to find objects in the classroom that start with A, like an apple or an art supply. Build an "A is for..." wall display together, adding a new picture each time someone spots one.

Homeschool Activity

Go on an A scavenger hunt around the house — look for an apple, an arrow, or anything else that starts with A. Say each word slowly, stretching out the beginning sound so your child can hear it clearly.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Practice tracing letter A with a finger in sand, shaving cream, or on a steamy window before moving to pencil and paper — it keeps the motion playful and low-pressure.
Teachers: Pair the letter A with a related craft, like an apple-stamp painting, so children connect the letter's shape to a real object they can hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is A in the alphabet?

A is the first letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It is the very first letter, and B comes after it.

Is A a vowel or a consonant?

A is a vowel. The five vowels are A, E, I, O and U. Every English word has at least one vowel sound, and vowels can make short and long sounds.

What sound does the letter A make?

A makes these sounds: short a as in apple; long a as in acorn; ah as in father.

What are some words that start with A?

Words that start with A include apple, ant, alligator, airplane, arm, acorn.

Why is A the first letter of the alphabet?

The order of our alphabet goes back thousands of years to the alphabets that Latin came from, and the letter that became A was already first in those ancient alphabets.

Summary

A is the first letter of the alphabet and is a vowel. Its main sounds are short a, long a, ah. Practice writing capital A and small a, and look for A in words around you. Next, learn the letter B.
