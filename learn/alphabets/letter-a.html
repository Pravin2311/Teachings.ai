Words That Start With A

Apple
Ant
Airplane
Arrow

These simple words help children recognize and pronounce the letter A easily.

How To Write Letter A

To write capital A, draw two slant lines and connect them in the middle. To write small a, draw a small circle and add a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter A

  • A is the first letter of the alphabet.
  • A is a vowel.
  • Many English words start with A.

Practice Letter A

Kids can trace, color, and read words starting with the letter A to improve learning.

The Letter A Sound

Letter A makes two main sounds. The short a sound is heard in words like cat and apple — this is usually the first sound preschoolers learn. The long a sound, which says its own name, is heard in words like cake and apron. Practicing both sounds together helps kids tell them apart as they start reading.

Classroom Activity

Ask children to find objects in the classroom that start with A, like an apple or an art supply. Build an "A is for..." wall display together, adding a new picture each time someone spots one.

Homeschool Activity

Go on an A scavenger hunt around the house — look for an apple, an arrow, or anything else that starts with A. Say each word slowly, stretching out the beginning sound so your child can hear it clearly.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Practice tracing letter A with a finger in sand, shaving cream, or on a steamy window before moving to pencil and paper — it keeps the motion playful and low-pressure.
Teachers: Pair the letter A with a related craft, like an apple-stamp painting, so children connect the letter's shape to a real object they can hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter A make?

Letter A makes two main sounds: the short a sound heard in cat and apple, and the long a sound heard in cake and apron. Preschoolers usually learn the short a sound first.

What are some easy words that start with A for preschoolers?

Easy A words for preschoolers include apple, ant, airplane, arrow, and alligator — starting with objects a child already recognizes makes the letter easier to remember.

How do I teach my child to write the letter A?

Start with tracing: guide your child's finger along a large printed A before moving to pencil and paper. For capital A, practice two slanted lines meeting at a connecting line; for lowercase a, practice a small circle with a short tail.

Why is tracing important before independent writing?

Tracing builds the fine motor control and muscle memory a child needs before writing letters unassisted, letting them focus on the correct shape without also controlling pencil pressure from scratch.

What activities help kids remember the letter A?

Hands-on activities work best: an A scavenger hunt, tracing A in sand or shaving cream, an apple-stamp painting craft, or coloring pictures of A words while saying each one aloud.
