Letter L for Kids

The letter L is the twelfth letter of the alphabet. Capital L looks like L and small l looks like l. The letter L makes a soft “lll” sound.

Words That Start With L

Lion
Leaf
Lamp
Lemon
Lake

The letter L helps children learn smooth flowing sounds in English words.

How To Write Letter L

To write capital L, draw one long line and one short line at the bottom. To write small l, draw a tall straight line.

Fun Facts About Letter L

  • L is easy to write.
  • Many nature words start with L.

Practice Letter L

Kids can trace L, color L pictures, and read L words aloud.

More to Discover

  • L comes right after K and before M in the alphabet.
  • To make the L sound, your tongue touches the top of your mouth.
