Letter C: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

C is the third letter of the English alphabet. It comes after B and before D. C is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is C and the small (lowercase) letter is c.

On this page you will learn the sounds C makes, how to write capital C and small c, words that use C, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does C Make?

  • hard c: the same sound as k. Examples: cat, cup, car, cake.
  • soft c: the same sound as s, usually when c comes before e, i or y. Examples: city, cent, circle, bicycle.
  • ch: when c and h team up. Examples: chair, cheese, chin.

How to Write Capital C and Small c

Capital C

  1. Start near the top on the right.
  2. Curve up and back to the left, then down and around the bottom.
  3. Stop on the right side, leaving the C open.

Small c

  1. Start just below the middle line on the right.
  2. Make the same open curve, but smaller, stopping at the bottom line on the right.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

Common Mix-Ups

Capital C and small c look the same except for size, so check where the letter starts: small c starts at the middle line.

Interesting Facts About C

  • C is a consonant that can make two different sounds, a hard k sound and a soft s sound.
  • The Romans created the letter G by adding a small line to the letter C.
  • C is one of the easiest letters to write because it is just one curve, and it is the start of writing a, d, g, o and q.

Quick Quiz: Letter C

Answer each question, then tap it to check.

Does the c in city make a k sound or an s sound?

An s sound. C usually says s before e, i or y.

Which letter comes right after C?

D.

Which word starts with a hard c: cat, city or circle?

Cat. City and circle start with a soft c.

Classroom Activity

Send children on a room scavenger hunt for C objects — a cup, a crayon, a chair. Gather the finds on a table and have the class name each one together, emphasizing the "kuh" sound at the start.

Homeschool Activity

Decorate a pretend paper cupcake or cake together while saying "C is for cake!" Afterward, trace the letter C on paper, drawing the same curved shape used to frost the cake.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since C makes two different sounds, don't worry if your child is confused at first — focus on the hard "kuh" sound until it's solid, then introduce the soft sound with words like city.
Teachers: Letter C is a great confidence-builder since both the capital and lowercase forms are just a single curve — perfect for early tracing practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is C in the alphabet?

C is the third letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after B and before D.

Is C a vowel or a consonant?

C is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter C make?

C makes these sounds: hard c as in cat; soft c as in city; ch as in chair.

What are some words that start with C?

Words that start with C include cat, car, cake, cow, cup, carrot.

When does C say k and when does it say s?

C usually says s before e, i or y (city, cent, cycle) and k before a, o, u or a consonant (cat, cot, cup, clap).

Summary

C is the third letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are hard c, soft c, ch. Practice writing capital C and small c, and look for C in words around you. Next, learn the letter D.
