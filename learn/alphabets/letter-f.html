Letter F: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

F is the sixth letter of the English alphabet. It comes after E and before G. F is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is F and the small (lowercase) letter is f.

How to Write Capital F and Small f

Follow the numbers: start each stroke at its numbered dot and move in the direction of the arrow. The blue top line, dashed middle line and red bottom line match the lines on handwriting paper.

Capital F (3 strokes)

  1. Start at the top. Draw a straight line down.
  2. Draw a line across the top, going right.
  3. Draw a shorter line across the middle.

Small f (2 strokes)

  1. Start just below the top line. Curve up and to the left like the top of a cane.
  2. Draw a straight line down to the bottom line.
  3. Draw a short line across at the middle line.

Small f is a tall letter: it reaches up toward the top line, like b, d and h.

Common mix-up: Capital F looks like E without the bottom line, and lowercase f can be confused with t. In f, the curve is at the top; t has no curve at the top.

What Sound Does F Make?

  • f: made by resting your top teeth on your bottom lip and blowing, without using your voice. Examples: fish, fan, fox, leaf.
  • ph: the letters p and h together often make the same f sound. Examples: phone, photo, dolphin.

Interesting Facts About F

  • F and V are made with the same mouth shape. V uses your voice and F does not.
  • F is often doubled at the end of short words, as in off, puff and cliff.

Activities for Parents and Teachers

In the Classroom

Hand out paper fans and have children wave them while chanting "F is for fan!" Then hop around the room like frogs, calling out other F words the class can think of along the way.

At Home

Hold a small piece of tissue paper in front of your child's mouth while they say words like fish or fan — they'll see the tissue flutter from the air, which helps the F sound click. Then trace the letter F together on paper.

Tips

Parents: If your child struggles to hear the F sound, have them place a hand in front of their mouth to feel the airflow — it's a quick, physical way to confirm they're making the sound correctly.
Teachers: Letter F pairs naturally with nature walks — flowers, fruit, and frogs are all common outdoor finds that reinforce the letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is F in the alphabet?

F is the sixth letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after E and before G.

Is F a vowel or a consonant?

F is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter F make?

F makes these sounds: f as in fish; ph as in phone.

How do you write a small f?

Start just below the top line. Curve up and to the left like the top of a cane. Draw a straight line down to the bottom line. Draw a short line across at the middle line. Small f is a tall letter: it reaches up toward the top line, like b, d and h.

What are some words that start with F?

Words that start with F include fish, fan, fox, frog, flower, feather.

Why does phone start with ph instead of f?

Many words with ph came into English from Greek, where that sound was written with a different letter. English kept the ph spelling.
