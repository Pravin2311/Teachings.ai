Letter F for Kids

The letter F is the sixth letter of the English alphabet. Capital F looks like F and small f looks like f. The letter F makes a soft “fff” sound.

Words That Start With F

Fish
Fan
Flower
Frog
Fruit

Learning F words helps children connect sounds with letters. The sound of F is easy to hear in words like fish and fan.

How To Write Letter F

To write capital F, draw one long line and two short lines across it. To write small f, draw a tall line with a small curve.

Fun Facts About Letter F

  • F is the sixth letter of the alphabet.
  • Many food names start with F.
  • F is easy to recognize by its straight lines.

Practice Letter F

Children can trace F, color F pictures, and find F words in story books.
