Letter F Worksheet for Kids (Free Printable PDF)

The letter F is the sixth letter of the English alphabet. Capital F looks like F and small f looks like f. The letter F makes a soft “fff” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter F worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Words That Start With F

Fish
Fan
Flower
Frog
Fruit

Learning F words helps children connect sounds with letters. The sound of F is easy to hear in words like fish and fan.

How To Write Letter F

To write capital F, draw one long line and two short lines across it. To write small f, draw a tall line with a small curve.

Fun Facts About Letter F

  • F is the sixth letter of the alphabet.
  • Many food names start with F.
  • F is easy to recognize by its straight lines.

Practice Letter F

Children can trace F, color F pictures, and find F words in story books.

The Letter F Sound

Letter F makes a soft "fff" sound, made by resting the top teeth on the bottom lip and blowing air out gently. It's an easy sound for kids to feel — they can sense the air on their hand as they say it.

Classroom Activity

Hand out paper fans and have children wave them while chanting "F is for fan!" Then hop around the room like frogs, calling out other F words the class can think of along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Hold a small piece of tissue paper in front of your child's mouth while they say words like fish or fan — they'll see the tissue flutter from the air, which helps the F sound click. Then trace the letter F together on paper.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child struggles to hear the F sound, have them place a hand in front of their mouth to feel the airflow — it's a quick, physical way to confirm they're making the sound correctly.
Teachers: Letter F pairs naturally with nature walks — flowers, fruit, and frogs are all common outdoor finds that reinforce the letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter F make?

Letter F makes a soft "fff" sound, made by resting the top teeth on the bottom lip and blowing air out gently. It's an easy sound for kids to feel because they can sense the air on their hand.

What are some easy words that start with F for preschoolers?

Easy F words for preschoolers include fish, fan, flower, frog, and fruit — animal and nature words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter F?

Practice one long vertical line first, then two short horizontal lines crossing it near the top and middle for capital F. Lowercase f is a tall curved line with one short horizontal line across the middle.

How can I help my child hear the F sound?

Have your child hold a hand or a small piece of tissue paper in front of their mouth while saying words like fish or fan — they'll feel or see the air movement that makes the F sound.

What activities help kids remember the letter F?

Waving a paper fan while saying "F is for fan," tracing F in sand or shaving cream, hopping like a frog around the room, or coloring pictures of F words while saying each one aloud.
