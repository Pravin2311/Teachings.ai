Letter D for Kids

The letter D is the fourth letter of the English alphabet. Capital D looks like D and small d looks like d. The letter D makes the sound “duh”.

Words That Start With D

Dog
Duck
Door
Drum
Dolphin

How To Write Letter D

To write capital D, draw a straight line and a big curve. To write small d, draw a long line and add a small circle.

Fun Facts About Letter D

  • D is the fourth letter of the alphabet.
  • D comes after C and before E.
