The letter D is the fourth letter of the English alphabet. Capital D looks like D and small d looks like d. The letter D makes the sound “duh”. This page includes a free printable Letter D worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Words That Start With D

Dog
Duck
Door
Drum
Dolphin

How To Write Letter D

To write capital D, draw a straight line and a big curve. To write small d, draw a long line and add a small circle.

Fun Facts About Letter D

  • D is the fourth letter of the alphabet.
  • D comes after C and before E.

The Letter D Sound

Letter D makes a "duh" sound, made by tapping the tongue just behind the front teeth. It's a strong, easy-to-hear sound that most children pick up quickly once they've mastered B and C.

Classroom Activity

Beat a toy drum together while chanting "D is for drum!" Then take turns waddling like a duck across the room, calling out other D words the class can think of along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Practice opening and closing a real door while saying "D is for door." Then trace the letter D on paper, pointing out how the curve of the letter matches the curve of a doorknob.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up B and D, practice them on separate days rather than side by side — the mirror-image shapes are easier to tell apart once each one is familiar on its own.
Teachers: Letter D pairs well with animal sounds — a duck's quack is a fun, memorable hook for the letter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter D make?

Letter D makes a "duh" sound, made by tapping the tongue just behind the front teeth — a strong, easy-to-hear sound at the start of words like dog and duck.

What are some easy words that start with D for preschoolers?

Easy D words for preschoolers include dog, duck, door, drum, and dolphin — animal words tend to be the most memorable starting point.

How do I teach my child to write the letter D?

Practice a straight line down first, then a big curve that connects back to the top and bottom of that line for capital D. Lowercase d is a small circle with a tall line on the right side.

How can I tell letter B and D apart when teaching my child?

B and D are commonly mixed up because they're mirror images. Practicing them on separate days, not side by side, helps — as does connecting d to a word like "door," with the curve matching a doorknob.

What activities help kids remember the letter D?

Beating a toy drum while saying "D is for drum," tracing D in sand or shaving cream, waddling like a duck around the room, or coloring pictures of D words while saying each one aloud.
