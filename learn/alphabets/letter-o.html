Letter O for Kids

The letter O is the fifteenth letter of the alphabet. Capital O looks like O and small o looks like o. The letter O makes a round “oh” sound.

Words That Start With O

Orange
Owl
Ocean
Onion
Ox

The letter O helps children learn round vowel sounds clearly.

How To Write Letter O

To write capital O, draw a big circle. To write small o, draw a small circle.

Fun Facts About Letter O

  • O is a vowel.
  • O looks the same in uppercase and lowercase.

Practice Letter O

Kids can trace O, color O pictures, and find O words around them.

More to Discover

  • O is a vowel, and your mouth makes a round shape to say it.
  • O can sound different in words like "dog" and "go."
