Letter W: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

W is the 23rd letter of the English alphabet. It comes after V and before X. W is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is W and the small (lowercase) letter is w.

On this page you will learn the sounds W makes, how to write capital W and small w, words that use W, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does W Make?

  • w: made by rounding your lips like you are about to blow out a candle, with your voice switched on. Examples: wolf, web, window, worm.
  • wh: in many question words. Examples: what, when, where, whale.
  • silent w: no sound before r and in a few words. Examples: write, wrist, two, sword.

Practice the sound with pictures on W is for Wolf, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital W and Small w

Capital W

  1. Start at the top on the left. Draw a slanted line down.
  2. Draw a slanted line up to the middle.
  3. Draw a slanted line down again.
  4. Draw a slanted line up to the top on the right.

Small w

  1. Make the same zigzag shape, but smaller, between the middle line and the bottom line.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

Common Mix-Ups

W is an upside-down M. Remember W starts with a line going down.

Interesting Facts About W

  • W is a consonant.
  • W is called "double U" because it was originally written as two Us (or two Vs) side by side.
  • Many question words start with wh: what, when, where, which and why.

Classroom Activity

Splash hands in a bowl of water while chanting "W is for water!" Then pretend to swim like whales around the room, calling out other W words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Look out a window together and describe what you see while saying "W is for window." Afterward, trace the letter W on paper, counting its four connected slanted lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: W can be tricky to write since it has four connected strokes — let your child practice each "V" shape separately before joining them.
Teachers: W pairs naturally with water play, a favorite sensory activity that reinforces the letter through a hands-on, memorable experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is W in the alphabet?

W is the 23rd letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after V and before X.

Is W a vowel or a consonant?

W is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter W make?

W makes these sounds: w as in wolf; wh as in what; silent w as in write.

What are some words that start with W?

Words that start with W include wolf, whale, watermelon, window, worm, web.

Why is W called double U?

Long ago it was written as two U or V shapes joined together, and in English the name double U stuck.

Summary

W is the 23rd letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sounds are w, wh, silent w. Practice writing capital W and small w, and look for W in words around you. Next, learn the letter X.
