Letter W for Kids

The letter W is the twenty-third letter of the alphabet. Capital W looks like W and small w looks like w. The letter W makes a “wuh” sound.

Words That Start With W

Water
Whale
Window
Watch
Wheel

The letter W helps children learn smooth lip sounds in English.

How To Write Letter W

To write capital W, draw four slant lines. To write small w, draw two small curves.

Fun Facts About Letter W

  • W is called double u.
  • W is one of the longest letters to say.

Practice Letter W

Kids can trace W, color W pictures, and find W words in books.
