Letter T for Kids

The letter T is the twentieth letter of the alphabet. Capital T looks like T and small t looks like t. The letter T makes a clear “tuh” sound.

Words That Start With T

Tiger
Tree
Train
Table
Turtle

The letter T helps children learn clear and sharp sounds in English words.

How To Write Letter T

To write capital T, draw one straight line and one small line across. To write small t, draw a short line and a small cross line.

Fun Facts About Letter T

  • T is easy to recognize.
  • Many tools start with T.

Practice Letter T

Kids can trace T, color T pictures, and read T words aloud.

The Letter T Sound

Letter T makes a quick "tuh" sound, made by tapping the tongue against the roof of the mouth, just behind the front teeth, and releasing it sharply. It's a crisp, easy-to-hear sound.

Classroom Activity

Prowl around like tigers while chanting "T is for tiger!" Then set a pretend table with paper plates and cups, calling out other T words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Take a walk to look at a tree together while saying "T is for tree." Afterward, trace the letter T on paper — since it's just two straight lines, it's a quick, satisfying letter to master.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: T is a great letter to pair with I and L for tracing practice since all three use only straight lines.
Teachers: T pairs well with a train theme — chugging around the room while saying "T is for train" gets kids moving while they learn.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter T make?

Letter T makes a quick "tuh" sound, made by tapping the tongue against the roof of the mouth, just behind the front teeth, and releasing it sharply.

What are some easy words that start with T for preschoolers?

Easy T words for preschoolers include tiger, tree, train, table, and turtle — animal and everyday object words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter T?

Letter T is one of the simplest letters: one straight vertical line, then one short horizontal line across the top for capital T. Lowercase t is a slightly shorter vertical line with a small cross near the middle.

Why is T a good letter for early tracing practice?

Like L and I, T uses only straight lines, making it one of the easiest letters for a child's hand to control — often taught alongside those letters for that reason.

What activities help kids remember the letter T?

Pretending to be a tiger while saying "T is for tiger," tracing T in sand or shaving cream, setting a pretend table, or coloring pictures of T words while saying each one aloud.
