The letter P is the sixteenth letter of the English alphabet. Capital P looks like P and small p looks like p. The letter P makes a popping “puh” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter P worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

Letter P helps children learn strong popping sounds in speech and reading.

How To Write Letter P

To write capital P, draw one long line and one small curve. To write small p, draw a long line down and add a small circle.

Fun Facts About Letter P

  • P is used in many playful words.
  • P sounds like a gentle pop.

Practice Letter P

Kids can trace P, color P pictures, and say P words aloud.

The Letter P Sound

Letter P makes a popping "puh" sound, made by pressing the lips together and releasing them with a quick burst of air. Hold a hand in front of the mouth while saying it — you'll feel a short puff of air.

Classroom Activity

Make pretend pizzas using paper circles and cut-out toppings while chanting "P is for pizza!" Then take turns squawking like a parrot, calling out other P words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Hand your child a pencil and paper and let them draw or scribble while saying "P is for pencil." Afterward, trace the letter P together, pointing out its single curve at the top.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up P and B, point out that P has just one curve at the top, while B has two curves stacked on top of each other.
Teachers: P pairs naturally with a pretend cooking or art activity — pizza, painting, and pencils are all easy, engaging classroom props.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter P make?

Letter P makes a popping "puh" sound, made by pressing the lips together and releasing them with a quick burst of air.

What are some easy words that start with P for preschoolers?

Easy P words for preschoolers include pen, parrot, pencil, panda, and pizza — animal and food words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter P?

Practice one straight vertical line, then a curve at the top for capital P. Lowercase p is a straight line that drops below the writing line, with a small circle attached near the top.

How can I tell letter P and B apart when teaching my child?

P's curve sits only at the top of the line, while B has two curves stacked on top of each other. Practicing them on separate days helps avoid confusion.

What activities help kids remember the letter P?

Making pretend pizza with paper toppings while saying "P is for pizza," tracing P in sand or shaving cream, squawking like a parrot, or coloring pictures of P words while saying each one aloud.
