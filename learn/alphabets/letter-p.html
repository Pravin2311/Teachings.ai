Letter P for Kids

The letter P is the sixteenth letter of the English alphabet. Capital P looks like P and small p looks like p. The letter P makes a popping “puh” sound.

Words That Start With P

Pen
Parrot
Pencil
Panda
Pizza

Letter P helps children learn strong popping sounds in speech and reading.

How To Write Letter P

To write capital P, draw one long line and one small curve. To write small p, draw a long line down and add a small circle.

Fun Facts About Letter P

  • P is used in many playful words.
  • P sounds like a gentle pop.

Practice Letter P

Kids can trace P, color P pictures, and say P words aloud.
