Letter E for Kids

The letter E is the fifth letter of the English alphabet. Capital E looks like E and small e looks like e. The letter E makes the sound “eh”.

Words That Start With E

Elephant
Egg
Eagle
Engine
Earth

How To Write Letter E

To write capital E, draw one straight line and three small lines. To write small e, draw a small curve with a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter E

  • E is a vowel.
  • E is used in many English words.
