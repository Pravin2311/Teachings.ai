The letter E is the fifth letter of the English alphabet. Capital E looks like E and small e looks like e. The letter E makes the sound “eh”. This page includes a free printable Letter E worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

How To Write Letter E

To write capital E, draw one straight line and three small lines. To write small e, draw a small curve with a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter E

  • E is a vowel.
  • E is used in many English words.

The Letter E Sound

Letter E makes two main sounds. The short e sound is heard in words like egg and elephant — this is usually the first sound preschoolers learn. The long e sound, which says its own name, is heard in words like bee and eagle.

Classroom Activity

Flap your arms like an eagle while the class chants "E is for eagle!" Then hand out plastic play eggs and have children open them one at a time, naming whatever small toy or picture is hiding inside.

Homeschool Activity

Crack a real (or play) egg together for a cooking activity, talking about how "egg" starts with the short E sound. Afterward, trace the letter E on paper, pointing out its three straight lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since E is a vowel with two different sounds, focus on the short e sound (as in egg) until it's solid before introducing the long e sound (as in bee).
Teachers: E is one of the most common letters in English — point it out in other words children already know to reinforce recognition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter E make?

Letter E makes two main sounds: the short e sound heard in egg and elephant, and the long e sound heard in bee and eagle. Preschoolers usually learn the short e sound first.

What are some easy words that start with E for preschoolers?

Easy E words for preschoolers include elephant, egg, eagle, engine, and earth — animal and food words tend to be the most memorable starting point.

How do I teach my child to write the letter E?

Practice one straight line down, then three short lines across the top, middle, and bottom for capital E. Lowercase e starts with a small horizontal line, curving up and around like a backwards C with a short tail.

Why is E called a vowel?

E is one of the five vowels (a, e, i, o, u) because it can be pronounced on its own without needing another letter, and every English word needs at least one vowel sound.

What activities help kids remember the letter E?

Flapping arms like an eagle while saying "E is for eagle," tracing E in sand or shaving cream, cracking a play egg and naming what's inside, or coloring pictures of E words while saying each one aloud.
