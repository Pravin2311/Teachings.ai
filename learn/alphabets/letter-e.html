Letter E: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

E is the fifth letter of the English alphabet. It comes after D and before F. E is a vowel. The capital (uppercase) letter is E and the small (lowercase) letter is e.

On this page you will learn the sounds E makes, how to write capital E and small e, words that use E, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

What Sound Does E Make?

  • short e: the sound at the start of egg and in the middle of bed. Examples: egg, elephant, bed, pen.
  • long e: E saying its own name. Examples: eagle, he, me, bee.
  • silent e: no sound at the end of a word, but it often makes the vowel before it say its name. Examples: cake, kite, rope, cute.

Practice the sound with pictures on E is for Egg, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital E and Small e

Capital E

  1. Start at the top. Draw a straight line down.
  2. Draw a line across the top, going right.
  3. Draw a slightly shorter line across the middle.
  4. Draw a line across the bottom.

Small e

  1. Start at the middle of the letter space. Draw a short line across to the right.
  2. Curve up, over to the left and down and around, like a c.
  3. Stop at the bottom on the right.

Common Mix-Ups

Capital E is easy to mix up with F. E has three lines across, but F has only two (top and middle).

Interesting Facts About E

  • E is the most common letter in written English. It appears in more words than any other letter.
  • E is one of the five vowels: A, E, I, O and U.
  • A silent E at the end of a word can change the sound of another vowel. It turns cap into cape and kit into kite.

Classroom Activity

Flap your arms like an eagle while the class chants "E is for eagle!" Then hand out plastic play eggs and have children open them one at a time, naming whatever small toy or picture is hiding inside.

Homeschool Activity

Crack a real (or play) egg together for a cooking activity, talking about how "egg" starts with the short E sound. Afterward, trace the letter E on paper, pointing out its three straight lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since E is a vowel with two different sounds, focus on the short e sound (as in egg) until it's solid before introducing the long e sound (as in bee).
Teachers: E is one of the most common letters in English — point it out in other words children already know to reinforce recognition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is E in the alphabet?

E is the fifth letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after D and before F.

Is E a vowel or a consonant?

E is a vowel. The five vowels are A, E, I, O and U. Every English word has at least one vowel sound, and vowels can make short and long sounds.

What sound does the letter E make?

E makes these sounds: short e as in egg; long e as in eagle; silent e as in cake.

What are some words that start with E?

Words that start with E include egg, elephant, eagle, engine, elbow, earth.

What is the silent E rule?

When a word ends in a vowel, a consonant and then e, the e is usually silent and makes the first vowel say its name: cap to cape, kit to kite, hop to hope.

Summary

E is the fifth letter of the alphabet and is a vowel. Its main sounds are short e, long e, silent e. Practice writing capital E and small e, and look for E in words around you. Next, learn the letter F.
