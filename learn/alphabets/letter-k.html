Letter K for Kids

The letter K is the eleventh letter of the English alphabet. Capital K looks like K and small k looks like k. The letter K makes a strong “kuh” sound.

Words That Start With K

Kite
Key
King
Kangaroo
Kitchen

The letter K helps children learn clear and strong consonant sounds.

How To Write Letter K

To write capital K, draw one long line and two slant lines. To write small k, draw a tall line and add two short slant lines.

Fun Facts About Letter K

  • K is often silent in some English words.
  • K is easy to recognize with sharp lines.

Practice Letter K

Kids can trace, color, and find K words in storybooks.

The Letter K Sound

Letter K makes a strong "kuh" sound, made at the back of the throat — the same sound as the hard C in words like cat. That overlap is exactly why kids sometimes mix K and hard-C words up, which is completely normal.

Classroom Activity

Take the class outside (or use a picture) to fly a kite while chanting "K is for kite!" Then hop around the room like kangaroos, calling out other K words along the way.

Homeschool Activity

Hunt for keys around the house — a door key, a toy key, a piano key — while saying "K is for key." Afterward, trace the letter K together on paper, pointing out its sharp, straight lines.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since K and hard C make the same sound, don't worry if your child mixes up which letter starts a word like "cat" or "kite" — that will sort itself out with more reading exposure.
Teachers: K pairs well with a "kitchen" theme — kids enjoy pretend cooking activities that reinforce the letter naturally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter K make?

Letter K makes a strong "kuh" sound, made at the back of the throat — the same sound as the hard C in words like cat, which is why kids sometimes mix the two letters up.

What are some easy words that start with K for preschoolers?

Easy K words for preschoolers include kite, key, king, kangaroo, and kitchen — toy and animal words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter K?

Practice one long vertical line, then two short slanted lines meeting it partway down for capital K. Lowercase k follows the same pattern, just shorter.

Why is the letter K sometimes silent?

K is silent when it comes before n at the start of a word, like in knee, knife, and know — worth pointing out once the basic K sound is solid.

What activities help kids remember the letter K?

Flying a kite outside while saying "K is for kite," tracing K in sand or shaving cream, hopping like a kangaroo, or coloring pictures of K words while saying each one aloud.
