Letter K: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

K is the 11th letter of the English alphabet. It comes after J and before L. K is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is K and the small (lowercase) letter is k.

How to Write Capital K and Small k

Follow the numbers: start each stroke at its numbered dot and move in the direction of the arrow. The blue top line, dashed middle line and red bottom line match the lines on handwriting paper.

Capital K (3 strokes)

  1. Draw a straight line down on the left.
  2. From the top right, draw a slanted line in to the middle of the first line.
  3. From that same point, draw a slanted line out to the bottom right.

Small k (2 strokes)

  1. Draw a tall straight line from the top line down to the bottom line.
  2. From the middle line on the right, draw a short slanted line in to the middle of the tall line.
  3. Then draw a short slanted line out to the bottom line.

Small k is a tall letter: it reaches up toward the top line, like b, d and h.

Common mix-up: K and C often make the same sound, which can make spelling tricky. At the end of short words, the k sound is often spelled -ck, as in duck and sock.

What Sound Does K Make?

  • k: made at the back of your mouth with a puff of air, no voice. Examples: kite, king, kangaroo, book.
  • silent k: no sound before n at the start of a word. Examples: knee, knife, know, knot.

Interesting Facts About K

  • The K in knee, knife and know is silent. Hundreds of years ago, people did say it.
  • The letter K comes from an ancient symbol for the palm of a hand.

Activities for Parents and Teachers

In the Classroom

Take the class outside (or use a picture) to fly a kite while chanting "K is for kite!" Then hop around the room like kangaroos, calling out other K words along the way.

At Home

Hunt for keys around the house — a door key, a toy key, a piano key — while saying "K is for key." Afterward, trace the letter K together on paper, pointing out its sharp, straight lines.

Tips

Parents: Since K and hard C make the same sound, don't worry if your child mixes up which letter starts a word like "cat" or "kite" — that will sort itself out with more reading exposure.
Teachers: K pairs well with a "kitchen" theme — kids enjoy pretend cooking activities that reinforce the letter naturally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is K in the alphabet?

K is the 11th letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after J and before L.

Is K a vowel or a consonant?

K is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter K make?

K makes these sounds: k as in kite; silent k as in knee.

How do you write a small k?

Draw a tall straight line from the top line down to the bottom line. From the middle line on the right, draw a short slanted line in to the middle of the tall line. Then draw a short slanted line out to the bottom line. Small k is a tall letter: it reaches up toward the top line, like b, d and h.

What are some words that start with K?

Words that start with K include kite, king, kangaroo, key, kitten, koala.

Should a word start with C or K?

There is a helpful pattern: use k before e, i or y (kite, key) and c before a, o or u (cat, cot, cup). There are exceptions, so reading lots of words helps.

Summary
