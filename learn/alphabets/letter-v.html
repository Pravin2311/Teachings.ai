The letter V is the twenty-second letter of the alphabet. Capital V looks like V and small v looks like v. The letter V makes a gentle “vuh” sound. This page includes a free printable Letter V worksheet with tracing practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

The letter V helps children learn soft vibration sounds in speech.

How To Write Letter V

To write capital V, draw two slant lines. To write small v, draw two short slant lines.

Fun Facts About Letter V

  • V is shaped like a valley.
  • Many place names start with V.

Practice Letter V

Kids can trace V, color V pictures, and read V words aloud.

The Letter V Sound

Letter V makes a gentle "vuh" sound, made by touching the top teeth to the bottom lip and buzzing gently. It's similar to the F sound, but with a voiced buzz — place a finger on your throat to feel the difference.

Classroom Activity

Line up chairs to make a pretend van and take turns "driving" while chanting "V is for van!" Then play a short clip of violin music, calling out other V words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Cut up vegetables together for a snack while saying "V is for vegetable." Afterward, trace the letter V on paper — it's just two straight lines meeting at a point, like a checkmark.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up V and F, have them place a finger on their throat — V has a buzzing vibration that F doesn't.
Teachers: V pairs well with a "sounds like a checkmark" visual cue — kids often remember the shape better when it's tied to something familiar.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter V make?

Letter V makes a gentle "vuh" sound, made by touching the top teeth to the bottom lip and buzzing gently — similar to F, but with a voiced buzz instead of just breath.

What are some easy words that start with V for preschoolers?

Easy V words for preschoolers include van, vegetable, violin, volcano, and village — vehicle and food words are especially memorable for young learners.

How do I teach my child to write the letter V?

Practice two slanted lines meeting at a point for capital V, like a checkmark. Lowercase v is the same shape, just smaller.

How can I tell letter V and U apart when teaching my child?

V has sharp, straight lines meeting at a point, while U has a rounded, curved bottom. Describing the difference as "pointy" vs. "round" can help.

What activities help kids remember the letter V?

Pretending to drive a van while saying "V is for van," tracing V in sand or shaving cream, listening to violin music, or coloring pictures of V words while saying each one aloud.
