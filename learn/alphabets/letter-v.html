Letter V: Sounds, Words and How to Write It

V is the 22nd letter of the English alphabet. It comes after U and before W. V is a consonant. The capital (uppercase) letter is V and the small (lowercase) letter is v.

On this page you will learn the sound V makes, how to write capital V and small v, words that use V, and a quick quiz to check what you know.

Letter V at a Glance

What Sound Does V Make?

  • v: made by resting your top teeth on your bottom lip and buzzing, with your voice switched on. Examples: van, vase, violin, five.

Practice the sound with pictures on V is for Van, which shows how to make the sound with your mouth.

How to Write Capital V and Small v

Capital V

  1. Start at the top on the left. Draw a slanted line down to a point at the bottom.
  2. Draw a slanted line back up to the top on the right.

Small v

  1. Make the same shape, but smaller, between the middle line and the bottom line.

Try tracing it on screen in the letter tracing game, then on paper with a crayon.

V also appears in the middle or at the end of words: five, love, seven, river, glove.

Common Mix-Ups

V can be mixed up with U (which has a round bottom) and Y (which has a tail). V has a sharp point at the bottom.

Interesting Facts About V

  • V is a consonant.
  • English words almost never end in V. Words like have, love and give add a silent e after it.
  • V and F use the same mouth shape. V uses your voice and F does not.

Classroom Activity

Line up chairs to make a pretend van and take turns "driving" while chanting "V is for van!" Then play a short clip of violin music, calling out other V words the class can think of.

Homeschool Activity

Cut up vegetables together for a snack while saying "V is for vegetable." Afterward, trace the letter V on paper — it's just two straight lines meeting at a point, like a checkmark.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: If your child mixes up V and F, have them place a finger on their throat — V has a buzzing vibration that F doesn't.
Teachers: V pairs well with a "sounds like a checkmark" visual cue — kids often remember the shape better when it's tied to something familiar.

Frequently Asked Questions

What number letter is V in the alphabet?

V is the 22nd letter of the 26-letter English alphabet. It comes after U and before W.

Is V a vowel or a consonant?

V is a consonant. All letters except the vowels A, E, I, O and U are consonants (Y can be both).

What sound does the letter V make?

V makes this sound: v as in van.

What are some words that start with V?

Words that start with V include van, vase, violin, volcano, vegetables, vest.

Why do words like have and give end in e?

English words almost never end in v, so a silent e is added after it. In these words, the e does not make the vowel long.

Summary

V is the 22nd letter of the alphabet and is a consonant. Its main sound is v. Practice writing capital V and small v, and look for V in words around you. Next, learn the letter W.
