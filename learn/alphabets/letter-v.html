Letter V for Kids

The letter V is the twenty-second letter of the alphabet. Capital V looks like V and small v looks like v. The letter V makes a gentle “vuh” sound.

Words That Start With V

Van
Vegetable
Violin
Volcano
Village

The letter V helps children learn soft vibration sounds in speech.

How To Write Letter V

To write capital V, draw two slant lines. To write small v, draw two short slant lines.

Fun Facts About Letter V

  • V is shaped like a valley.
  • Many place names start with V.

Practice Letter V

Kids can trace V, color V pictures, and read V words aloud.

More to Discover

  • V is one of the less common letters to start English words.
  • To say V, your top teeth gently touch your bottom lip.
