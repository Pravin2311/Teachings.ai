How To Write Letter Q

To write capital Q, draw a big circle and add a small line. To write small q, draw a circle and a tail.

Fun Facts About Letter Q

  • Q almost always comes with U.
  • Q is one of the rare letters in English.

Practice Letter Q

Kids can trace Q, match Q words, and color Q pictures.

The Letter Q Sound

Letter Q makes a "kw" sound, like the start of queen and quick. In English, Q is almost always followed by the letter U, so children usually learn the two letters together as a "qu" team rather than Q on its own.

Classroom Activity

Make paper crowns and let each child be "queen or king for a minute" while the class chants "Q is for queen!" Then whisper other Q words quietly, since "quiet" starts with Q too.

Homeschool Activity

Snuggle under a quilt or blanket together while saying "Q is for quilt." Afterward, trace the letter Q on paper, pointing out how it looks like an O with a little tail.

Tips for Parents & Teachers

Parents: Since real Q words are rarer than other letters, don't worry if your child needs more repetition and pictures to remember them compared to letters like B or C.
Teachers: Always teach "qu" together as a pair rather than isolating Q — it reflects how the letter actually works in real words.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter Q make?

Letter Q makes a "kw" sound, like the start of queen and quick. In English, Q is almost always followed by U, so children usually learn them together as a "qu" team.

What are some easy words that start with Q for preschoolers?

Easy Q words for preschoolers include queen, quilt, quick, quiet, and quiz — pairing each one with a picture or action helps it stick since Q words are less common.

How do I teach my child to write the letter Q?

Practice a big circle with a small diagonal line crossing the bottom-right for capital Q — it looks almost like an O with a little tail. Lowercase q is a small circle with a straight line hanging down on the right.

Why does Q almost always appear with U?

In English, Q is followed by U in nearly every word (queen, quick, quiet) because the "kw" sound needs both letters together to be spelled correctly.

What activities help kids remember the letter Q?

Wearing a paper crown while pretending to be a queen, tracing Q in sand or shaving cream, snuggling under a quilt while saying "Q is for quilt," or coloring pictures of Q words while saying each one aloud.
