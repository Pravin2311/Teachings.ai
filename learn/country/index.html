Why Learn About Countries?

Learning about countries helps us understand how people live in different parts of the world. We can learn about new languages, try new foods, and see amazing animals that don't live near us!

Continents of the World

Countries are grouped into big lands called Continents. There are 7 continents:

  • 🌍 Africa: Home to lions, elephants, and the Sahara Desert.
  • 🌎 Americas: North and South America, home to the USA, Canada, and Brazil.
  • 🌏 Asia: The biggest continent, home to India, China, and Japan.
  • 🌏 Europe: Home to France, Italy, and many castles.
  • 🌏 Australia: An island continent with kangaroos and koalas.
  • 🧊 Antarctica: A frozen land where only penguins live!
