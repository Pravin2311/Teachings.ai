🇮🇹 Italy

The flag of Italy

Italy is a country located in Europe, known for pizza & pasta. Every country has its own flag, food, and amazing places to explore!

🌍 Where is Italy?

Italy is found in Europe. Each country has a special flag, and Italy's flag is a symbol that represents its people and history.

🌟 Fun Facts about Italy

  • Pizza and pasta both come from Italy, and are now loved all around the world.
  • Italy is shaped like a big boot when you look at it on a map!
  • The famous Leaning Tower of Pisa in Italy tilts to one side.
  • Italy is home to ancient Roman landmarks like the Colosseum.

✨ What Makes Italy Special?

Italy is known around the world for pizza & pasta. Learning about different countries helps us understand and appreciate the many amazing cultures around our planet!
