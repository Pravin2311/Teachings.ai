🇵🇹 Portugal

The flag of Portugal

Portugal is a country located in Europe, known for sunny coastlines. Every country has its own flag, food, and amazing places to explore!

🌍 Where is Portugal?

Portugal is found in Europe. Each country has a special flag, and Portugal's flag is a symbol that represents its people and history.

🌟 Fun Facts about Portugal

  • Portugal has beautiful sunny beaches along the Atlantic Ocean.
  • Portuguese sailors were some of the first explorers to sail all around the world.
  • Portugal is famous for colorful tiles called "azulejos" decorating buildings.
  • Portugal is one of the oldest countries in Europe, with borders that have barely changed in centuries.

✨ What Makes Portugal Special?

Portugal is known around the world for sunny coastlines. Learning about different countries helps us understand and appreciate the many amazing cultures around our planet!
