🇷🇺 Russia

The flag of Russia

Russia is a country located in Europe & Asia, known for the biggest country. Every country has its own flag, food, and amazing places to explore!

🌍 Where is Russia?

Russia is found in Europe & Asia. Each country has a special flag, and Russia's flag is a symbol that represents its people and history.

🌟 Fun Facts about Russia

  • Russia is the largest country in the world by land area.
  • It is so big that it spans 11 different time zones!
  • Russia is home to colorful, onion-domed buildings called St. Basil’s Cathedral.
  • Some parts of Russia are so cold that temperatures can drop far below freezing.

✨ What Makes Russia Special?

Russia is known around the world for the biggest country. Learning about different countries helps us understand and appreciate the many amazing cultures around our planet!

What continent is Russia located in?

🏙️ Capital, Language & Currency

  • 🏛️ Capital City: Moscow
  • 🗣️ Language: Russian
  • 💰 Currency: Russian Ruble
  • 🥟 Famous Food: Borscht (beet soup) and pelmeni (dumplings).

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children color the onion-shaped domes of St. Basil's Cathedral, then compare Russia's size to your own country on a map.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about how spread out Russia's 11 time zones are, and compare it to how many time zones your own country has. Draw a snowy winter scene since parts of Russia get extremely cold.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What continent is Russia in?

Russia spans both Europe and Asia.

What language do people speak in Russia?

Russian.

How many time zones does Russia have?

11 time zones, more than any other country.

What is St. Basil's Cathedral?

A famous building in Moscow with colorful onion-shaped domes.

What activities help kids learn about Russia?

Drawing the cathedral domes, talking about Russia's size, discussing cold weather, or coloring a printable worksheet.
