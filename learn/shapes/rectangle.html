Rectangle Shape for Kids: Sides, Corners and How to Draw It

A rectangle is a flat shape with 4 straight sides and 4 square corners, where the opposite sides are the same length. It has 4 straight sides (two long and two short) and 4 corners. Below you will find its properties, how to draw it, where to spot it in real life, and a quick quiz.

📐 What Makes a Rectangle Special?

A rectangle has 4 straight sides and 4 square corners, called right angles. Its opposite sides are the same length and parallel: two long sides and two short sides. A square is a special kind of rectangle where all four sides are the same length. To find a rectangle's perimeter, add up all four sides.

🌟 Fun Facts about Rectangle

  • A rectangle has 4 right angles — corners that form a perfect L shape.
  • Opposite sides of a rectangle are always the same length.
  • Most doors, windows, and books are shaped like rectangles.
  • A square is actually a special kind of rectangle where all 4 sides are equal!

How to Draw a Rectangle

  1. Draw a long straight line across.
  2. Draw a shorter straight line down from each end.
  3. Join the bottoms with another long line, the same length as the top.

🔎 Where Can You Find a Rectangle?

You can spot the rectangle shape in everyday life, like a door, a book, a phone, and a picture frame. Try going on a shape hunt around your house or classroom — how many rectangles can you find today?

Common Mix-Ups

Every square is a special kind of rectangle, with all four sides the same length. But most rectangles are not squares, because two sides are longer.

🏫 Classroom Activity: Shape Hunt & Matching

Send children on a hunt for rectangular objects — a book, a door, a phone — and gather the finds on a table. Then play a matching game: draw several shapes on cards and have kids sort out only the rectangles.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Line up a square block and a rectangle block side by side and measure each side with a ruler or your fingers, talking about how the rectangle's sides aren't all equal like the square's.

🔬 STEM Connection

Rectangles stack and tile neatly, which is why bricks, books, and boxes are almost always rectangular — try stacking rectangular blocks versus round ones to see which stacks better.
