🟦 Square Shape

A bright square for little learners to explore!

A Square is a shape with 4 equal sides and 4 equal square corners. Shapes are all around us, and learning to spot a square helps you understand the world of math and geometry!

📐 What Makes a Square Special?

Every shape has its own special features that make it easy to recognize. A square has 4 equal sides and 4 equal square corners. Once you know what to look for, you can find squares everywhere you go!

🌟 Fun Facts about Square

  • All four sides of a square are exactly the same length.
  • A square has 4 right-angle corners, just like a rectangle.
  • Chess and checkers boards are made of many small squares.
  • If you cut a square in half diagonally, you get two triangles!

🔎 Where Can You Find a Square?

You can spot the square shape in everyday life, like a checkerboard tile, a window pane, and a picture frame. Try going on a shape hunt around your house or classroom — how many squares can you find today?
