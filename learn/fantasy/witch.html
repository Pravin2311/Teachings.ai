🧙 Witch

Meet the Witch!

The Witch is a favorite imaginative character found in fairy tales and storybooks. Fantasy characters like this spark our imagination and are the stars of countless fun stories!

✨ Where Does the Witch Come From?

The Witch is a character often found in fairy tales and storybooks. Storytellers and artists have imagined characters like this for a very long time, and they continue to appear in books, movies, and games today.

🌟 Fun Facts about Witch

  • In fairy tales, witches are magical characters who can cast spells.
  • Storybook witches are often shown wearing a pointy hat and flying on a broomstick.
  • Not all storybook witches are the same — some tales show good witches who help heroes!
  • Witches appear in many classic fairy tales, like "Hansel and Gretel" and "The Wizard of Oz."

📖 Fun with Storytelling

Characters like the Witch help us use our imagination! Try drawing your own picture of a witch, or make up a short story about an adventure they might go on.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw their own witch character, choosing whether she is a helpful good witch or a spooky storybook witch, and invent a spell she might cast.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Read or retell a favorite fairy tale that includes a witch, like "Hansel and Gretel," and talk about the character's role in the story.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What can witches do in fairy tales?

Cast magical spells and make imaginative things happen.

What do storybook witches usually wear?

A pointy hat, often flying on a broomstick.

Are all storybook witches the same?

No — some tales show good witches who help heroes.

What famous fairy tales include witches?

"Hansel and Gretel" and "The Wizard of Oz."

What activities help kids learn about witches?

Drawing a witch, inventing a spell, retelling a fairy tale, or coloring a printable worksheet.
