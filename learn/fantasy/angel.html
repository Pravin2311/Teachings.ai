😇 Angel

Meet the Angel!

The Angel is a favorite imaginative character found in holiday stories, art, and folklore. Fantasy characters like this spark our imagination and are the stars of countless fun stories!

✨ Where Does the Angel Come From?

The Angel is a character often found in holiday stories, art, and folklore. Storytellers and artists have imagined characters like this for a very long time, and they continue to appear in books, movies, and games today.

🌟 Fun Facts about Angel

  • Angels are often shown in stories and art with soft, feathery wings and a warm glow.
  • In many stories, angels are kind, gentle characters who help and watch over others.
  • Angels are a popular decoration during winter holidays, often shown on top of trees.
  • Artists have painted angels for hundreds of years, usually dressed in flowing robes.

📖 Fun with Storytelling

Characters like the Angel help us use our imagination! Try drawing your own picture of a angel, or make up a short story about an adventure they might go on.
