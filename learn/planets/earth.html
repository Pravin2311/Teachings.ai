Earth for Kids: Our Beautiful Blue Home

Earth is the only planet known to have life!

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place in the entire universe where we know life exists! It is a beautiful blue and green ball covered mostly by water. Earth has air to breathe, water to drink, and land to live on. It spins around once every 24 hours (giving us day and night) and travels around the Sun once every 365 days (giving us a year). Let's explore our amazing home!

🌍 Why Is Earth Special?

Earth is just right for life! Here is why:

  • Water: About 71% of Earth is covered in water (oceans, lakes, rivers). Life needs water to survive.
  • Air: Earth has an atmosphere full of oxygen, which humans and animals need to breathe.
  • Temperature: Earth is not too hot (like Venus) and not too cold (like Neptune). It is "just right" for plants and animals.
  • Protection: Our atmosphere acts like a blanket, keeping us warm and protecting us from harmful space rays.

🌑 Earth's Best Friend: The Moon

Earth has one natural satellite called The Moon. It orbits around Earth and changes shape every night (phases). The Moon doesn't make its own light; it reflects sunlight. The Moon's gravity pulls on Earth's oceans, causing the tides to go in and out. Humans landed on the Moon for the first time in 1969!

🚀 Amazing Earth Facts

  • Name: Earth is the only planet not named after a Greek or Roman god. Its name comes from Old English meaning "ground."
  • Shape: Earth is not a perfect sphere; it is slightly squashed at the poles, making it an "oblate spheroid."
  • Speed: Earth zooms through space at 67,000 miles per hour as it orbits the Sun!
  • Oldest Rock: The oldest rocks found on Earth are over 4 billion years old.
  • Population: Over 8 billion people live on Earth today!

🌋 Inside the Earth

Earth is like an onion with layers:

  • Crust: The thin, hard outer layer where we live. It has continents and ocean floors.
  • Mantle: A thick layer of hot, soft rock beneath the crust.
  • Outer Core: A liquid layer made of iron and nickel. It creates Earth's magnetic field.
  • Inner Core: A super-hot solid ball of iron at the very center.
