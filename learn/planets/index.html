What is the Solar System?

The Solar System is our cosmic neighborhood! It consists of the Sun (a giant star) in the center, and eight planets that orbit around it. There are also dwarf planets like Pluto, moons, asteroids, and comets.

Two Types of Planets

  • 🪨 Rocky Planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. They have solid surfaces you could stand on.
  • 💨 Gas Giants: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. They are huge balls of gas and ice with no solid surface.

The Planets in Order

Our solar system has 8 planets. In order from the Sun they are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. A memory trick: My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles. The first four are small and rocky; the last four are giant planets made mostly of gas and ice. Pluto was called the ninth planet until 2006, when astronomers decided it is a dwarf planet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many planets are in our solar system?

There are 8 planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. Pluto is a dwarf planet.

Is the Moon a planet?

No. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite, which means it travels around Earth rather than around the Sun on its own.

Why is Pluto not a planet anymore?

In 2006 astronomers agreed that a planet must clear other objects out of its path around the Sun. Pluto shares its area with many other icy objects, so it is called a dwarf planet.
