Why Eat Fruits?

Fruits are nature's candy! They are full of vitamins and fiber that help you grow strong, keep your tummy happy, and give you energy to play. Eating different colored fruits gives your body different superpowers!

Rainbow of Health

  • 🔴 Red Fruits: Good for your heart and memory (like Apples & Strawberries).
  • 🟠 Orange Fruits: Great for your eyes and immune system (like Oranges & Mangoes).
  • 🟡 Yellow Fruits: Help your bones and digestion (like Bananas).
  • 🟢 Green Fruits: Keep your teeth and bones strong (like Kiwi & Grapes).
  • 🟣 Purple Fruits: Help your brain think clearly (like Grapes & Cherries).
