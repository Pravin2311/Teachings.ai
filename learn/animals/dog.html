Dog for Kids

Dogs are our best friends! They bark, wag their tails, and love to play fetch. They come in many sizes and colors.

Where Do Dogs Live?

Dogs live in homes with people. They sleep in beds, crates, or cozy corners.

What Do Dogs Eat?

Dogs eat meat, bones, and special dog food. They also love treats and sometimes steal snacks!

Fun Facts About Dogs

  • Dogs can smell 10,000 times better than humans!
  • They wag their tails to show happiness.
  • Baby dogs are called puppies.
  • Some dogs can learn over 100 tricks!

Why Dogs Are Special

Dogs are loyal, smart, and always excited to see you. They protect homes and make great companions.
