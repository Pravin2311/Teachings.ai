Dog for Kids

Dogs are our best friends! They bark, wag their tails, and love to play fetch. They come in many sizes and colors.

Where Do Dogs Live?

Dogs live in homes with people. They sleep in beds, crates, or cozy corners.

What Do Dogs Eat?

Dogs eat meat, bones, and special dog food. They also love treats and sometimes steal snacks!

Fun Facts About Dogs

  • Dogs can smell 10,000 times better than humans!
  • They wag their tails to show happiness.
  • Baby dogs are called puppies.
  • Some dogs can learn over 100 tricks!

Why Dogs Are Special

Dogs are loyal, smart, and always excited to see you. They protect homes and make great companions.

More to Discover

  • A dog's sense of smell is up to 100,000 times stronger than a human's.
  • A dog wagging its tail usually means it's happy and excited.

New words to learn: bark, paw, leash, breed

Try This: Practice teaching a pretend trick, like "sit" or "shake," to a stuffed animal dog!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice gentle dog manners: how to ask before petting a dog, how to stay calm around one, and how to "sit" and "stay" like they're training a puppy. Talk about responsibility if a family has a pet dog.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look up pictures of different dog breeds together and talk about how they're all different sizes and colors but are still all dogs. Ask your child which breed they'd choose as a pet and why.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do dogs live?

Most pet dogs live in homes with people, sleeping in beds, crates, or cozy corners.

What do dogs eat?

Dogs eat meat, bones, and special dog food, plus the occasional treat.

Why do dogs wag their tails?

Dogs usually wag their tails when happy or excited, though the wag can mean different things.

How good is a dog's sense of smell?

A dog's sense of smell can be tens of thousands of times stronger than a human's.

What activities help kids learn about dogs?

Practicing a bark, teaching a pretend trick, talking about being gentle with dogs, or coloring a printable worksheet.
