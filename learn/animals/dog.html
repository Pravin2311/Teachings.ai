Dog for Kids

A dog is a loyal and friendly pet animal. Dogs protect homes and love their owners.

Where Do Dogs Live?

Dogs live in homes, farms and streets.

What Do Dogs Eat?

Dogs eat meat, rice and dog food.

Fun Facts About Dogs

  • Dogs can run very fast.
  • Dogs are good guards.
  • Dogs can understand humans.

Why Dogs Are Special

Dogs are best friends of humans.
