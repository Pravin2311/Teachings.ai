Tiger for Kids

Tigers are big, beautiful cats with orange fur and black stripes. They are the largest cats in the world and love to hunt at night.

Where Do Tigers Live?

Tigers live in forests, grasslands, and mangroves in Asia — especially in India, Russia, and Indonesia.

What Do Tigers Eat?

Tigers eat deer, wild boar, and other animals. They are stealthy hunters and pounce on their prey.

Fun Facts About Tigers

  • Every tiger has a unique stripe pattern — like a fingerprint!
  • They can swim and love water.
  • Baby tigers are called cubs.
  • They can leap up to 30 feet in one jump!

Why Tigers Are Special

Tigers are powerful, graceful, and majestic. They’re also endangered — so we must protect them!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Give children orange paper and black strips to create their own unique tiger stripe pattern, just like a real tiger's one-of-a-kind stripes. Practice a tiger roar and talk about why tigers are endangered.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about how tigers, unlike most cats, actually love swimming — ask your child if they know any other animals that like water. Compare a tiger's size to a house cat using pictures.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do tigers live?

Tigers live in forests, grasslands, and mangroves across Asia, especially India and Russia.

What do tigers eat?

Tigers eat deer, wild boar, and other animals, sneaking up close before pouncing.

Why does every tiger have different stripes?

Every tiger's stripe pattern is unique, like a human fingerprint.

Do tigers like water?

Yes — tigers are strong swimmers and often cool off in rivers and ponds.

What activities help kids learn about tigers?

Painting stripe patterns, practicing a roar, talking about tiger conservation, or coloring a printable worksheet.
