Tigers for Kids

Tigers are the biggest cats in the world. They have orange fur with black stripes, and they are strong, quiet hunters. Tigers live alone in the wild, and they do most of their hunting at dusk and at night.

Tiger Facts at a Glance

  • Animal group: mammal, in the cat family
  • Food: meat, mostly deer and wild pigs
  • Home: forests, grasslands and swamps in Asia
  • Size: a big male can be about 3 metres (10 feet) long from nose to tail tip
  • Babies: called cubs; they stay with their mother for about two years
  • Sound: a loud roar, plus a friendly puffing sound called chuffing
  • Status: endangered, with only a few thousand left in the wild

What Do Tigers Eat?

Tigers are carnivores: they eat only meat. Their main food is big animals with hooves, such as deer, wild pigs (wild boar) and wild cattle like water buffalo. When big animals are hard to find, tigers also eat smaller animals, such as monkeys, birds and fish.

Tigers do not eat every day. When a tiger catches a big animal, it eats a very large meal. It often drags the food to a hidden spot and comes back to it over the next few days. Then it may not hunt again for several days.

How Do Tigers Hunt?

Tigers hunt alone, mostly at dusk and at night. A tiger does not chase its prey for a long way like a cheetah. Instead it:

  1. Sneaks slowly and quietly through tall grass, keeping its body low.
  2. Waits until it is very close, hidden by its stripes.
  3. Pounces with a fast rush and a big leap, usually from the side or behind.

Soft pads on a tiger's paws make its steps quiet, and its sharp claws stay tucked away until it needs them. Even so, most hunts fail. The animal often hears or smells the tiger and runs away, so the tiger has to try again another time.

The Tiger Food Chain

A food chain shows who eats whom. In a tiger's forest, a simple food chain looks like this:

☀️ Sun→🌿 Grass and leaves→🦌 Deer→🐅 Tiger

Plants use sunlight to grow. Deer eat the plants, and tigers eat the deer. Grown-up tigers are apex predators, which means they are at the top of the food chain and no other animal usually hunts them. Tiger cubs are different: they can be in danger from other animals when their mother is away hunting.

Tigers help keep their forest healthy. If there were no tigers, there could be too many deer, and the deer could eat too many young plants. When a forest has tigers, it usually has plenty of other animals and plants too.

Where Do Tigers Live? (Tiger Habitat)

Wild tigers live only in Asia. They live in many kinds of places: hot, wet rainforests, tall grasslands, mangrove swamps by the sea, and cold, snowy forests in Russia. India has more wild tigers than any other country. Tigers also live in Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and a few other countries.

Every tiger needs three things in its home: animals to eat, water to drink and cool off in, and thick plants to hide in. Each tiger has its own area, called a territory. It marks the edges with scratches on trees and scent so other tigers know to stay away.

Kinds of Tigers

All tigers are one kind (species) of animal, but tigers from different places look a little different. Here are names children often see in books:

White tigers are not a separate kind. They are tigers born with a rare color that makes their fur white with dark stripes.

Tiger Cubs

A mother tiger usually has two to four cubs at a time. The cubs are born blind and helpless, and she hides them in a safe den. After a few months they follow her around and play-fight, which helps them practise pouncing. The father does not help. Cubs stay with their mother for about two years, learning to hunt, before they go off to find their own territory.

Amazing Tiger Facts

  • Every tiger has its own stripe pattern, like a fingerprint. Its skin is striped too, not just its fur.
  • Orange fur seems easy to spot, but deer cannot see orange the way people do. Scientists think a tiger may look greenish to a deer, so it blends in with the plants.
  • Unlike most cats, tigers love water and are strong swimmers.
  • A tiger's roar is so loud it can be heard far across the forest.
  • Tigers have white spots on the backs of their ears. Cubs may use them to follow their mother.
  • Tigers are endangered. In some places, such as India and Nepal, careful protection has helped the number of wild tigers go up again.

🏫 Classroom Activities

  • Sneak and pounce (Pre-K to K): one child is the "tiger" and creeps slowly toward a beanbag "deer" while the class watches for any sound. Talk about why tigers need to be quiet.
  • My stripe pattern (Pre-K to K): give each child orange paper and black strips to make their own stripes. Compare them: are any two the same?
  • Food chain line-up (Grade 1 to 2): give four children cards that say sun, grass, deer and tiger. Have the class put them in order and explain what each one needs.
  • How long is a tiger? (K to Grade 2): measure 3 metres (10 feet) on the floor with tape. How many children lying head to toe does it take to match a big tiger?

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Compare a tiger with a pet cat or a cat in a picture book. Make two columns: the same (whiskers, claws that tuck away, they both pounce) and different (size, stripes, tigers love swimming). Then find India and Russia on a map and talk about how a tiger in the snow and a tiger in the jungle might look different.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What do tigers eat?

Tigers are carnivores, so they eat only meat. Their main food is big animals such as deer, wild pigs and wild cattle. When they cannot find big prey, they also eat smaller animals like monkeys, birds and fish.

How often do tigers eat?

Not every day. Most hunts fail, so a tiger may go several days without a big meal. When it does catch something large, it eats a lot at once and comes back to the same meal over a few days.

What eats tigers?

Grown-up tigers are at the top of the food chain, so no other animal usually hunts them. Tiger cubs can be in danger from other animals when their mother is away. The biggest danger to tigers is people, through hunting and cutting down forests.

Where do tigers live?

Wild tigers live only in Asia. They live in rainforests, grasslands, mangrove swamps and snowy forests, in countries such as India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Russia, Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia. India has the most wild tigers.

Why do tigers have stripes?

Stripes help a tiger hide in tall grass and shadows while it sneaks up on prey. Every tiger's stripe pattern is different, like a fingerprint, and its skin is striped too.

Do tigers like water?

Yes. Unlike most cats, tigers are strong swimmers. They often cool off in rivers and pools on hot days and can even catch food in the water.
