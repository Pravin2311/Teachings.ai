Wild Animals, Farm Animals and Pets

A fun way to sort animals is by where they live. Wild animals, like lions, tigers, giraffes, zebras, kangaroos and penguins, live in nature and find their own food. Farm animals, like cows, horses and donkeys, are raised by people. Pets, like cats and dogs, live with families in their homes. Dolphins are wild animals that live in the sea.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between wild animals and farm animals?

Wild animals live in nature and find their own food, like lions and giraffes. Farm animals, like cows, horses and donkeys, are raised and cared for by people.

Why do kids love learning about animals?

Animals are colorful, make interesting sounds, and connect to stories and pretend play kids already enjoy, which makes new vocabulary stick.

What is a good way to extend animal learning at home?

Visit a zoo or farm, watch a nature documentary together, or ask your child to act out how their favorite animal moves and sounds.
