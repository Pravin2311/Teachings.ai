Giraffe for Kids

A giraffe is the tallest animal in the world. Giraffes have very long necks.

Where Do Giraffes Live?

Giraffes live in grasslands and forests.

What Do Giraffes Eat?

Giraffes eat leaves from tall trees.

Fun Facts About Giraffes

  • Giraffes have long tongues.
  • Giraffes can see far.
  • Giraffes sleep standing.

Why Giraffes Are Special

Giraffes can eat leaves other animals cannot reach.
