Giraffe for Kids

Giraffes are the tallest animals on Earth! They have long necks, spotted coats, and walk gracefully across the savanna.

Where Do Giraffes Live?

Giraffes live in Africa’s grasslands and woodlands. They need tall trees to eat from.

What Do Giraffes Eat?

Giraffes eat leaves, especially from acacia trees. Their long tongues help them grab leaves without getting pricked!

Fun Facts About Giraffes

  • Giraffes have the longest necks of any animal — up to 6 feet long!
  • Their tongues are blue-black and can be 18 inches long.
  • Baby giraffes are called calves.
  • They only need to drink water every few days.

Why Giraffes Are Special

Giraffes are gentle giants who watch over the savanna. They’re amazing at reaching high branches no other animal can!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Use a measuring tape to show 6 feet on the floor or wall and compare it to each child's height — that's how long a giraffe's neck is! Talk about why a long neck helps giraffes reach food other animals can't.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Practice sticking out your tongue and talk about how a giraffe's tongue is nearly as long as a ruler. Draw a giraffe and count its spots, then talk about how every giraffe's spot pattern is unique, like a fingerprint.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do giraffes live?

Giraffes live in Africa's grasslands and woodlands, near the tall trees they eat from.

What do giraffes eat?

Giraffes eat leaves, especially from acacia trees, using their long tongues to avoid thorns.

How long is a giraffe's neck?

A giraffe's neck can be up to 6 feet long, the longest of any animal.

How long is a giraffe's tongue?

A giraffe's tongue can be up to 18 inches long and is dark blue-black in color.

What activities help kids learn about giraffes?

Measuring 6 feet to compare to a giraffe's neck, stretching tall, watching a nature video, or coloring a printable worksheet.
