Giraffe for Kids

Giraffes are the tallest animals on Earth! They have long necks, spotted coats, and walk gracefully across the savanna.

Where Do Giraffes Live?

Giraffes live in Africa’s grasslands and woodlands. They need tall trees to eat from.

What Do Giraffes Eat?

Giraffes eat leaves, especially from acacia trees. Their long tongues help them grab leaves without getting pricked!

Fun Facts About Giraffes

  • Giraffes have the longest necks of any animal — up to 6 feet long!
  • Their tongues are blue-black and can be 18 inches long.
  • Baby giraffes are called calves.
  • They only need to drink water every few days.

Why Giraffes Are Special

Giraffes are gentle giants who watch over the savanna. They’re amazing at reaching high branches no other animal can!
