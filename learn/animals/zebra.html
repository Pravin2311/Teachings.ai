Zebra for Kids

Zebras are wild horses with black-and-white stripes! No two zebras have the same pattern — each one is unique.

Where Do Zebras Live?

Zebras live in Africa’s grasslands and savannas. They travel in groups called herds.

What Do Zebras Eat?

Zebras eat grass, leaves, and shrubs. They spend most of the day grazing.

Fun Facts About Zebras

  • Each zebra’s stripe pattern is unique — like human fingerprints!
  • They run fast to escape lions and hyenas.
  • Baby zebras are called foals.
  • They sleep standing up.

Why Zebras Are Special

Zebras are beautiful, fast, and smart. Their stripes help them hide from predators — nature’s camouflage!
