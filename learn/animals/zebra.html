Zebra for Kids

A zebra is an animal with black and white stripes. Each zebra has different stripes.

Where Do Zebras Live?

Zebras live in grasslands.

What Do Zebras Eat?

Zebras eat grass.

Fun Facts About Zebras

  • No two zebras have same stripes.
  • Zebras run fast.
  • Zebras live in groups.

Why Zebras Are Special

Zebra stripes help protect them.
