Elephant for Kids

An elephant is the largest land animal. Elephants have long trunks and big ears.

Where Do Elephants Live?

Elephants live in forests and grasslands.

What Do Elephants Eat?

Elephants eat grass, leaves, fruits and plants.

Fun Facts About Elephants

  • Elephants are very intelligent.
  • Elephants love water.
  • Elephants remember places.

Why Elephants Are Special

Elephants care for their families and protect babies.
