Elephant for Kids

Elephants are the biggest land animals! They have long trunks, big ears, and strong legs. They live in groups called herds.

Where Do Elephants Live?

Elephants live in jungles, savannas, and forests in Africa and Asia.

What Do Elephants Eat?

Elephants eat leaves, grass, fruit, and tree bark. They need to eat a lot — up to 300 pounds a day!

Fun Facts About Elephants

  • Elephants use their trunks to drink, eat, and hug friends.
  • They can remember places and people for years.
  • Baby elephants are called calves.
  • They communicate by making low rumbles you can’t hear!

Elephant Families

Elephant herds are led by the oldest, wisest female, called the matriarch. She remembers where to find food and water, even in places she hasn't visited in years, and the whole herd follows her lead.

Why Elephants Are Special

Elephants are wise, gentle, and very social. They take care of their babies and protect each other.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children use one arm as a pretend trunk to pick up a soft toy or "drink" from a cup, just like an elephant would. Talk about why elephants need such a strong, flexible trunk.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about the matriarch who leads an elephant herd, then ask your child who leads or helps take care of your family. Draw an elephant family and label the trunk, ears, and tusks.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do elephants live?

Elephants live in jungles, savannas, and forests across Africa and Asia.

What do elephants eat?

Elephants eat leaves, grass, fruit, and tree bark — up to 300 pounds a day.

What do elephants use their trunks for?

Elephants use their trunks to drink, grab food, greet friends, and take dust baths.

Who leads an elephant herd?

Elephant herds are led by the oldest, wisest female, called the matriarch.

What activities help kids learn about elephants?

Pretending an arm is a trunk, talking about elephant families, watching a nature video, or coloring a printable worksheet.
