Cat for Kids

Cats are soft, cuddly pets that love to nap and play. They have whiskers, sharp claws, and can jump high!

Where Do Cats Live?

Cats live in homes with people, or sometimes outside as strays. They like warm, cozy spots to sleep.

What Do Cats Eat?

Cats eat meat, fish, and special cat food. They need protein to stay healthy.

Fun Facts About Cats

  • Cats purr when they’re happy or relaxed.
  • They have excellent night vision.
  • Baby cats are called kittens.
  • Cats can land on their feet when they fall!

Why Cats Are Special

Cats are quiet, clean, and love to curl up next to you. They’re great at catching mice too!

More to Discover

  • A group of cats is called a "clouder."
  • Cats sleep for up to 16 hours a day — more than almost any other pet!

New words to learn: paw, purr, whiskers, feline

Try This: Try meowing like a cat and see if a real cat (or a friend) meows back!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice cat behaviors: stretch like waking from a nap, pretend to wash their paws, and take turns meowing and purring. Talk about how cats communicate differently from dogs.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

If you have a pet cat, watch it for a few minutes and write down or draw everything it does — stretching, grooming, napping. If not, watch a short cat video together and make the same list.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do cats live?

Most pet cats live in homes with people, while some live outside as strays.

What do cats eat?

Cats eat meat, fish, and special cat food to get the protein they need.

Why do cats purr?

Cats purr when happy or relaxed, and sometimes to help calm or heal themselves.

Why do cats always land on their feet?

A flexible spine and strong sense of balance let cats twist in midair to land on their feet.

What activities help kids learn about cats?

Practicing meows and purrs, acting out stretching and napping, observing a cat, or coloring a printable worksheet.
