Cat for Kids

A cat is a small and cute pet animal. Cats have soft fur and sharp eyes. They like to sleep and play.

Where Do Cats Live?

Cats live in homes, streets, farms and villages.

What Do Cats Eat?

Cats drink milk and eat fish, meat and cat food.

Fun Facts About Cats

  • Cats can jump very high.
  • Cats love to sleep.
  • Baby cats are called kittens.

Why Cats Are Special

Cats are friendly pets and help keep homes free from rats.
