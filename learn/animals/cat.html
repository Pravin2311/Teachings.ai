Cat for Kids

Cats are soft, cuddly pets that love to nap and play. They have whiskers, sharp claws, and can jump high!

Where Do Cats Live?

Cats live in homes with people, or sometimes outside as strays. They like warm, cozy spots to sleep.

What Do Cats Eat?

Cats eat meat, fish, and special cat food. They need protein to stay healthy.

Fun Facts About Cats

  • Cats purr when they’re happy or relaxed.
  • They have excellent night vision.
  • Baby cats are called kittens.
  • Cats can land on their feet when they fall!

Why Cats Are Special

Cats are quiet, clean, and love to curl up next to you. They’re great at catching mice too!

More to Discover

  • Cats sleep for up to 16 hours a day — more than almost any other pet!
