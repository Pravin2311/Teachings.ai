Lion for Kids

Lions are big, brave cats known as the “King of the Jungle.” Male lions have fluffy manes, and they roar to scare away enemies.

Where Do Lions Live?

Lions live in Africa’s grasslands and savannas. Some also live in India.

What Do Lions Eat?

Lions eat meat — usually antelopes, zebras, and wildebeest. Lionesses do most of the hunting.

Fun Facts About Lions

  • Lions can roar so loud it can be heard 5 miles away!
  • They sleep up to 20 hours a day.
  • Baby lions are called cubs.
  • Lions live in groups called prides.

Why Lions Are Special

Lions are strong leaders and protect their families. They’re not just fierce — they’re also loving parents!

More to Discover

New words to learn: roar, mane, pride, hunt

Try This: Practice your best (and friendliest) lion roar!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children act out a lion pride: some hunt (stalking quietly), some rest (napping like lions do up to 20 hours a day), and one practices a big roar. Talk about how every member of a pride has a job to do.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Watch a short nature video of lions hunting or roaring, then ask your child to describe what they saw in their own words. Compare a lion's roar to your family cat's meow — how are they similar and different?

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do lions live?

Most lions live in Africa's grasslands and savannas, with a small population in the Gir Forest of India.

What do lions eat?

Lions are carnivores that hunt antelopes, zebras, and wildebeest. Lionesses do most of the hunting.

Why do lions live in groups?

Lions live in family groups called prides, which helps them hunt together and protect their cubs.

How loud is a lion's roar?

A lion's roar can be heard from up to 5 miles away — one of the loudest animal calls in the world.

What activities help kids learn about lions?

Practicing a lion roar, acting out pride behaviors, watching a nature video, or coloring a printable worksheet.
