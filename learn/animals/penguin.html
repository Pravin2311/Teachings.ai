Penguin for Kids

A penguin is a bird that cannot fly. Penguins swim very well.

Where Do Penguins Live?

Penguins live in cold regions.

What Do Penguins Eat?

Penguins eat fish.

Fun Facts About Penguins

  • Penguins walk funny.
  • Penguins swim fast.
  • Penguins live in groups.

Why Penguins Are Special

Penguins are expert swimmers.
