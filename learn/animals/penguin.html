Penguin for Kids

Penguins are cute, black-and-white birds that can’t fly — but they swim like champions! They live in cold places and waddle on land.

Where Do Penguins Live?

Most penguins live in Antarctica, but some live near the equator too — like in Peru and South Africa.

What Do Penguins Eat?

Penguins eat fish, squid, and krill. They dive deep into the ocean to catch their food.

Fun Facts About Penguins

  • Penguins can swim up to 15 mph!
  • They huddle together to stay warm in the cold.
  • Baby penguins are called chicks.
  • They lay eggs and take turns keeping them warm.

Why Penguins Are Special

Penguins are amazing swimmers and loving parents. They stand upright and look like little gentlemen in tuxedos!
