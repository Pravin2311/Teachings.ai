Bear for Kids

A bear is a big and strong wild animal. Bears have thick fur and sharp claws. They are known for their powerful bodies and gentle walking style.

Where Do Bears Live?

Bears live in forests, mountains, and cold regions. Some bears also live near rivers and snowy places.

What Do Bears Eat?

Bears eat fruits, honey, fish, insects, and sometimes small animals. Bears love honey very much.

Fun Facts About Bears

  • Bears can run very fast.
  • Bears can swim well.
  • Baby bears are called cubs.
  • Bears sleep for long time in winter.

Why Bears Are Special

Bears are intelligent animals. They can remember places where food is found and protect their babies carefully.
