Cow for Kids

Cows are big, gentle farm animals that give us milk. They have soft fur, big eyes, and love to eat grass.

Where Do Cows Live?

Cows live on farms in big fields. They graze on grass and rest under trees.

What Do Cows Eat?

Cows eat grass, hay, and grains. They chew their food slowly — sometimes they chew the same food twice!

Fun Facts About Cows

  • Cows give us milk, cheese, butter, and yogurt.
  • Baby cows are called calves.
  • Cows have four stomachs to help them digest grass.
  • They moo to talk to each other.

Why Cows Are Special

Cows are important for farming. They help feed people with milk and meat — and they’re very calm and kind!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Talk about where milk comes from before it reaches the store, then have children sort pictures of foods made from milk (cheese, yogurt, butter) versus foods that aren't. Practice mooing together!

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look at the milk carton or container in your fridge and talk about the journey from cow to farm to store to home. If possible, visit a local farm or petting zoo to meet a real cow.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do cows live?

Cows live on farms in big open fields, grazing on grass and resting under trees.

What do cows eat?

Cows eat grass, hay, and grains, and often chew their food a second time as cud.

Why do cows have four stomachs?

Four stomach chambers work together to break down tough grass so cows can digest it fully.

What do cows give us?

Cows give us milk, which is used to make cheese, butter, and yogurt.

What activities help kids learn about cows?

Practicing a moo, talking about where milk comes from, visiting a farm, or coloring a printable worksheet.
