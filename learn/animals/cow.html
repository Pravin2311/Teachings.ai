Cow for Kids

A cow is a gentle farm animal. Cows give us milk and help farmers.

Where Do Cows Live?

Cows live on farms and villages.

What Do Cows Eat?

Cows eat grass, hay and grains.

Fun Facts About Cows

  • Cows are very calm animals.
  • Cows give healthy milk.
  • Cows move in groups.

Why Cows Are Special

Cows help farmers and provide food for people.
