Monkey for Kids

Monkeys are clever, funny animals that love to swing from trees! They live in jungles and rainforests and eat bananas and berries.

Where Do Monkeys Live?

Monkeys live in tropical forests, jungles, and mountains in Africa, Asia, and South America.

What Do Monkeys Eat?

Monkeys eat fruits, nuts, leaves, and sometimes insects. They love bananas — but not all monkeys eat them!

Fun Facts About Monkeys

  • Monkeys use their tails to balance while swinging.
  • They live in groups called troops.
  • Baby monkeys are called infants.
  • They can use tools and solve puzzles!

Why Monkeys Are Special

Monkeys are super smart and playful. They laugh, hug, and even mimic human actions — making them fascinating friends!
