Horse for Kids

Horses are strong, beautiful animals that can run very fast. They have long manes, tails, and love to eat grass.

Where Do Horses Live?

Horses live on farms, ranches, and in stables. Some wild horses live in open plains.

What Do Horses Eat?

Horses eat grass, hay, oats, and grains. They chew their food slowly and need lots of water.

Fun Facts About Horses

  • Horses can sleep standing up!
  • They can run up to 55 mph.
  • Baby horses are called foals.
  • They communicate with neighs, whinnies, and body language.

Why Horses Are Special

Horses are loyal friends and great athletes. They’ve helped people for thousands of years — for travel, work, and fun!
