Horse for Kids

A horse is a fast and strong animal. Horses help people in travel and farming.

Where Do Horses Live?

Horses live on farms and fields.

What Do Horses Eat?

Horses eat grass, hay and grains.

Fun Facts About Horses

  • Horses can run very fast.
  • Horses can stand while sleeping.
  • Horses remember people.

Why Horses Are Special

Horses have helped humans for many years.
