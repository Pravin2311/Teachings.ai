Dolphin for Kids

Dolphins are smart, friendly sea animals that love to jump and splash! They live in oceans and swim in groups called pods.

Where Do Dolphins Live?

Dolphins live in warm oceans around the world. They swim near coasts and in deep waters.

What Do Dolphins Eat?

Dolphins eat fish, squid, and shrimp. They use echolocation to find food underwater.

Fun Facts About Dolphins

  • Dolphins can jump up to 20 feet in the air!
  • They breathe through a hole on top of their head called a blowhole.
  • Baby dolphins are called calves.
  • They are very social and play games together.

How Dolphins Talk

Dolphins "talk" to each other using clicks and whistles instead of words. Each dolphin even has its own special whistle, kind of like a name, so its pod knows who's calling!

Why Dolphins Are Special

Dolphins are super smart — they can solve puzzles, recognize themselves, and even help humans!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice "dolphin talk" using clicks and whistle sounds, then act out jumping out of the water like a dolphin at play. Talk about why dolphins live in groups called pods.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Time how long your child can hold their breath, then talk about how dolphins have to swim to the surface to breathe too, even though they live underwater. Watch a short ocean video together.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What activities help kids learn about dolphins?
