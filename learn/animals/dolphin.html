Dolphin for Kids

A dolphin is a smart sea animal. Dolphins are friendly and love to swim and jump in water.

Where Do Dolphins Live?

Dolphins live in oceans and seas.

What Do Dolphins Eat?

Dolphins eat fish and small sea animals.

Fun Facts About Dolphins

  • Dolphins are very intelligent.
  • Dolphins can jump out of water.
  • Dolphins breathe air.

Why Dolphins Are Special

Dolphins are friendly to humans and help other dolphins.
