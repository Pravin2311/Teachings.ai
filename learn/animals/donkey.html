Donkey for Kids

Donkeys are strong, gentle animals that help farmers carry heavy things. They have long ears and love to eat grass and hay.

Where Do Donkeys Live?

Donkeys live on farms, deserts, and mountains. They are often used to carry goods in villages.

What Do Donkeys Eat?

Donkeys eat grass, hay, and grains. They are not picky eaters and can survive in dry places.

Fun Facts About Donkeys

  • Donkeys have very good memories — they never forget a place or person!
  • They make a “hee-haw” sound.
  • Baby donkeys are called foals.
  • They are smarter than horses in some ways!

Why Donkeys Are Special

Donkeys are loyal workers and great at carrying things. They’re also very patient and kind!
