Donkey for Kids

Donkeys are strong, gentle animals that help farmers carry heavy things. They have long ears and love to eat grass and hay.

Where Do Donkeys Live?

Donkeys live on farms, deserts, and mountains. They are often used to carry goods in villages.

What Do Donkeys Eat?

Donkeys eat grass, hay, and grains. They are not picky eaters and can survive in dry places.

Fun Facts About Donkeys

  • Donkeys have very good memories — they never forget a place or person!
  • They make a “hee-haw” sound.
  • Baby donkeys are called foals.
  • They are smarter than horses in some ways!

Donkey Families

A baby donkey stays close to its mother for the first few months and learns to walk and run alongside her. Donkeys often make close friends and get upset if they're separated from their favorite companion!

Why Donkeys Are Special

Donkeys are loyal workers and great at carrying things. They’re also very patient and kind!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice a loud "hee-haw" sound, then act out carrying a "heavy load" (a stuffed backpack) the way a donkey helps carry goods for farmers. Talk about why donkeys are such good helpers.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about how donkeys remember friends and places for a very long time, and ask your child to name a place or person they'll always remember. Draw a donkey with its long ears and label the parts.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do donkeys live?

Donkeys live on farms, in deserts, and in mountain villages, often carrying goods for farmers.

What do donkeys eat?

Donkeys eat grass, hay, and grains, and are not picky eaters.

What sound does a donkey make?

Donkeys make a loud "hee-haw" sound to communicate with other donkeys.

Are donkeys really good at remembering things?

Yes — donkeys have excellent memories and rarely forget a place or person.

What activities help kids learn about donkeys?

Practicing a hee-haw sound, talking about carrying loads, visiting a petting zoo, or coloring a printable worksheet.
