Donkey for Kids

A donkey is a hardworking farm animal. Donkeys help people carry heavy loads.

Where Do Donkeys Live?

Donkeys live on farms and villages.

What Do Donkeys Eat?

Donkeys eat grass, hay and grains.

Fun Facts About Donkeys

  • Donkeys are very strong.
  • Donkeys are calm animals.
  • Donkeys have loud voices.

Why Donkeys Are Special

Donkeys help farmers and villagers in daily work.
