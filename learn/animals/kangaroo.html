Kangaroo for Kids

A kangaroo is a jumping animal. Kangaroos carry babies in a pouch.

Where Do Kangaroos Live?

Kangaroos live in Australia.

What Do Kangaroos Eat?

Kangaroos eat grass and plants.

Fun Facts About Kangaroos

  • Kangaroos jump very high.
  • Baby kangaroos live in pouch.
  • Kangaroos use tail for balance.

Why Kangaroos Are Special

Kangaroos move by jumping instead of walking.
