Kangaroo for Kids

Kangaroos are fun, bouncy animals that hop instead of walk! They live in Australia and carry their babies in a pouch.

Where Do Kangaroos Live?

Kangaroos live in Australia’s grasslands, forests, and deserts. They love to hop around in the sun.

What Do Kangaroos Eat?

Kangaroos eat grass, leaves, and plants. They chew their food slowly and rest during the day.

Fun Facts About Kangaroos

  • Kangaroos can hop up to 30 feet in one jump!
  • Baby kangaroos are called joeys.
  • They have a pouch to carry and nurse their babies.
  • They use their tails for balance while hopping.

Why Kangaroos Are Special

Kangaroos are unique because they’re marsupials — they carry their young in a pouch. They’re also super fast hoppers!
