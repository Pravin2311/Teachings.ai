Why Eat Vegetables?

Vegetables are nature's superpowers! They are full of vitamins, minerals, and fiber that help you grow tall, keep your tummy happy, and give you energy to play. Eating different colored vegetables gives your body different superpowers!

Rainbow of Health

  • 🟠 Orange Veggies: Good for your eyes and immune system (like Carrots & Corn).
  • 🟢 Green Veggies: Keep your bones and teeth strong (like Broccoli & Spinach).
  • 🔴 Red Veggies: Good for your heart and memory (like Tomatoes & Peppers).
  • ⚪ White Veggies: Help fight germs and keep you healthy (like Onions & Mushrooms).
  • 🟣 Purple Veggies: Help your brain think clearly (like Eggplant & Purple Cabbage).
