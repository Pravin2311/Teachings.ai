🌵 Cactus

Meet the amazing Cactus!

The Cactus is a spiky desert plant. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Cactus?

A Cactus is a spiky desert plant. You can usually find it growing in hot, dry deserts. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Cactus

  • A cactus stores water inside its thick stem so it can survive without rain for a long time.
  • Sharp spines protect the cactus from thirsty desert animals.
  • Some cactus plants can live for over 150 years!
  • Many cactus plants bloom with beautiful, colorful flowers.

🔎 Where Does the Cactus Grow?

The Cactus loves to grow in hot, dry deserts. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a desert scene with cacti of different shapes and sizes, then talk about why cacti store water instead of needing daily rain.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

If you have a small cactus at home, observe it together without touching the spines and talk about how it survives with very little water.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does a cactus survive in the desert?

It stores water inside its thick stem.

Why do cacti have spines instead of leaves?

Spines protect it from animals and lose less water than leaves.

Do cactus plants ever flower?

Yes — many bloom with colorful flowers.

How long can a cactus live?

Some live over 150 years.

What activities help kids learn about cacti?

Looking closely at spines, drawing a desert scene, talking about water storage, or coloring a printable worksheet.
