🌻 Sunflower

Meet the amazing Sunflower!

The Sunflower is a tall, bright yellow flower. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Sunflower?

A Sunflower is a tall, bright yellow flower. You can usually find it growing in sunny fields and gardens. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Sunflower

  • Young sunflowers slowly turn their heads to follow the sun across the sky!
  • A single sunflower head can be made up of over 1,000 tiny seeds.
  • Sunflowers can grow taller than a grown-up — some reach over 12 feet (3.7 m) tall!
  • Birds and people both love to snack on roasted sunflower seeds.

🔎 Where Does the Sunflower Grow?

The Sunflower loves to grow in sunny fields and gardens. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Plant sunflower seeds in small cups and have children track their growth over several weeks, measuring the stem's height each day.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Place a sunflower (real or drawn) near a window and observe how it tilts toward the light throughout the day. Count the spiral pattern of seeds in a real sunflower head if you have one.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why do sunflowers follow the sun?

Young sunflowers track the sun across the sky, called heliotropism, to soak up more light.

How tall can sunflowers grow?

Some grow taller than a grown-up, over 12 feet tall.

How many seeds does a sunflower have?

A single head can hold over 1,000 tiny seeds.

What do sunflowers need to grow?

Sunlight, water, and air, like all plants.

What activities help kids learn about sunflowers?

Planting a seed and tracking growth, counting seeds, roasting and tasting seeds, or coloring a printable worksheet.
