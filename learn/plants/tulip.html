🌷 Tulip

Meet the amazing Tulip!

The Tulip is a cup-shaped spring flower. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Tulip?

A Tulip is a cup-shaped spring flower. You can usually find it growing in gardens, especially in the Netherlands. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Tulip

  • Tulips grow from a bulb planted under the soil in the fall.
  • Tulips bloom in early spring in almost every color except true blue.
  • The Netherlands is famous for growing millions of colorful tulips every year.
  • A tulip flower closes up at night and opens again when the sun rises.

🔎 Where Does the Tulip Grow?

The Tulip loves to grow in gardens, especially in the Netherlands. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!
