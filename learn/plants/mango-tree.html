🥭 Mango Tree

Meet the amazing Mango Tree!

The Mango Tree is a large fruit tree. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Mango Tree?

A Mango Tree is a large fruit tree. You can usually find it growing in warm, tropical regions. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Mango Tree

  • A mango tree can grow very tall and live for over 100 years.
  • One mango tree can produce hundreds of juicy mangoes in a single season.
  • Mango trees have thick, shiny green leaves that stay green all year round.
  • Mangoes are sometimes called the "king of fruits" because they are so loved around the world.

🔎 Where Does the Mango Tree Grow?

The Mango Tree loves to grow in warm, tropical regions. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!
