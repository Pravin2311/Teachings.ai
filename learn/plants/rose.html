🌹 Rose

Meet the amazing Rose!

The Rose is a fragrant flowering shrub. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Rose?

A Rose is a fragrant flowering shrub. You can usually find it growing in gardens all around the world. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Rose

  • Roses come in many colors, and each color can mean something different, like red for love.
  • A rose stem often has sharp little thorns to protect it.
  • Roses have a lovely sweet smell that is used to make perfumes.
  • There are thousands of different types of roses grown around the world.

🔎 Where Does the Rose Grow?

The Rose loves to grow in gardens all around the world. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children smell a few different flowers (or flower-scented items) and talk about which smells sweetest, like roses.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look at a real or pictured rose and carefully point out the thorns, petals, and stem. Talk about why the color of a rose might change its meaning as a gift.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why do roses have thorns?

Thorns help protect the plant from animals that might eat it.

Do different rose colors mean different things?

Yes — red means love, yellow means friendship, white means purity.

Why do roses smell so sweet?

Fragrant oils in their petals are used to make perfumes.

Where do roses grow?

In gardens around the world, needing sunlight, water, and air.

What activities help kids learn about roses?

Smelling flowers, touching a stem to feel thorns, sorting colors and meanings, or coloring a printable worksheet.
