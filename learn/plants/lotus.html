🪷 Lotus

Meet the amazing Lotus!

The Lotus is a beautiful flowering water plant. Plants like this grow, breathe, and make their own food using sunlight — just like every other plant on Earth!

🌱 What is a Lotus?

A Lotus is a beautiful flowering water plant. You can usually find it growing in ponds, lakes, and quiet water. Just like all plants, it needs sunlight, water, and air to grow big and healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Lotus

  • The lotus flower grows in muddy water but stays perfectly clean and beautiful.
  • Its leaves are naturally waterproof — water droplets roll right off them!
  • The lotus is a symbol of peace and new beginnings in many cultures.
  • Lotus seeds can stay alive for hundreds of years before sprouting.

🔎 Where Does the Lotus Grow?

The Lotus loves to grow in ponds, lakes, and quiet water. Next time you see one, take a closer look at its leaves, stem, and how it reaches toward the sunlight!
