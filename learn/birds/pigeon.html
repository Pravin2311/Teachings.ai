Pigeon for Kids

Pigeons are friendly city birds that you often see in parks and squares. They coo and love to eat breadcrumbs!

Fun Facts About Pigeons

  • Pigeons can find their way home from far away — they’re called homing pigeons!
  • They were used to send messages during wars.
  • Pigeons coo to talk to each other.
  • They build nests on buildings and bridges.

What Do Pigeons Eat?

Pigeons eat seeds, bread crumbs, and sometimes insects. In cities, they love leftover food!

More to Discover

  • Pigeons are excellent navigators and have even been trained to deliver messages.
  • They can recognize themselves in a mirror — a rare skill among animals.
