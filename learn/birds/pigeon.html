Pigeon for Kids

Pigeons are friendly city birds that you often see in parks and squares. They coo and love to eat breadcrumbs!

Fun Facts About Pigeons

  • Pigeons can find their way home from far away — they’re called homing pigeons!
  • They were used to send messages during wars.
  • Pigeons coo to talk to each other.
  • They build nests on buildings and bridges.

What Do Pigeons Eat?

Pigeons eat seeds, bread crumbs, and sometimes insects. In cities, they love leftover food!

More to Discover

  • Pigeons are excellent navigators and have even been trained to deliver messages.
  • They can recognize themselves in a mirror — a rare skill among animals.

New words to learn: coo, flock, navigate, wings

Try This: Practice a soft pigeon "coo" sound and see who can copy it best!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice a soft pigeon "coo" and talk about how homing pigeons once carried messages between cities. Ask what a modern-day "message carrier" looks like today.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Go on a short walk or look out the window together to spot real pigeons and observe how they walk, peck, and fly. Talk about why pigeons are so common in cities.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do pigeons live?

Pigeons live in cities, parks, and squares, nesting on buildings and bridges.

What do pigeons eat?

Pigeons eat seeds, breadcrumbs, and sometimes insects.

Why were pigeons used to send messages?

Homing pigeons can find their way home from far away, so they carried messages, especially during wars.

Are pigeons really smart?

Yes — pigeons can recognize themselves in a mirror and remember hundreds of images.

What activities help kids learn about pigeons?

Practicing a coo, talking about homing pigeons, drawing a pigeon in a park, or coloring a printable worksheet.
