Peacock for Kids

Peacocks are famous for their beautiful, shiny tail feathers! Male peacocks spread their tails like a fan to show off.

Fun Facts About Peacocks

  • Only male peacocks have big, colorful tails — females are called peahens.
  • They can run fast and even fly short distances.
  • Peacocks make loud calls to warn others of danger.
  • They eat insects, plants, and small animals.

What Do Peacocks Eat?

Peacocks eat berries, seeds, insects, and even small snakes or lizards!

More to Discover

  • Only male peacocks grow the giant, colorful tail feathers used to attract mates.
  • A peacock's tail can have over 200 feathers.

New words to learn: feathers, display, male, colorful

Try This: Spread your arms out wide like a peacock showing off its beautiful tail!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children spread their arms wide like a peacock's tail fan and strut around the room. Talk about why only male peacocks have colorful tails while females (peahens) don't.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Cut out paper feather shapes and let your child color them blue, green, and gold to build a paper peacock tail. Count how many feathers you made and compare to the 200+ a real peacock has.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do all peacocks have colorful tails?

No — only male peacocks grow colorful tails; females (peahens) are plainer.

What do peacocks eat?

Peacocks eat berries, seeds, insects, and sometimes small snakes or lizards.

Why do peacocks spread their tails?

Males spread their tails to attract a mate and sometimes to startle predators.

How many feathers does a peacock's tail have?

A peacock's tail can have over 200 shimmering blue, green, and gold feathers.

What activities help kids learn about peacocks?

Spreading arms like a tail fan, coloring a peacock tail, comparing peacocks to peahens, or coloring a printable worksheet.
