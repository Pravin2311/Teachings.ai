Flamingo for Kids

Flamingos are beautiful pink birds with long legs and necks. They live near lakes and lagoons and love to stand on one leg!

Fun Facts About Flamingos

  • Flamingos are pink because of the food they eat (shrimp and algae)!
  • They can stand on one leg for hours to stay warm.
  • Flamingos live in big groups called flocks.
  • Baby flamingos are gray — they turn pink as they grow!

What Do Flamingos Eat?

Flamingos eat tiny shrimp, algae, and small bugs from the water. They use their beaks like filters to catch food.
