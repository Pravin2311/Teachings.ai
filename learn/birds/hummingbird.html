Hummingbird for Kids

Hummingbirds are tiny, speedy birds that can hover like helicopters! They love sipping sweet nectar from bright flowers.

Fun Facts About Hummingbirds

  • Hummingbirds flap their wings up to 80 times per second!
  • They can fly backwards and upside down.
  • They drink nectar from flowers using their long beaks.
  • They are the only birds that can hover in place.

What Do Hummingbirds Eat?

Hummingbirds drink sweet flower nectar and sometimes eat tiny insects for protein.
