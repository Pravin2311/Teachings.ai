Duck for Kids

Ducks are cute, waddling birds that love to swim! They have webbed feet and flat beaks. Many ducks live near ponds, lakes, and rivers.

Fun Facts About Ducks

  • Ducks can swim because of their webbed feet.
  • Most ducks make a “quack” sound.
  • Baby ducks are called ducklings.
  • Ducks can fly, but some are better swimmers than fliers!

What Do Ducks Eat?

Ducks eat plants, seeds, small fish, and insects. Some ducks even tip upside down in water to find food!

More to Discover

  • Duck feathers are waterproof, which helps them stay dry while swimming.
  • Ducks often fly long distances together in a V-shaped flock.

New words to learn: waddle, webbed feet, pond, flock

Try This: Practice waddling like a duck across the room!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice a duck waddle and quack, then talk about why duck feathers don't get soggy in water. Play a "duck, duck, goose" style game to reinforce the animal name.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Fill a bowl with water and drop in a feather (or cotton ball dipped lightly in oil) to show how waterproofing works. If possible, visit a local pond to watch real ducks swim and dabble for food.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do ducks live?

Ducks live near ponds, lakes, and rivers, where they swim and find food.

What do ducks eat?

Ducks eat plants, seeds, small fish, and insects.

Why don't ducks get wet when they swim?

Duck feathers are naturally waterproof, coated in oil that lets water roll right off.

Why do ducks fly in a V shape?

Flying in a V lets each duck ride the air current of the bird ahead, saving energy.

What activities help kids learn about ducks?

Practicing a waddle, quacking, talking about waterproof feathers, or coloring a printable worksheet.
