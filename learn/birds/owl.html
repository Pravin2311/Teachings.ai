Owl for Kids

Owls are mysterious night birds with big eyes and soft feathers. They hunt mice and other small animals while we sleep!

Fun Facts About Owls

  • Owls can turn their heads almost 270 degrees!
  • They fly silently so their prey doesn’t hear them.
  • They have excellent night vision.
  • Baby owls are called owlets.

What Do Owls Eat?

Owls eat mice, rats, frogs, and insects. They swallow their food whole and later cough up the bones!

More to Discover

  • Owls can turn their heads almost all the way around — about 270 degrees!
  • Most owls are nocturnal, meaning they're awake at night and sleep during the day.

New words to learn: nocturnal, hoot, talons, silent

Try This: Practice a soft owl "hoot" and slowly turn your head like an owl looking around.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children practice a soft "hoot" and slowly turn their heads as far as comfortable, talking about how an owl can turn almost all the way around. Dim the lights to talk about nocturnal animals.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about what other animals are awake at night (nocturnal) versus during the day (diurnal), and make a list together. Draw an owl with big round eyes and color it perched on a branch.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do owls live?

Owls live in forests, fields, and barns, resting during the day since most are nocturnal.

What do owls eat?

Owls eat mice, rats, frogs, and insects, swallowing food whole.

How far can an owl turn its head?

An owl can turn its head almost 270 degrees since its eyes can't move in their sockets.

Why do owls fly so quietly?

Special soft, fringed feathers muffle sound, letting owls fly silently to sneak up on prey.

What activities help kids learn about owls?

Practicing a hoot, turning the head slowly, talking about nighttime animals, or coloring a printable worksheet.
