Parrot for Kids

Parrots are bright, smart birds that can copy human words! They live in jungles and love to swing from branches.

Fun Facts About Parrots

  • Some parrots can talk and repeat words they hear!
  • They have strong beaks to crack nuts and seeds.
  • Parrots live in warm places like rainforests.
  • They use their tails to balance while climbing.

What Do Parrots Eat?

Parrots eat fruits, nuts, seeds, and sometimes flowers. Pet parrots also get special bird food.

More to Discover

  • Some parrots can live for over 50 years.
  • Parrots are very social birds and often live in flocks in the wild.
