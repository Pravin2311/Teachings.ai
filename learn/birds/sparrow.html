Sparrow for Kids

Sparrows are tiny, cheerful birds that hop around gardens and parks. They chirp happily and love to eat seeds!

Fun Facts About Sparrows

  • Sparrows are very social — they live in groups called flocks.
  • They build nests in bushes, eaves, or under roofs.
  • Sparrows chirp to talk to each other and warn of danger.
  • They are found all over the world — even in big cities!

What Do Sparrows Eat?

Sparrows eat seeds, grains, and tiny insects. They often hop on the ground to find food.

More to Discover

  • Sparrows are one of the most common birds found in cities and towns.
  • They often build nests in small, hidden spots like rooftops or bushes.

New words to learn: chirp, nest, flock, perch

Try This: Listen outside for a sparrow's chirp — how many can you count?

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children hop around the room like a sparrow searching for seeds, then chirp to "talk" to their flock. Talk about why sparrows live in groups instead of alone.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Sit quietly outside or near a window for a few minutes and count how many sparrow chirps you can hear. Scatter a few birdseed if you have a yard and watch how sparrows hop to find it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do sparrows live?

Sparrows live almost everywhere, including gardens, parks, and cities.

What do sparrows eat?

Sparrows eat seeds, grains, and tiny insects, hopping along the ground to find food.

Why do sparrows chirp?

Sparrows chirp to talk to each other, warn of danger, and stay connected with their flock.

Do sparrows live alone or in groups?

Sparrows are social and usually live in groups called flocks.

What activities help kids learn about sparrows?

Listening for chirps outside, hopping like a sparrow, drawing one on grass, or coloring a printable worksheet.
