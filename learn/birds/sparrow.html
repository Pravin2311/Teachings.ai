Sparrow for Kids

Sparrows are tiny, cheerful birds that hop around gardens and parks. They chirp happily and love to eat seeds!

Fun Facts About Sparrows

  • Sparrows are very social — they live in groups called flocks.
  • They build nests in bushes, eaves, or under roofs.
  • Sparrows chirp to talk to each other and warn of danger.
  • They are found all over the world — even in big cities!

What Do Sparrows Eat?

Sparrows eat seeds, grains, and tiny insects. They often hop on the ground to find food.
