Eagle for Kids

Eagles are strong, majestic birds that fly very high in the sky. They have sharp eyes and big wings. Eagles are hunters and catch fish or small animals.

Fun Facts About Eagles

  • Eagles can see 4 times better than humans!
  • They build huge nests called eyries.
  • The Bald Eagle is the national bird of the USA.
  • Baby eagles are called eaglets.

What Do Eagles Eat?

Eagles eat fish, rabbits, snakes, and other small animals. They use their sharp talons to grab their food.
