Eagle for Kids

Eagles are strong, majestic birds that fly very high in the sky. They have sharp eyes and big wings. Eagles are hunters and catch fish or small animals.

Fun Facts About Eagles

  • Eagles can see 4 times better than humans!
  • They build huge nests called eyries.
  • The Bald Eagle is the national bird of the USA.
  • Baby eagles are called eaglets.

What Do Eagles Eat?

Eagles eat fish, rabbits, snakes, and other small animals. They use their sharp talons to grab their food.

More to Discover

  • Eagles have incredible eyesight — about 4 times sharper than a human's.
  • Eagles build huge nests, sometimes several feet wide, high in trees or cliffs.

New words to learn: talons, soar, nest, prey

Try This: Stretch your arms out wide like an eagle's wings and pretend to soar!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children stretch their arms wide and glide around the room like a soaring eagle, then "swoop down" to grab a pretend fish. Talk about why eagles need such sharp eyesight to hunt from high in the sky.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look at a picture of the Bald Eagle together and talk about why it was chosen as a national symbol. Build a pretend "eyrie" nest out of sticks or building blocks and talk about how big real eagle nests can get.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where do eagles live?

Eagles live near lakes, rivers, and coastlines, building huge nests called eyries.

What do eagles eat?

Eagles eat fish, rabbits, snakes, and other small animals using their sharp talons.

How good is an eagle's eyesight?

An eagle can see about 4 times better than a human, spotting prey from far away.

Which eagle is the national bird of the USA?

The Bald Eagle is the national bird of the United States.

What activities help kids learn about eagles?

Pretending to soar with wide arms, talking about eagle eyesight, drawing a nest, or coloring a printable worksheet.
