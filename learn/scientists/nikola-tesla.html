⚡ Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla — a brilliant mind in electrical engineering

Nikola Tesla was a brilliant scientist known for developing alternating current (AC) electricity. Scientists like Nikola help us understand the world and make amazing new discoveries!

🔬 What Did Nikola Do?

Nikola Tesla worked in the field of electrical engineering. This great scientist is best remembered for developing alternating current (AC) electricity, which changed how people understood the world.

🌟 Fun Facts about Nikola Tesla

  • Tesla helped design the alternating current (AC) electrical system that powers homes today.
  • He built wireless devices and dreamed of sending electricity through the air.
  • Tesla could speak several languages and had an amazing memory.
  • A famous electric car company is named after him!

🌟 Why Nikola Matters

Because of scientists like Nikola Tesla, we understand our world so much better today. Their curiosity and hard work remind us that asking questions and exploring can lead to amazing discoveries!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a lightning bolt or a glowing lightbulb, then talk about how electricity travels through wires to power the things we use every day.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Trace where the electricity goes from an outlet to a lamp or toy, and talk about how Tesla's AC system helps power travel long distances.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
