🗼 Gustave Eiffel

Gustave Eiffel — a brilliant mind in engineering

Gustave Eiffel was a brilliant scientist known for designing the Eiffel Tower in Paris. Scientists like Gustave help us understand the world and make amazing new discoveries!

🔬 What Did Gustave Do?

Gustave Eiffel worked in the field of engineering. This great scientist is best remembered for designing the Eiffel Tower in Paris, which changed how people understood the world.

🌟 Fun Facts about Gustave Eiffel

  • Eiffel designed the famous Eiffel Tower for a big exhibition in Paris, France in 1889.
  • The Eiffel Tower is made of iron and was the tallest structure in the world when it was built.
  • Eiffel also helped design the metal frame inside the Statue of Liberty!
  • Millions of people visit the Eiffel Tower every year.

🌟 Why Gustave Matters

Because of scientists like Gustave Eiffel, we understand our world so much better today. Their curiosity and hard work remind us that asking questions and exploring can lead to amazing discoveries!
