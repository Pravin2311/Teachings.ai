🐢 Charles Darwin

Charles Darwin — a brilliant mind in biology and natural science

Charles Darwin was a brilliant scientist known for explaining how animals and plants change over time (evolution). Scientists like Charles help us understand the world and make amazing new discoveries!

🔬 What Did Charles Do?

Charles Darwin worked in the field of biology and natural science. This great scientist is best remembered for explaining how animals and plants change over time (evolution), which changed how people understood the world.

🌟 Fun Facts about Charles Darwin

  • Darwin sailed around the world on a ship called the HMS Beagle, studying animals and plants.
  • He studied giant tortoises and finches on the Galápagos Islands.
  • Darwin wrote a famous book called "On the Origin of Species."
  • His ideas helped scientists understand how living things adapt to their environment.

🌟 Why Charles Matters

Because of scientists like Charles Darwin, we understand our world so much better today. Their curiosity and hard work remind us that asking questions and exploring can lead to amazing discoveries!
