💡 Thomas Edison

Thomas Edison — a brilliant mind in invention and engineering

Thomas Edison was a brilliant scientist known for inventing the long-lasting light bulb and the phonograph. Scientists like Thomas help us understand the world and make amazing new discoveries!

🔬 What Did Thomas Do?

Thomas Edison worked in the field of invention and engineering. This great scientist is best remembered for inventing the long-lasting light bulb and the phonograph, which changed how people understood the world.

🌟 Fun Facts about Thomas Edison

  • Edison tried thousands of different materials before finding one that made a light bulb glow for a long time.
  • He held over 1,000 patents for his many inventions.
  • Edison also invented an early machine for recording and playing sound, called the phonograph.
  • He once said, "Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration."

🌟 Why Thomas Matters

Because of scientists like Thomas Edison, we understand our world so much better today. Their curiosity and hard work remind us that asking questions and exploring can lead to amazing discoveries!
