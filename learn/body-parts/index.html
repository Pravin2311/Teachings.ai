Body Parts and Organs

Body parts like your hands, feet and eyes are parts of your body you can see and touch. Organs, like your heart, lungs and brain, are inside your body and do special jobs, like pumping blood or helping you breathe. Learn about them on the human organs pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which body parts do toddlers usually learn first?

Many toddlers learn the parts they can point to on their own face and body first, like eyes, nose, mouth, ears, hands and feet. Songs like Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes help.

What is the difference between body parts and organs?

Body parts, like hands and feet, are on the outside where you can see them. Organs, like the heart and lungs, are inside the body and do special jobs.

How can I make learning body parts fun?

Play Simon Says ("touch your elbow!"), sing action songs, and name body parts during bath time or while getting dressed.
