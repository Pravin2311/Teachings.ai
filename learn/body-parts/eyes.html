Eyes for Kids

Your eyes help you see the world! They come in many colors — brown, blue, green, and even gray.

How Do Eyes Work?

Light enters your eyes and sends signals to your brain. That’s how you see everything — from your toys to your friends!

Fun Facts About Eyes

  • Humans blink about 15–20 times per minute.
  • Babies are born with blue eyes — color may change as they grow.
  • Some animals have eyes on the sides of their heads — but humans have them in front!
  • Your eyes can see over 10 million colors.

More to Discover

  • Your eyes blink about 15-20 times every minute without you even noticing.
  • The colored part of your eye is called the iris, and everyone's is a little different.

New words to learn: blink, iris, see, vision

Try This: Try a staring contest with a friend — who blinks first?

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children look in a small mirror and identify their own eye color, then compare with classmates to see how many different colors are in the room. Talk about how light helps us see.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Sit in a dim room for a minute, then turn on the light and talk about how your eyes adjust. Draw a self-portrait and color the eyes to match your child's real eye color.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do eyes work?

Light enters the eye and travels to the brain, which turns the signal into the pictures we see.

What is the colored part of the eye called?

The colored part is called the iris, and everyone's pattern is a little different.

Why do we blink?

Blinking spreads tears to keep the eye clean, moist, and protected.

Do babies' eye colors change?

Many babies' eye colors can change over their first year as pigment develops.

What activities help kids learn about eyes?

A staring contest, drawing a self-portrait, talking about eye colors, or coloring a printable worksheet.
