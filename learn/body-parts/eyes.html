Eyes for Kids

Your eyes help you see the world! They come in many colors — brown, blue, green, and even gray.

How Do Eyes Work?

Light enters your eyes and sends signals to your brain. That’s how you see everything — from your toys to your friends!

Fun Facts About Eyes

  • Humans blink about 15–20 times per minute.
  • Babies are born with blue eyes — color may change as they grow.
  • Some animals have eyes on the sides of their heads — but humans have them in front!
  • Your eyes can see over 10 million colors.

More to Discover

  • Your eyes blink about 15-20 times every minute without you even noticing.
  • The colored part of your eye is called the iris, and everyone's is a little different.
