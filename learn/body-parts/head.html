Head for Kids

Your head is the most important part of your body! It holds your brain, eyes, ears, nose, and mouth.

What’s Inside Your Head?

Your head has your brain (which helps you think), your eyes (to see), your ears (to hear), your nose (to smell), and your mouth (to eat and talk).

Fun Facts About Your Head

  • Your brain is like a supercomputer!
  • You can blink your eyes over 10,000 times a day.
  • Your nose can remember over 50,000 different smells.
  • Your tongue has thousands of tiny taste buds.

More to Discover

  • Your skull protects your brain, the control center for your whole body.
  • Your head holds your eyes, ears, nose, and mouth — most of your senses!
