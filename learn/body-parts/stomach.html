Stomach for Kids

Your stomach is like a food mixer! It breaks down what you eat so your body can use it for energy.

How Does Your Stomach Work?

When you eat, food goes to your stomach. There, it gets mixed with juices and turned into a mushy liquid.

Fun Facts About Stomachs

  • Your stomach can stretch to hold a lot of food — even a whole pizza!
  • Sometimes your stomach makes noises — that means it’s hungry!
  • Your stomach is protected by your ribs and muscles.
  • Even when you’re asleep, your stomach is still working!
