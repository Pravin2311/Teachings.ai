Feet for Kids

Your feet help you stand, walk, run, jump, and even dance! They’re strong and flexible.

What Can Your Feet Do?

You can tiptoe, hop, skip, kick a ball, and even climb stairs — all with your feet!

Fun Facts About Feet

  • Each foot has 5 toes — just like your hands have 5 fingers!
  • Your feet have over 250,000 sweat glands — that’s why they need clean socks!
  • Some animals have hooves, paws, or flippers instead of feet.
  • You take about 8,000 to 10,000 steps every day!
