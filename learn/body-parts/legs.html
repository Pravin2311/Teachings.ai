Legs for Kids

Your legs help you stand tall, run fast, kick a ball, and climb playground equipment!

What Are Legs Made Of?

Legs have bones, muscles, and skin. You can bend them at the knee and move them forward and backward.

Fun Facts About Legs

  • Your legs are connected to your hips — that’s where they start!
  • Some animals have four legs, some have two, and some have none!
  • You can jump high using your legs — try it!
  • Your legs carry your whole body — that’s why they’re so strong.

Let’s Learn More!

Stand up and march in place — feel your legs moving!

More to Discover

  • Your legs have some of the strongest muscles and bones in your whole body.
  • Legs help you walk, run, jump, and kick.

New words to learn: muscle, knee, kick, strong

Try This: See how high you can jump using just your leg muscles!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children march in place, then try jumping as high as they can, talking about how leg muscles power both movements. Bend and straighten a knee slowly to feel the joint at work.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about how legs carry the whole body's weight all day, then compare your child's leg length to your own using a tape measure or string. Practice standing on one leg to test balance.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are legs made of?

Legs are made of bones, muscles, and skin, bending at the knee.

Where do legs connect to the body?

Legs connect at the hips, allowing them to swing in many directions.

Why are legs so strong?

Legs carry the body's entire weight, giving them large, strong muscles and bones.

What can legs do?

Legs help us stand, walk, run, jump, kick, and climb.

What activities help kids learn about legs?

Marching in place, jumping high, bending the knee, or coloring a printable worksheet.
