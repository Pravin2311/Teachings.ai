Legs for Kids

Your legs help you stand tall, run fast, kick a ball, and climb playground equipment!

What Are Legs Made Of?

Legs have bones, muscles, and skin. You can bend them at the knee and move them forward and backward.

Fun Facts About Legs

  • Your legs are connected to your hips — that’s where they start!
  • Some animals have four legs, some have two, and some have none!
  • You can jump high using your legs — try it!
  • Your legs carry your whole body — that’s why they’re so strong.
