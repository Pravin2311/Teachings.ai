Hands for Kids

Your hands help you grab toys, hold crayons, wave hello, and give hugs!

What Can Your Hands Do?

You can clap, draw, build blocks, play piano, and even count to 10 using your fingers!

Fun Facts About Hands

  • Each hand has 5 fingers — 4 fingers and 1 thumb.
  • Your fingerprints are unique — no one else has the same pattern!
  • You use your hands to feel textures — smooth, rough, soft, bumpy.
  • Some animals have paws or claws instead of hands.

Let’s Learn More!

Trace your hand on paper and decorate it — add eyes, a smile, and arms!

More to Discover

  • Each hand has 27 bones, which is why your fingers can bend and move so easily.
  • Your fingerprints are completely unique — no one else has the same pattern.

New words to learn: fingers, grip, fingerprint, palm
