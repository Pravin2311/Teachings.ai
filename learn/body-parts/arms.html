Arms for Kids

Your arms help you do so many things — hug your friends, throw a ball, wave goodbye, and reach for snacks!

What Are Arms Made Of?

Arms have bones, muscles, and skin. You can bend them at the elbow and move them up and down.

Fun Facts About Arms

  • Your arms are connected to your shoulders — that’s where they start!
  • Some animals have wings or fins instead of arms.
  • You can lift things with your arms — even your backpack!
  • When you hug someone, you’re using your arms to show love.

Let’s Learn More!

Stretch your arms out wide — can you touch your toes? Try it!
