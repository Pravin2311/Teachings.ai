Arms for Kids

Your arms help you do so many things — hug your friends, throw a ball, wave goodbye, and reach for snacks!

What Are Arms Made Of?

Arms have bones, muscles, and skin. You can bend them at the elbow and move them up and down.

Fun Facts About Arms

  • Your arms are connected to your shoulders — that’s where they start!
  • Some animals have wings or fins instead of arms.
  • You can lift things with your arms — even your backpack!
  • When you hug someone, you’re using your arms to show love.

Let’s Learn More!

Stretch your arms out wide — can you touch your toes? Try it!

Hands Feet Legs

More to Discover

  • Your arm bends at the elbow, letting you fold it in half.
  • Arm muscles get stronger the more you use them, like when you play and lift things.

New words to learn: elbow, muscle, reach, hug

Try This: Do 5 gentle arm stretches and count out loud as you go!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children do 5 gentle arm stretches while counting out loud, then bend and straighten their elbows to feel how the joint works. Talk about all the things arms help us do every day.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Practice a big wave, a gentle hug, and a pretend throw, talking about which muscles each motion uses. Draw a picture of your child's arms and label the shoulder and elbow.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are arms made of?

Arms are made of bones, muscles, and skin, bending at the elbow.

Where do arms connect to the body?

Arms connect at the shoulders, which let them move in many directions.

What can arms do?

Arms help us lift, carry, hug, wave, throw, and reach.

How can kids make their arm muscles stronger?

Regular play, climbing, carrying, and gentle stretches build arm strength.

What activities help kids learn about arms?

Arm stretches while counting, bending the elbow, practicing a wave or hug, or coloring a printable worksheet.
