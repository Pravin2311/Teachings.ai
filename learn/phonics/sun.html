☀️ S is for Sun

Sun — say it with the "sss" sound!

The letter S makes the "sss" sound, just like at the start of the word Sun. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter S

When you see the letter S, try making the "sss" sound out loud. Say "Sun" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Sun

  • The letter S makes a hissing "sss" sound, like at the start of Sun, Snake, and Star.
  • The Sun is a giant, glowing star that gives Earth light and warmth.
  • Without the Sun, plants could not grow and nothing on Earth could survive.
  • To write a capital S, draw one curvy line, like a snake!
