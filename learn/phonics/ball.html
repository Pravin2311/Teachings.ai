⚽ B is for Ball

Ball — say it with the "buh" sound!

The letter B makes the "buh" sound, just like at the start of the word Ball. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter B

When you see the letter B, try making the "buh" sound out loud. Say "Ball" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Ball

  • The letter B makes a "buh" sound, like at the start of Ball, Bear, and Banana.
  • Balls come in many shapes and sizes — round soccer balls, oval footballs, and more!
  • To write a capital B, draw a straight line down, then two bumps on the right side.
  • Try bouncing a ball while saying "B is for Ball" out loud!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children trace the letter B in the air or on paper, then look around the room to find other things that start with B.

🏠 Homeschool Activity
