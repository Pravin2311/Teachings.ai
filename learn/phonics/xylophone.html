🎼 X is for Xylophone

Xylophone — say it with the "z" sound!

The letter X makes the "z" sound, just like at the start of the word Xylophone. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter X

When you see the letter X, try making the "z" sound out loud. Say "Xylophone" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Xylophone

  • The letter X is tricky — in Xylophone, it actually sounds like a "z"!
  • A xylophone is a musical instrument with colorful bars you hit with mallets.
  • Each bar on a xylophone makes a different musical note when tapped.
  • X is one of the rarest letters used at the start of English words.
