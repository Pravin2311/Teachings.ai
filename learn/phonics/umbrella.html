☂️ U is for Umbrella

Umbrella — say it with the "uh" sound!

The letter U makes the "uh" sound, just like at the start of the word Umbrella. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter U

When you see the letter U, try making the "uh" sound out loud. Say "Umbrella" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Umbrella

  • The letter U makes an "uh" sound, like at the start of Umbrella and Up.
  • An umbrella keeps you dry by blocking the rain with its curved top.
  • Some umbrellas can also protect you from the hot sun on a bright day.
  • U is one of the five vowels: A, E, I, O, U.
