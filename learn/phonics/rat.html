🐀 R is for Rat

Rat — say it with the "rrr" sound!

The letter R makes the "rrr" sound, just like at the start of the word Rat. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter R

When you see the letter R, try making the "rrr" sound out loud. Say "Rat" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Rat

  • The letter R makes a rolling "rrr" sound, like at the start of Rat, Rabbit, and Rainbow.
  • Rats are small, clever animals with long tails and great memories.
  • Rats can be found almost everywhere in the world.
  • To write a capital R, draw a tall line, a bump, and a small kicking leg.
