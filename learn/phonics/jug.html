🏺 J is for Jug

Jug — say it with the "juh" sound!

The letter J makes the "juh" sound, just like at the start of the word Jug. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter J

When you see the letter J, try making the "juh" sound out loud. Say "Jug" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Jug

  • The letter J makes a "juh" sound, like at the start of Jug, Jump, and Jelly.
  • A jug is a container with a handle, used for pouring liquids like water or juice.
  • The letter J is often used less often than other letters in English words.
  • To write a capital J, draw a hook shape with a small line at the top.
