📓 N is for Notebook

Notebook — say it with the "nnn" sound!

The letter N makes the "nnn" sound, just like at the start of the word Notebook. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter N

When you see the letter N, try making the "nnn" sound out loud. Say "Notebook" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Notebook

  • The letter N makes a "nnn" sound, like at the start of Notebook, Nest, and Nose.
  • A notebook is full of blank pages, perfect for writing, drawing, or doodling.
  • Writing in a notebook every day is a great way to practice your letters!
  • Some notebooks have lines to help you write neatly, while others are blank for drawing.
