🖊️ P is for Pen

Pen — say it with the "puh" sound!

The letter P makes the "puh" sound, just like at the start of the word Pen. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter P

When you see the letter P, try making the "puh" sound out loud. Say "Pen" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Pen

  • The letter P makes a "puh" sound, like at the start of Pen, Pig, and Panda.
  • A pen uses ink to write words that cannot be erased like pencil marks.
  • Long ago, people used feathers dipped in ink to write instead of pens!
  • To write a capital P, draw one tall line and a bump at the top.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter P make?

A "puh" sound, like Pen, Pig, Panda.

How is a pen different from a pencil?

Pen ink can't be erased like pencil marks.

What did people use to write before pens?

Feathers dipped in ink.

How do you write a capital P?

One tall line and a bump at the top.

What activities help kids learn the letter P?

Tracing the letter, writing with a pen, drawing a feather quill, or coloring a printable worksheet.
