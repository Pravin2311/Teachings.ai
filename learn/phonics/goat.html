🐐 G is for Goat

Goat — say it with the "guh" sound!

The letter G makes the "guh" sound, just like at the start of the word Goat. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter G

When you see the letter G, try making the "guh" sound out loud. Say "Goat" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Goat

  • The letter G makes a "guh" sound, like at the start of Goat, Gorilla, and Grapes.
  • Goats are great climbers and can even climb steep, rocky hills!
  • A baby goat is called a kid.
  • Goats love to munch on grass, leaves, and even weeds.
