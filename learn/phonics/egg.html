🥚 E is for Egg

Egg — say it with the "eh" sound!

The letter E makes the "eh" sound, just like at the start of the word Egg. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter E

When you see the letter E, try making the "eh" sound out loud. Say "Egg" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Egg

  • The letter E makes a short "eh" sound, like at the start of Egg, Elephant, and Elbow.
  • Eggs are laid by birds, like chickens, and some other animals too.
  • An egg has a hard outer shell that protects the baby growing inside.
  • E is one of the five vowels in the English alphabet: A, E, I, O, U.
