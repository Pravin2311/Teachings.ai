🐙 O is for Octopus

Octopus — say it with the "ah" sound!

The letter O makes the "ah" sound, just like at the start of the word Octopus. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter O

When you see the letter O, try making the "ah" sound out loud. Say "Octopus" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Octopus

  • The letter O makes an "ah" sound, like at the start of Octopus and Orange.
  • An octopus has eight long, wiggly arms called tentacles.
  • Octopuses are super smart and can even change color to hide from danger.
  • O is one of the five vowels: A, E, I, O, U.
