👑 Q is for Queen

Queen — say it with the "kw" sound!

The letter Q makes the "kw" sound, just like at the start of the word Queen. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter Q

When you see the letter Q, try making the "kw" sound out loud. Say "Queen" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Queen

  • The letter Q almost always teams up with the letter U, making a "kw" sound, like in Queen.
  • A queen is a woman who rules a kingdom, often wearing a sparkly crown.
  • Q is one of the least-used letters in the English alphabet.
  • In a chess game, the queen piece can move in any direction!
