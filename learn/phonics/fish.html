🐟 F is for Fish

Fish — say it with the "fff" sound!

The letter F makes the "fff" sound, just like at the start of the word Fish. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter F

When you see the letter F, try making the "fff" sound out loud. Say "Fish" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Fish

  • The letter F makes a soft "fff" sound, like at the start of Fish, Frog, and Fan.
  • Fish breathe underwater using special body parts called gills.
  • There are thousands of different kinds of fish living in oceans, rivers, and lakes.
  • To write a capital F, draw one tall line and two short lines across it.

✨ All About Fish

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a fish with fins and gills, then trace the letter F and talk about how fish use gills to breathe.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look at pictures of different kinds of fish together and talk about the many places they live, like oceans, rivers, and lakes.
