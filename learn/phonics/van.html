🚐 V is for Van

Van — say it with the "vvv" sound!

The letter V makes the "vvv" sound, just like at the start of the word Van. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter V

When you see the letter V, try making the "vvv" sound out loud. Say "Van" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Van

  • The letter V makes a "vvv" sound, like at the start of Van, Violin, and Vase.
  • A van is a boxy vehicle used to carry lots of people or cargo.
  • Delivery drivers often use vans to bring packages to your house.
  • To write a capital V, draw two slanted lines that meet at the bottom.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a delivery van carrying packages, then trace the letter V and talk about jobs that use vans.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Go for a walk or drive together and count how many vans you can spot, talking about what each one might be carrying.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter V make?

A "vvv" sound, like Van, Violin, Vase.

What is a van used for?

Carrying lots of people or cargo.

Who uses vans to deliver packages?

Delivery drivers.

How do you write a capital V?

Two slanted lines meeting at the bottom.

What activities help kids learn the letter V?

Tracing the letter, spotting vans, drawing a delivery van, or coloring a printable worksheet.
