🪁 K is for Kite

Kite — say it with the "kuh" sound!

The letter K makes the "kuh" sound, just like at the start of the word Kite. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter K

When you see the letter K, try making the "kuh" sound out loud. Say "Kite" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Kite

  • The letter K makes a "kuh" sound, like at the start of Kite, Kangaroo, and King.
  • A kite flies high in the sky when the wind catches its light frame.
  • People have been flying kites for thousands of years, starting in ancient China.
  • You need a breezy day and open space to fly a kite really high!
