🐺 W is for Wolf

Wolf — say it with the "wuh" sound!

The letter W makes the "wuh" sound, just like at the start of the word Wolf. Practicing letter sounds with fun objects like this helps you become a great reader!

🔊 The Sound of Letter W

When you see the letter W, try making the "wuh" sound out loud. Say "Wolf" slowly and listen for that sound at the very beginning of the word.

🌟 Fun Facts about Wolf

  • The letter W makes a "wuh" sound, like at the start of Wolf, Watermelon, and Window.
  • Wolves are wild animals that live and hunt together in groups called packs.
  • Wolves howl to talk to each other, sometimes from very far away!
  • To write a capital W, draw four slanted lines like two connected V shapes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What sound does the letter W make?

A "wuh" sound, like Wolf, Watermelon, Window.

How do wolves live and hunt?

Together in groups called packs.

Why do wolves howl?

To talk to each other from far away.

How do you write a capital W?

Four slanted lines like two connected V shapes.

What activities help kids learn the letter W?

Tracing the letter, practicing a howl, drawing a wolf pack, or coloring a printable worksheet.
