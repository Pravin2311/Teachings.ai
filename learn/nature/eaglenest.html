🦅 Eagle Nest

Exploring the wonder of a eagle nest!

A Eagle Nest is a large nest built by eagles high up in trees or on cliffs. Nature is full of amazing places to explore, and learning about them helps us appreciate our beautiful planet!

🌍 What is a Eagle Nest?

A eagle nest is a large nest built by eagles high up in trees or on cliffs. Places like this are found in many parts of the world, and each one has its own special plants, animals, and features.

🌟 Fun Facts about Eagle Nest

  • Eagle nests, called "eyries," can be huge — some weigh over 1,000 pounds (450 kg)!
  • Eagles build their nests high up to keep their eggs and babies safe from other animals.
  • A mother and father eagle often use and add to the same nest for many years.
  • Baby eagles, called eaglets, stay in the nest for several weeks before learning to fly.

🔎 Amazing Things About the Eagle Nest

Nature explorers love learning about the eagle nest because it shows how powerful and beautiful our planet can be. Next time you see one — in real life, a book, or a video — see how many facts you remember!
