🌳 Nature Explorer

From tall mountains to deep oceans! Click a place below to discover fun facts about our amazing planet.

⛰️ Mountain

a very tall landform that rises high above the surrounding land

🌊 Ocean

a huge body of salty water that covers most of the Earth

🏞️ River

a long, flowing stream of fresh water

🏔️ Lake

a large body of water surrounded by land

🌲 Forest

a large area covered with many trees and plants

🏜️ Desert

a dry area that gets very little rain

🌋 Volcano

a mountain with an opening that can release hot melted rock

💦 Waterfall

a place where a river drops suddenly over a steep cliff

🕳️ Cave

a hollow, natural space underground or inside a mountain

🏖️ Beach

a sandy or rocky shore next to the ocean or a lake

🐜 Anthill

a mound of dirt built by ants above their underground nest

🦅 Eagle Nest

a large nest built by eagles high up in trees or on cliffs

🌌 Aurora (Northern Lights)

a beautiful, glowing light show that dances in the night sky

🌅 Sunset

the moment the sun sinks below the horizon at the end of the day

⛺ Camp Site

an outdoor spot where people set up tents to sleep and enjoy nature

Why Explore Nature?

Our planet is full of incredible places — towering mountains, deep oceans, and glowing sunsets. Learning about nature helps children appreciate and take care of the world around them. Click on any place above to start your nature adventure!
