🌊 Ocean

Exploring the wonder of a ocean!

A Ocean is a huge body of salty water that covers most of the Earth. Nature is full of amazing places to explore, and learning about them helps us appreciate our beautiful planet!

🌍 What is a Ocean?

A ocean is a huge body of salty water that covers most of the Earth. Places like this are found in many parts of the world, and each one has its own special plants, animals, and features.

🌟 Fun Facts about Ocean

  • Oceans cover more than 70% of the entire Earth’s surface.
  • The deepest part of the ocean, the Mariana Trench, is deeper than Mount Everest is tall!
  • Oceans are home to amazing creatures like whales, dolphins, and colorful coral reefs.
  • Ocean water is salty because rivers carry tiny bits of salt from rocks into the sea.

🔎 Amazing Things About the Ocean

Nature explorers love learning about the ocean because it shows how powerful and beautiful our planet can be. Next time you see one — in real life, a book, or a video — see how many facts you remember!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a colorful coral reef full of fish, whales, and dolphins, then talk about how much of the Earth is covered by ocean.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Mix a little salt into a cup of water and let your child taste (or smell) it, then talk about why ocean water is salty.
