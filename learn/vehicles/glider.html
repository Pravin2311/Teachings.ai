🪂 Glider

Vroom! Meet the Glider.

A Glider is an engineless flying aircraft, famous for soaring silently through the sky using wind currents. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Glider?

A glider is an engineless flying aircraft. Its main job is soaring silently through the sky using wind currents. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Glider

  • A glider has no engine — it flies by riding rising currents of warm air.
  • Gliders are usually towed into the sky by another airplane, then released to fly freely.
  • Skilled glider pilots can stay airborne for hours by finding rising air.
  • Because it has no engine, a glider flight is amazingly quiet.

🔎 Where Might You See a Glider?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a glider while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
