🚢 Cargo Ship

Vroom! Meet the Cargo Ship.

A Cargo Ship is a giant ocean vehicle, famous for carrying huge containers of goods across the ocean. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Cargo Ship?

A cargo ship is a giant ocean vehicle. Its main job is carrying huge containers of goods across the ocean. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Cargo Ship

  • Cargo ships carry giant metal containers filled with everything from toys to cars.
  • Some of the biggest cargo ships can carry over 20,000 containers at once!
  • Cargo ships travel between countries, delivering goods people buy in stores.
  • It can take a cargo ship several weeks to cross a huge ocean.

🔎 Where Might You See a Cargo Ship?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a cargo ship while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
