🚌 Double-Decker Bus

Vroom! Meet the Double-Decker Bus.

A Double-Decker Bus is a land public transport vehicle, famous for carrying lots of passengers on two levels. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Double-Decker Bus?

A double-decker bus is a land public transport vehicle. Its main job is carrying lots of passengers on two levels. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Double-Decker Bus

  • A double-decker bus has two levels, so it can carry more passengers than a regular bus.
  • London, England is famous for its red double-decker buses.
  • Riding on the top level gives passengers a great view of the city.
  • Double-decker buses need extra-tall bridges and roads to drive safely underneath.

🔎 Where Might You See a Double-Decker Bus?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a double-decker bus while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
