🏍️ Dirt Bike

Vroom! Meet the Dirt Bike.

A Dirt Bike is an off-road land vehicle, famous for riding over bumpy dirt trails and jumps. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Dirt Bike?

A dirt bike is an off-road land vehicle. Its main job is riding over bumpy dirt trails and jumps. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Dirt Bike

  • Dirt bikes have extra-grippy tires designed for riding on mud, dirt, and rocks.
  • Riders wear helmets and padded gear to stay safe while riding.
  • Dirt bikes are much lighter than regular motorcycles, which helps with jumps and tricks.
  • Dirt bike racing on bumpy tracks is called motocross.

🔎 Where Might You See a Dirt Bike?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a dirt bike while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
