🗑️ Garbage Truck

Vroom! Meet the Garbage Truck.

A Garbage Truck is a land sanitation vehicle, famous for collecting trash from homes and businesses. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Garbage Truck?

A garbage truck is a land sanitation vehicle. Its main job is collecting trash from homes and businesses. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Garbage Truck

  • Garbage trucks have a powerful compactor that squishes trash to fit more in.
  • Many neighborhoods have their garbage collected on the same day every week.
  • Recycling trucks work like garbage trucks but collect paper, plastic, and glass instead.
  • Garbage trucks help keep our streets and homes clean and healthy.

🔎 Where Might You See a Garbage Truck?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a garbage truck while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
