🛳️ Cruise Ship

Vroom! Meet the Cruise Ship.

A Cruise Ship is a giant passenger ship, famous for carrying vacationers on fun ocean voyages. Vehicles like this help people and goods travel all around the world!

🚦 What is a Cruise Ship?

A cruise ship is a giant passenger ship. Its main job is carrying vacationers on fun ocean voyages. Vehicles like this are specially designed to do their job safely and efficiently.

🌟 Fun Facts about Cruise Ship

  • Cruise ships are like floating hotels, with pools, restaurants, and even theaters onboard!
  • Some cruise ships can carry over 5,000 passengers at one time.
  • Cruise ships travel to many different ports so passengers can visit new places.
  • The biggest cruise ships are longer than three football fields put together!

🔎 Where Might You See a Cruise Ship?

Keep your eyes open — you might spot a cruise ship while you're out and about! Once you learn what makes it special, you'll never look at vehicles the same way again.
