What are Vehicles?

Vehicles are machines that help us move from one place to another! Some run on gas, some on electricity, and some even use pedal power. They help us go to school, deliver food, fly to vacation spots, and explore the deep ocean.

Where Do They Go?

  • 🛣️ Land: Cars, trucks, buses, trains, and bikes travel on roads or tracks.
  • ✈️ Air: Airplanes and helicopters fly in the sky.
  • 🌊 Water: Boats, ships, and submarines travel on or under the water.
