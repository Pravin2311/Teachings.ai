🏢 The City Hall

Meet the helpers at the city hall!

The City Hall is where the mayor and city workers work to make decisions and provide services for the whole city. Community helpers like these do important jobs that keep our neighborhoods running smoothly!

👷 Who Works at the City Hall?

At the city hall, you will find the mayor and city workers. Their important job is to make decisions and provide services for the whole city, and our community depends on them every single day.

🌟 Fun Facts about City Hall

  • City hall is where the mayor and city leaders work to help run the town or city.
  • Important city decisions, like building new roads or parks, are often made at city hall.
  • People can visit city hall to get documents, ask questions, or attend community meetings.
  • Every city or town usually has its own city hall building.

🌟 Why the City Hall is Important

Without the city hall, our community would be missing something very important. It's a great idea to say thank you to community helpers whenever you see them working hard!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children draw a picture of their dream city, then talk about what decisions a mayor might make to help take care of it.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Let your child pretend to "vote" on a small family decision, like what game to play, and talk about how city hall works the same way for bigger decisions.
