✈️ The Airport

Meet the helpers at the airport!

The Airport is where pilots and airport workers work to help planes take off, land, and carry passengers safely. Community helpers like these do important jobs that keep our neighborhoods running smoothly!

👷 Who Works at the Airport?

At the airport, you will find pilots and airport workers. Their important job is to help planes take off, land, and carry passengers safely, and our community depends on them every single day.

🌟 Fun Facts about Airport

  • Airports have long runways where airplanes speed up to take off and slow down to land.
  • Air traffic controllers help guide planes safely in the sky and on the ground.
  • Airports can be extremely busy, with hundreds of planes arriving and leaving every day.
  • Before you fly, your bags are carefully checked to make sure everyone stays safe.

🌟 Why the Airport is Important

Without the airport, our community would be missing something very important. It's a great idea to say thank you to community helpers whenever you see them working hard!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Build a pretend airport out of blocks with a runway, and let children take turns being the pilot, passenger, and air traffic controller.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Pack a small pretend suitcase together, talk about what a passenger needs for a trip, and talk about why bags get checked for safety before flying.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What jobs do people do at the airport?

Pilots fly planes, workers help passengers, controllers guide planes.

Why do airports need long runways?

So planes have room to speed up and slow down safely.

What do air traffic controllers do?

Guide planes safely in the sky and on the ground.

Why are bags checked before flying?

To make sure everyone on the plane stays safe.

What activities help kids learn about the airport?

Building a pretend airport, drawing a plane taking off, packing a suitcase, or coloring a printable worksheet.
