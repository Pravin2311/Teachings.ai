🚒 The Fire Station

Meet the helpers at the fire station!

The Fire Station is where firefighters work to put out fires and rescue people in danger. Community helpers like these do important jobs that keep our neighborhoods running smoothly!

👷 Who Works at the Fire Station?

At the fire station, you will find firefighters. Their important job is to put out fires and rescue people in danger, and our community depends on them every single day.

🌟 Fun Facts about Fire Station

  • Firefighters slide down a pole to get to their big red fire truck super fast.
  • Fire trucks carry hoses, ladders, and water tanks to fight fires.
  • Firefighters wear special suits and helmets to protect them from heat and smoke.
  • Fire stations also teach kids important fire safety tips, like "stop, drop, and roll."

🌟 Why the Fire Station is Important

Without the fire station, our community would be missing something very important. It's a great idea to say thank you to community helpers whenever you see them working hard!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Practice "stop, drop, and roll" together, then have children draw a fire truck with a ladder and hose.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Talk about your family's fire safety plan, like where to meet outside if there were ever a fire, and why firefighters are important helpers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do firefighters get to the fire truck so fast?

By sliding down a pole.

What equipment do fire trucks carry?

Hoses, ladders, and water tanks.

Why do firefighters wear special suits?

To protect them from heat and smoke.

What do firefighters teach kids about fire safety?

Tips like "stop, drop, and roll."

What activities help kids learn about the fire station?

Practicing stop, drop, and roll, drawing a fire truck, making a safety plan, or coloring a printable worksheet.
