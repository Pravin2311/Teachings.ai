📮 The Post Office

Meet the helpers at the post office!

The Post Office is where mail carriers work to deliver letters and packages to homes. Community helpers like these do important jobs that keep our neighborhoods running smoothly!

👷 Who Works at the Post Office?

At the post office, you will find mail carriers. Their important job is to deliver letters and packages to homes, and our community depends on them every single day.

🌟 Fun Facts about Post Office

  • Mail carriers deliver letters and packages to houses almost every day.
  • A stamp on a letter shows that the postage, or delivery fee, has been paid.
  • The post office can send mail across the country or even around the world.
  • Before email, sending a letter through the post office was the main way people wrote to each other.

🌟 Why the Post Office is Important

Without the post office, our community would be missing something very important. It's a great idea to say thank you to community helpers whenever you see them working hard!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Have children write and decorate a pretend letter to a friend or family member, then draw a stamp in the corner to "mail" it.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Write a real letter or card together and take a trip to mail it, talking about how it will travel to reach the person you're sending it to.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What do mail carriers do?

Deliver letters and packages almost every day.

What does a stamp on a letter mean?

That the delivery fee has been paid.

How far can the post office send mail?

Across the country or around the world.

How did people send messages before email?

Through letters mailed at the post office.

What activities help kids learn about the post office?

Writing a pretend letter, drawing a stamp, pretending to be a mail carrier, or coloring a printable worksheet.
