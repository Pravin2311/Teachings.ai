🌳 The Park

Meet the helpers at the park!

The Park is where park rangers and city workers work to take care of green spaces where people play and relax. Community helpers like these do important jobs that keep our neighborhoods running smoothly!

👷 Who Works at the Park?

At the park, you will find park rangers and city workers. Their important job is to take care of green spaces where people play and relax, and our community depends on them every single day.

🌟 Fun Facts about Park

  • Parks give people a safe, green place to play, run, and enjoy fresh air.
  • Many parks have playgrounds, walking trails, and picnic areas.
  • Parks are home to trees, flowers, birds, and small animals like squirrels.
  • Taking care of a park, like not littering, helps keep it beautiful for everyone.

🌟 Why the Park is Important

Without the park, our community would be missing something very important. It's a great idea to say thank you to community helpers whenever you see them working hard!
