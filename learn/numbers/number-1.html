1️⃣ Number 1: One

The number 1, written as "One"

The number 1 is written as "One." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about One.

🔢 Counting to One

When you count out loud, you say the number 1 as "One." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 1 — like counting one bright star in the sky!

🌟 Fun Facts about 1 (One)

  • One is the very first counting number.
  • You have one nose and one belly button!
  • Anything multiplied by one stays exactly the same.
  • One is the loneliest number — but also a great place to start counting!

✨ One in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 1 in many places, such as one bright star in the sky. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!
