0️⃣ Number 0: Zero

The number 0, written as "Zero"

The number 0 is written as "Zero." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Zero.

🔢 Counting to Zero

When you count out loud, you say the number 0 as "Zero." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 0 — like counting zero cookies left in the jar!

🌟 Fun Facts about 0 (Zero)

  • Zero means "nothing" or "none" — like an empty plate with no cookies on it!
  • Zero was invented as a number a very long time ago in ancient India.
  • Zero is neither a positive nor a negative number.
  • If you have 0 apples, that means you have no apples at all.

✨ Zero in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 0 in many places, such as zero cookies left in the jar. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!

✍️ Tracing & Writing Practice

To write 0, draw one continuous oval shape, just like the letter O. Our free worksheets library has printable number tracing and counting sheets you can print and reuse at home or in the classroom.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Show an empty basket or bowl and ask children how many items are inside — the answer is zero! Then fill it with a few toys and empty it again, counting down to zero each time to make the concept concrete.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

At snack time, eat crackers one at a time while counting down out loud — "three, two, one, zero!" — until the plate is empty. This turns an abstract idea into something your child can see and experience directly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I explain the number 0 to my child?

Use an empty container: eat crackers one at a time while counting down, landing on "zero" when the bowl is empty. Zero means "none left," a concrete idea kids can see and feel.

How do I teach my child to write the number 0?

Zero is one oval or circle shape, similar to the letter O. Tracing large 0s with a finger before using a pencil helps kids get comfortable with the curve.

Why is zero an important number?

Zero represents "nothing" or "none," and works as a placeholder that makes numbers like 10, 20, and 100 possible. It was formally invented in ancient India, later than counting numbers like 1, 2, and 3.

Is zero a positive or negative number?

Zero is neither positive nor negative — it sits exactly in the middle, a more advanced idea best introduced once a child is comfortable with 0 through 10.

What's a good age to start learning the number 0?

Zero is often taught slightly after 1-5, since "nothing" is more abstract than counting real objects. Most children grasp it comfortably by age 4-5.
