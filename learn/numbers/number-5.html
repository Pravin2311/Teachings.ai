Number 5 (Five) for Kids: Counting, Writing and Fun Facts

5 is written in words as five. It means a group of 5 things, like five fingers on each hand and five toes on each foot. In counting, 5 comes after 4 (four) and before 6 (six). 5 is an odd number.

Counting to 5

Count out loud and touch one object for each number you say: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. The last number you say, 5, tells you how many there are altogether.

How to Write the Number 5

  1. Start at the top. Draw a short straight line down.
  2. Curve around to make a round belly, ending at the bottom left.
  3. Lift your pencil and draw a short flag across the top, going right.

The word is spelled F-I-V-E. Practice tracing 5 in the tracing game or on the Number Writing Practice worksheet.

Ways to Make 5

Two smaller numbers can be put together to make 5. These are called number bonds:

  • 5 + 0 = 5
  • 4 + 1 = 5
  • 3 + 2 = 5

Try it with real objects: put 5 blocks in a row and split them into two groups in different ways.

Five in Real Life

  • Five fingers on each hand and five toes on each foot
  • Most starfish have five arms
  • A pentagon has five sides

Fun Facts About 5

  • Five is exactly half of ten, and two fives make ten.
  • Five is an odd number.
  • Tally marks are grouped in fives: four lines with a fifth line across them.

Quick Quiz: Number 5

Answer each question, then tap it to check.

What number comes right after 5?

6 (six).

Is 5 odd or even?

Odd. If you split 5 into pairs, one is left over.

If you have 5, how many more do you need to make 10?

5, because 5 + 5 = 10.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Give each child five small counters (buttons, blocks, or beans) and have them arrange and rearrange the group while counting aloud each time — in a line, in a circle, in a pile. Rearranging the same five objects helps kids understand that the count stays the same no matter how they're grouped.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

At snack time, count out exactly five crackers or grapes together before eating. Repeating this at every snack builds real-world number sense far faster than worksheets alone.
