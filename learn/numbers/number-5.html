5️⃣ Number 5: Five

The number 5, written as "Five"

The number 5 is written as "Five." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Five.

🔢 Counting to Five

When you count out loud, you say the number 5 as "Five." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 5 — like counting five fingers on your hand!

🌟 Fun Facts about 5 (Five)

  • You have five fingers on each hand and five toes on each foot!
  • A starfish usually has five arms.
  • High five! Give someone a high five using all five fingers.
  • Five is exactly half of ten.

✨ Five in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 5 in many places, such as five fingers on your hand. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!
