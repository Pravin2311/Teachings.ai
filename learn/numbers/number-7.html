Number 7 (Seven) for Kids: Counting, Writing and Fun Facts

7 is written in words as seven. It means a group of 7 things, like seven days in a week. In counting, 7 comes after 6 (six) and before 8 (eight). 7 is an odd number.

Counting to 7

Count out loud and touch one object for each number you say: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. The last number you say, 7, tells you how many there are altogether.

How to Write the Number 7

  1. Draw a straight line across the top, going right.
  2. Draw a slanted line down to the bottom left.
  3. Some people add a short line across the middle so 7 does not look like 1.

The word is spelled S-E-V-E-N. Practice tracing 7 in the tracing game or on the Number Writing Practice worksheet.

Ways to Make 7

Two smaller numbers can be put together to make 7. These are called number bonds:

  • 7 + 0 = 7
  • 6 + 1 = 7
  • 5 + 2 = 7
  • 4 + 3 = 7

Try it with real objects: put 7 blocks in a row and split them into two groups in different ways.

Seven in Real Life

  • Seven days in a week
  • Seven colors usually listed in a rainbow: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet
  • Seven continents on Earth

Fun Facts About 7

  • There are seven days in a week: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
  • Seven is an odd number.
  • Earth's land is usually divided into seven continents.

Quick Quiz: Number 7

Answer each question, then tap it to check.

What number comes right after 7?

8 (eight).

Is 7 odd or even?

Odd. If you split 7 into pairs, one is left over.

If you have 7, how many more do you need to make 10?

3, because 7 + 3 = 10.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Make a class calendar and name each of the seven days together every morning. Then use a prism or a rainbow picture to count all seven colors, saying each one aloud.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Count down the days of the week together on a calendar each morning, reinforcing "seven days make one week." It's a natural daily routine that builds the number without a separate activity.
