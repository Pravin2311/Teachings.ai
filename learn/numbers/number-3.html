3️⃣ Number 3: Three

The number 3, written as "Three"

The number 3 is written as "Three." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Three.

🔢 Counting to Three

When you count out loud, you say the number 3 as "Three." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 3 — like counting three wheels on a tricycle!

🌟 Fun Facts about 3 (Three)

  • A triangle has three sides and three corners.
  • Three little pigs built three different houses in the famous story!
  • Three wheels make a tricycle roll.
  • You can count to three super fast: 1, 2, 3!

✨ Three in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 3 in many places, such as three wheels on a tricycle. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!
