8️⃣ Number 8: Eight

The number 8, written as "Eight"

The number 8 is written as "Eight." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Eight.

🔢 Counting to Eight

When you count out loud, you say the number 8 as "Eight." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 8 — like counting eight arms on an octopus!

🌟 Fun Facts about 8 (Eight)

  • An octopus has eight long, wiggly arms!
  • A spider has eight legs, which makes it different from an insect.
  • A stop sign has eight sides — that shape is called an octagon.
  • Eight is double of four.

✨ Eight in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 8 in many places, such as eight arms on an octopus. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!

✍️ Tracing & Writing Practice

To write 8, draw two loops stacked on top of each other, like a snowman, in one continuous motion. Our free worksheets library has printable number tracing and counting sheets you can print and reuse at home or in the classroom.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Look at a picture of an octopus and count all eight arms together, or count a toy spider's eight legs. Then find a stop sign picture and trace its eight sides, connecting the number to a real octagon shape.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Build a tower of eight blocks together, or count out eight snacks before eating them. Repeating "eight" across a few different objects in one sitting helps it stick.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I teach my child to count to 8?

Count the eight legs on a toy spider or octopus together, or count out eight small objects like blocks or crackers.

How do I teach my child to write the number 8?

Practice two loops stacked on top of each other, like a snowman shape, or one continuous figure-eight motion.

What are good counting activities for the number 8?

Counting the legs on a toy spider or octopus, tracing a stop sign's eight sides, or building an eight-block tower.

How does the number 8 connect to shapes?

8 connects to the octagon — an eight-sided shape best known from stop signs.

What's a good age to start learning the number 8?

Most children learn to count to 8 around age 4-5, with confident numeral writing often developing closer to kindergarten.
