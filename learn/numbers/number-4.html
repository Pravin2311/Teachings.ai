Number 4 (Four) for Kids: Counting, Writing and Fun Facts

4 is written in words as four. It means a group of 4 things, like four legs on a dog, cat or chair. In counting, 4 comes after 3 (three) and before 5 (five). 4 is an even number.

Counting to 4

Count out loud and touch one object for each number you say: 1, 2, 3, 4. The last number you say, 4, tells you how many there are altogether.

How to Write the Number 4

  1. Start at the top. Draw a straight line down to the middle.
  2. Draw a line across to the right.
  3. Lift your pencil. Start at the top on the right and draw a long straight line down, crossing the line you just made.

The word is spelled F-O-U-R. Practice tracing 4 in the tracing game or on the Number Writing Practice worksheet.

Ways to Make 4

Two smaller numbers can be put together to make 4. These are called number bonds:

  • 4 + 0 = 4
  • 3 + 1 = 4
  • 2 + 2 = 4

Try it with real objects: put 4 blocks in a row and split them into two groups in different ways.

Four in Real Life

  • Four legs on a dog, cat or chair
  • Four wheels on a car
  • Four seasons: spring, summer, autumn and winter

Fun Facts About 4

  • A square and a rectangle both have 4 sides and 4 corners.
  • Four is an even number: 2 + 2 = 4.
  • Many animals walk on four legs.

Quick Quiz: Number 4

Answer each question, then tap it to check.

What number comes right after 4?

5 (five).

Is 4 odd or even?

Even. 4 can be split into two equal groups of 2.

If you have 4, how many more do you need to make 10?

6, because 4 + 6 = 10.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Count the legs on classroom chairs or tables together, touching each one while counting "one, two, three, four!" Then draw a square on the board and count its four sides and four corners as a class.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look for four-legged pets or animals in picture books together, counting each leg out loud. If you have a pet at home, counting its legs in person makes it even more memorable.
