4️⃣ Number 4: Four

The number 4, written as "Four"

The number 4 is written as "Four." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Four.

🔢 Counting to Four

When you count out loud, you say the number 4 as "Four." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 4 — like counting four legs on a dog!

🌟 Fun Facts about 4 (Four)

  • Most cars and tables stand steady on four wheels or four legs.
  • A square has four equal sides.
  • Four seasons make up one whole year: spring, summer, fall, and winter.
  • A four-leaf clover is considered extra lucky to find!

✨ Four in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 4 in many places, such as four legs on a dog. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!

✍️ Tracing & Writing Practice

To write 4, draw a diagonal line down, a straight line across, then a vertical line down through both — one of the trickier early numerals since the lines cross. Our free worksheets library has printable number tracing and counting sheets you can print and reuse at home or in the classroom.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Count the legs on classroom chairs or tables together, touching each one while counting "one, two, three, four!" Then draw a square on the board and count its four sides and four corners as a class.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Look for four-legged pets or animals in picture books together, counting each leg out loud. If you have a pet at home, counting its legs in person makes it even more memorable.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I teach my child to count to 4?

Count familiar groups of four together — four legs on a dog or table, four sides of a square, or four crayons in a hand.

How do I teach my child to write the number 4?

Practice a diagonal line down, a straight line across, and then a vertical line down through both — three strokes total.

What are good counting activities for the number 4?

Counting the legs of a table or chair, building a four-block tower, or finding the four sides of a square.

How does the number 4 connect to shapes?

4 connects directly to squares and rectangles, which both have exactly four sides and four corners.

What's a good age to start learning the number 4?

Most children can count to 4 by age 3, though writing the numeral clearly often takes more practice and may not be solid until closer to kindergarten.
