The number 10, written as "Ten"

The number 10 is written as "Ten." Numbers help us count everything around us — toys, snacks, friends, and more! Let's learn all about Ten. This page includes a free printable Number 10 worksheet with counting practice and activities kids can use at home or in the classroom.

🔢 Counting to Ten

When you count out loud, you say the number 10 as "Ten." Try counting objects around your room until you reach 10 — like counting ten fingers on your hands!

🌟 Fun Facts about 10 (Ten)

  • You have ten fingers and ten toes in total!
  • Ten is a whole "group of ten" — the basis of how we count bigger numbers.
  • A perfect score in gymnastics used to be a 10!
  • Counting from 1 to 10 is one of the first math skills kids learn.

✨ Ten in Everyday Life

You can spot the number 10 in many places, such as ten fingers on your hands. Numbers are everywhere once you start looking for them!

✍️ Tracing & Writing Practice

To write 10, draw a straight line for the 1, then a circle for the 0 right next to it — a child's very first two-digit number. Our free worksheets library has printable number tracing and counting sheets you can print and reuse at home or in the classroom.

🏫 Classroom Activity

Count fingers together as a class, holding up both hands and counting each one aloud to ten. Then celebrate reaching ten — it's a genuine milestone worth marking with a cheer or a small sticker.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Count steps together on a staircase, or count out ten pieces of cereal before a snack. Practicing the full 1-to-10 sequence in different everyday contexts builds real, flexible counting skill.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I teach my child to count to 10?

Counting fingers is the classic approach — ten fingers give every child a built-in counting tool they carry everywhere.

How do I teach my child to write the number 10?

10 is two numerals together: a straight line for the 1, then a circle for the 0 — a child's first two-digit number.

What are good counting activities for the number 10?

Counting fingers and toes, counting steps on a staircase, or building a ten-block tower.

Why is 10 such an important number?

10 is the basis of our whole number system — once kids know 1 through 10, counting 11, 12, and beyond largely repeats that same pattern.

What's a good age to start learning the number 10?

Most children can count to 10 by age 4-5, often one of the clearest markers of kindergarten readiness in early math.
