🛡️ Your Lymph Nodes

Your amazing lymph nodes at work!

Your Lymph Nodes help your body by helping your body fight off germs. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the lymph nodes has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Lymph Nodes Work?

Your lymph nodes are always busy helping your body fight off germs. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Lymph Nodes

  • Lymph nodes are tiny, bean-shaped helpers found throughout your body.
  • They act like little filters that trap germs so your body can fight them off.
  • You might feel small lumps in your neck get bigger when you are fighting a cold — that’s your lymph nodes working hard!
  • Lymph nodes are an important part of your body’s defense team, called the immune system.

💚 Taking Care of Your Lymph Nodes

You can help keep your lymph nodes healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
