🫁 Your Lungs

Your amazing lungs at work!

Your Lungs helps your body by breathing in fresh air and breathing out used air. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the lungs has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Lungs Work?

Your lungs are always busy breathing in fresh air and breathing out used air. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Lungs

  • You have two lungs that fill up with air like balloons every time you breathe in.
  • Your lungs take in oxygen from the air, which your blood carries to the rest of your body.
  • When you run and play, you breathe faster because your lungs need to bring in more oxygen.
  • Singing and blowing bubbles are fun ways to practice using your lungs!

💚 Taking Care of Your Lungs

You can help keep your lungs healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
