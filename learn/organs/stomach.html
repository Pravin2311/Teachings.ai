🍽️ Your Stomach

Your amazing stomach at work!

Your Stomach helps your body by breaking down the food you eat. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the stomach has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Stomach Work?

Your stomach are always busy breaking down the food you eat. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Stomach

  • Your stomach mixes and mashes up food using special juices to help digest it.
  • A grumbling stomach sound often just means it is squeezing air and food around, and can mean you are hungry.
  • It takes several hours for your stomach to fully digest a meal.
  • Chewing your food well helps your stomach do its job more easily.

💚 Taking Care of Your Stomach

You can help keep your stomach healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Practice chewing food slowly and counting each bite, then talk about how chewing well helps the stomach do its job more easily.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Listen for a grumbling tummy before mealtime and talk about what that sound means and how the stomach digests food afterward.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the stomach break down food?

By mixing and mashing it with special juices.

Why does your stomach make grumbling sounds?

It's squeezing air and food, or you're hungry.

How long does it take to digest a meal?

Several hours.

Why is chewing your food well important?

It helps your stomach do its job more easily.

What activities help kids learn about the stomach?

Chewing slowly and counting bites, listening for a grumbling tummy, drawing a meal, or coloring a printable worksheet.
