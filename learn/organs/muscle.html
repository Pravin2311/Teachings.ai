💪 Your Muscles

Your amazing muscles at work!

Your Muscles help your body by helping your body move, run, and jump. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the muscles has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Muscles Work?

Your muscles are always busy helping your body move, run, and jump. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Muscles

  • Muscles work by squeezing (contracting) and relaxing to move your bones.
  • You have hundreds of muscles all over your body, even in your face for smiling!
  • Your heart is actually a powerful muscle that never stops working.
  • Exercise and healthy food help your muscles grow strong.

💚 Taking Care of Your Muscles

You can help keep your muscles healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
