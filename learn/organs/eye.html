👀 Your Eyes

Your amazing eyes at work!

Your Eyes helps your body by seeing the colors, shapes, and people around you. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the eyes has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Eyes Work?

Your eyes are always busy seeing the colors, shapes, and people around you. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Eyes

  • Your eyes work like tiny cameras, sending pictures to your brain.
  • You blink about 15-20 times every minute without even thinking about it.
  • Eyes need light to see — that’s why it’s hard to see in a dark room.
  • Your eyes can tell the difference between millions of different colors!

💚 Taking Care of Your Eyes

You can help keep your eyes healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
