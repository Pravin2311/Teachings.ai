🦷 Your Teeth

Your amazing teeth at work!

Your Teeth help your body by biting and chewing your food. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the teeth has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Teeth Work?

Your teeth are always busy biting and chewing your food. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Teeth

  • Adults usually have 32 teeth, while kids start with 20 baby teeth.
  • The outer layer of your teeth, called enamel, is the hardest material in your whole body.
  • Brushing twice a day helps keep your teeth strong and free from cavities.
  • Different teeth have different jobs — some cut, some tear, and some grind food.

💚 Taking Care of Your Teeth

You can help keep your teeth healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!

🏫 Classroom Activity

Practice good brushing technique with a toy toothbrush, and sort pictures of foods by whether they'd need cutting, tearing, or grinding teeth.

🏠 Homeschool Activity

Use your tongue to count how many teeth you can feel, and talk about why brushing twice a day keeps them strong and healthy.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many teeth do adults and kids have?

Adults 32, kids start with 20 baby teeth.

What is enamel?

The hardest material in your body, covering your teeth.

How often should you brush your teeth?

Twice a day.

Do all teeth do the same job?

No — some cut, some tear, some grind.

What activities help kids learn about teeth?

Practicing brushing, counting teeth with your tongue, sorting foods by teeth type, or coloring a printable worksheet.
