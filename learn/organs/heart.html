❤️ Your Heart

Your amazing heart at work!

Your Heart helps your body by pumping blood all around your body. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the heart has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Heart Work?

Your heart are always busy pumping blood all around your body. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Heart

  • Your heart beats about 100,000 times every single day!
  • The heart pumps blood that carries oxygen to every part of your body.
  • Exercise, like running and jumping, makes your heart strong.
  • You can feel your heartbeat by placing your hand gently on your chest.

💚 Taking Care of Your Heart

You can help keep your heart healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
