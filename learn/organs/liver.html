🫘 Your Liver

Your amazing liver at work!

Your Liver helps your body by cleaning your blood and helping digest food. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the liver has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Liver Work?

Your liver are always busy cleaning your blood and helping digest food. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Liver

  • The liver is one of the biggest organs inside your body.
  • It works like a filter, cleaning out things your body does not need.
  • The liver helps store energy from the food you eat so you have power to play.
  • Amazingly, the liver can heal and regrow itself if it gets a little bit hurt.

💚 Taking Care of Your Liver

You can help keep your liver healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
