🫀 Human Body for Kids

Discover the amazing organs inside your body! Click one below to learn its job and fun facts.

🧠 Brain

thinking, remembering, and controlling your whole body

👀 Eyes

seeing the colors, shapes, and people around you

❤️ Heart

pumping blood all around your body

🫁 Lungs

breathing in fresh air and breathing out used air

🍽️ Stomach

breaking down the food you eat

🫘 Liver

cleaning your blood and helping digest food

🫘 Kidneys

filtering your blood and making urine

🌀 Intestines

absorbing nutrients from food and moving waste along

💪 Muscles

helping your body move, run, and jump

🦷 Teeth

biting and chewing your food

🛡️ Lymph Nodes

helping your body fight off germs

Why Learn About Your Body?

Your body is an incredible machine made of many parts working together as a team. Understanding how your brain, heart, lungs, and other organs work helps you take better care of yourself. Click on any organ above to explore how it keeps you healthy and strong!
