🌀 Your Intestines

Your amazing intestines at work!

Your Intestines help your body by absorbing nutrients from food and moving waste along. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the intestines has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Intestines Work?

Your intestines are always busy absorbing nutrients from food and moving waste along. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Intestines

  • Your intestines are actually one very long, coiled tube inside your belly.
  • If you stretched out your small intestine, it would be several times longer than your whole body!
  • Intestines soak up vitamins and nutrients from the food you eat.
  • Eating fruits and vegetables helps keep your intestines healthy.

💚 Taking Care of Your Intestines

You can help keep your intestines healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What shape are your intestines?

One very long, coiled tube.

How long is the small intestine?

Several times longer than your whole body.

What do intestines do with the food you eat?

Soak up vitamins and nutrients.

What foods help keep your intestines healthy?

Fruits and vegetables.

What activities help kids learn about intestines?

Coiling a long string, drawing food's journey through your body, tasting fruits and vegetables, or coloring a printable worksheet.
