🫘 Your Kidneys

Your amazing kidneys at work!

Your Kidneys help your body by filtering your blood and making urine. Every part of your body works together like a team, and the kidneys has a very important job!

🔬 How Do Your Kidneys Work?

Your kidneys are always busy filtering your blood and making urine. Even when you are resting or asleep, this amazing part of your body keeps doing its job to keep you healthy.

🌟 Fun Facts about Kidneys

  • You have two kidneys, shaped like beans, on either side of your body.
  • Kidneys filter your blood to remove waste your body does not need.
  • Drinking enough water every day helps your kidneys work well.
  • Your kidneys filter your entire body’s blood many times a day.

💚 Taking Care of Your Kidneys

You can help keep your kidneys healthy by eating good food, drinking water, exercising, and getting plenty of sleep. A healthy body helps you play, learn, and grow!
