🎮 How to Play: Word Matching

  1. Look at the picture or word shown.
  2. Tap the matching word or picture.
  3. The game switches between picture-to-word and word-to-picture, so stay alert!

What your child practices

Switching between matching pictures to words and words to pictures makes children read more carefully. It builds vocabulary with animals, birds and fruits, and strengthens early word recognition.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask your child to read the word aloud before choosing.
  • Talk about each animal or fruit after matching it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What words are included?

Words include animals, birds and fruits such as lion, elephant, peacock, parrot, apple, orange and strawberry.

How is this different from Word Match?

Word Match uses drag and drop with six words. This game uses tapping and switches between two kinds of matching.

What age is this for?

It suits children from about 4 to 7.
