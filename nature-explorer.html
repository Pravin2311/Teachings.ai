🌿 Parent Tip: Connect to Nature Every Day

After playing, explore nature together: go for a walk, plant seeds, or observe insects. Ask your child: “What do trees need to grow?” or “Can you hear the birds?” This builds curiosity, science skills, and a love for the natural world in preschool and kindergarten kids.

🎮 How to Play: Nature Scratch Card

  1. Scratch away the grey card to reveal a place in nature.
  2. Listen to its name, such as cave, volcano or waterfall.
  3. Tap Next to explore another natural wonder.

What your child practices

Children learn the names of natural places and features, like deserts, lakes, mountains and auroras, building the vocabulary they need for science and geography. Each picture is a chance to talk about weather, animals and plants that live there.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask "Would you like to visit this place? What would you need to bring?"
  • Compare two cards: how is a desert different from a forest?

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What nature places are in the game?

It includes an anthill, aurora, beach, camp site, cave, desert, eagle nest, forest, lake, mountain, ocean, river, sunset, volcano and waterfall.

What age is it for?

It suits children from about 3 to 8, and older children can use the learning pages to find out more.

How can we explore nature for real?

Go on a short nature walk and look for the same features: a hill, a stream, an anthill or a beautiful sunset.
