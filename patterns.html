Patterns & Sequences

🧠 Tip: Look closely at the repeating emojis or shapes. Can you spot the pattern?

🧠 Parent Tip: Build Logical Thinking Through Patterns

Encourage your child to find patterns in daily life: tiles, clothing stripes, or nature (like leaf arrangements). Ask, “What comes next?” This strengthens logical reasoning, prediction skills, and early math thinking — perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Patterns & Sequences

  1. Look at the row of emojis or shapes.
  2. Work out what repeats, and tap the item that comes next.
  3. Tap Next Pattern for a new one.

What your child practices

Spotting patterns is a building block of math and logical thinking. Children learn to look for what repeats, like red, blue, red, blue, and use that to predict what comes next.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Say the pattern out loud: "apple, banana, apple, banana..."
  • Make patterns with real objects, like blocks or claps and stomps.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why are patterns important for math?

Patterns help children predict and reason, skills they later use for counting by twos, times tables and algebra.

What age is this pattern game for?

It suits children from about 3 to 6.

How can we practice patterns away from the screen?

Make bead necklaces with repeating colors, or clap and stomp a rhythm for your child to copy.
