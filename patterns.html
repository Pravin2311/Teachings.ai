🧠 Parent Tip: Build Logical Thinking Through Patterns

Encourage your child to find patterns in daily life: tiles, clothing stripes, or nature (like leaf arrangements). Ask, “What comes next?” This strengthens logical reasoning, prediction skills, and early math thinking — perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.
