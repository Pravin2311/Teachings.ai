Parenting Tips

  • Encourage your child to say the number out loud when the audio plays.
  • Let your child try multiple times; mistakes are part of learning.
  • Use counting objects at home (fruits, toys) to reinforce numbers 1–10.
  • Praise effort, not just correct answers, to boost confidence.
  • Play the game in short sessions (5–10 minutes) to maintain attention.
  • Ask questions: “Can you find the number 3?” before the audio plays to engage memory.
