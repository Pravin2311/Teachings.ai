🎨 Parent Tip: Strengthen Color & Categorization Skills

After playing, extend the fun with real-life sorting! Use colored toys, blocks, or clothes and ask your child to group them by color. This hands-on practice builds color recognition, fine motor skills, and early math concepts like sorting and categorizing — perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.
