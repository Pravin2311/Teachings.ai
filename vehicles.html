🚗 Learn Vehicles

Turn Daily Rides Into Learning Moments

After playing, point out vehicles during walks or car rides: “That’s a fire truck!” or “Hear the ambulance siren?” This builds vocabulary, listening skills, and real-world awareness—perfect for curious preschool and kindergarten learners.
