Learn About Scientists

🔬 Parent Tip: Inspire Curiosity & Wonder

After playing, talk about how scientists explore the world: “How do we know about stars?” or “Why do plants grow?” This builds scientific thinking, vocabulary, and a love for discovery—perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.
