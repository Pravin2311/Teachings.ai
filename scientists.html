🔬 Parent Tip: Inspire Curiosity & Wonder

After playing, talk about how scientists explore the world: “How do we know about stars?” or “Why do plants grow?” This builds scientific thinking, vocabulary, and a love for discovery—perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Scientists Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the card to reveal a famous scientist or inventor.
  2. Listen to their name.
  3. Tap Next and talk about what each person discovered or invented.

What your child practices

Children meet real people whose curiosity changed the world, such as Isaac Newton, Charles Darwin and Srinivasa Ramanujan. Linking a face to a discovery shows that science is done by people who ask questions and keep trying.

Tips for grown-ups

  • After each card, ask "What question do you think they were trying to answer?"
  • Try a simple experiment linked to one scientist, such as dropping objects to explore gravity.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which scientists are included?

The game includes Antoine Lavoisier, Charles Darwin, Gustave Eiffel, Isaac Newton, Leonardo da Vinci, Nikola Tesla, Robert Oppenheimer, Srinivasa Ramanujan, Thomas Edison and Alexander Graham Bell.

What age is this for?

It suits children from about 5 to 10, especially those who love asking how and why.

Where can we learn each scientist's story?

Each scientist has a learning page about their life, their discoveries and why their work still matters.
