👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

Unlike our single-skill worksheets (tracing, flashcards, letter sounds), this one is designed as a quick review — useful at the end of a unit to see which specific skill a student still needs more practice with, rather than as a first introduction to any of them. Watch for a pattern in errors: a student who struggles across the whole sheet needs broader review, while one who struggles only on sound-related items needs targeted phonics work specifically.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

Use this one as a check-in rather than a first practice sheet — if several sections feel hard, it's a signal to spend more time on our single-skill worksheets first (alphabet tracing, letter sounds, or ABC flashcards) before coming back to this mixed review.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

Because this sheet mixes several skill types, there's no single answer key to check against here. Instead, use it diagnostically: note which sections were completed confidently and which required help, and let that guide which single-skill worksheet to focus on next. A mixed result across sections is completely normal — few children are equally strong across recognition, sound, and formation at the same time.
