👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

Unlike our ABC and math mixed reviews, which check in on one subject area across several skills, this pack spans multiple subject areas entirely — letters, numbers, shapes, and colors together. It's best used as a broad, low-stakes snapshot of where a child is starting from, not a pass/fail readiness test. Split it across a few short sessions rather than one long sitting; the breadth is meant to reduce pressure, not add to it.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

This works well as a first activity before diving into our more focused single-subject worksheets — it gives a general sense of which area (letters, numbers, shapes, or colors) might need the most attention first. There's no need to complete it all in one sitting, and a low score on any one section just points to where to spend more time next, not a cause for concern.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

Because this pack spans multiple unrelated subject areas, there's no single combined answer key to check against. Use it purely as a starting snapshot: a child who breezes through the shapes section but struggles with letters simply needs more letter-focused practice next — see our alphabet tracing or counting and number recognition worksheets as natural next steps depending on what this pack reveals.
