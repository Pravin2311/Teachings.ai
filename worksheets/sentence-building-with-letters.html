👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

This worksheet sits a step beyond our individual letter-tracing sheets — students aren't just forming isolated letters, they're using that skill to build real words and short sentences, which requires spacing letters correctly within a word and words correctly within a sentence. Watch specifically for spacing issues (letters crammed together or words running into each other) since that's the new challenge this sheet introduces, beyond letter formation itself.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

This is a natural next step once your child traces letters comfortably (see our alphabet tracing and lowercase tracing worksheets). Read the finished word or sentence back together once it's written, connecting the physical writing to actual meaning rather than treating it as just another tracing exercise.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

The target words and sentences are fixed on the printed sheet, so accuracy there is straightforward to check against the print itself. Beyond correctness, look for reasonable spacing between letters within a word and between separate words — cramped or run-together writing is the most common early issue at this stage, more so than individual letter formation.
