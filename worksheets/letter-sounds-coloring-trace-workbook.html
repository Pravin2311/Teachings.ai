👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

This differs from our letter sounds worksheet, which focuses on matching letters to sounds directly with common mix-ups addressed. This workbook gives each letter its own full page: four objects whose names start with the letter's sound to color, a numbered directional-arrow model showing exactly how to form the letter, and four separate trace-and-color repetitions per page. Say each object's name aloud, emphasizing the initial sound, before students color and trace.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

The numbered arrows on each letter model are worth pointing out explicitly — they show the correct stroke order and direction, which matters more for later handwriting fluency than getting the shape "close enough." One letter a day is a comfortable pace; there's no need to rush through all 26 pages quickly.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

Each page's four objects are fixed and match the target letter — there's no ambiguity in what to color. Watch for a child tracing in the wrong stroke direction despite the numbered arrows; gently redirecting to follow the numbers, rather than just "getting the shape right," builds habits that transfer better to unguided writing later.
