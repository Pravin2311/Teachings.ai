👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

Like our ABC mixed review activity, this is designed as a check-in rather than a first introduction to any single skill — useful at the end of a numbers unit to see which specific area (formation, counting, or recognition) needs more focused practice. A student who struggles broadly across the sheet needs a general numbers review, while one who struggles only on the counting section specifically needs targeted one-to-one correspondence practice.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

Use this as a way to see where things stand rather than a first practice sheet — if several sections feel hard, spend more time on our single-skill worksheets first (number writing or counting and number recognition) before returning to this mixed review.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

Because this sheet mixes several skill types, there's no single answer key to check against. Use it diagnostically instead: note which sections were completed confidently and which needed help, and let that guide which single-skill worksheet to return to. Uneven results across sections are completely normal — few children are equally strong at formation, counting, and recognition all at the same time.
