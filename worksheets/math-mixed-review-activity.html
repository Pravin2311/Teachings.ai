👩‍🏫 Teacher Instructions

In the Classroom

Like our ABC and number mixed reviews, this one spans several skills rather than drilling one — useful at the end of a broader math unit to see where a student's specific gaps are. A student who struggles only on the addition section but does fine on counting and recognition needs targeted arithmetic practice, not a general math review.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Instructions

At Home

Use this to see the overall picture rather than as a first practice sheet — if several sections feel hard, our single-skill worksheets (counting and number recognition or addition and subtraction) are a better starting point before returning to this broader review.

✅ What Successful Completion Looks Like

Not a Traditional "Answer Key"

Since this sheet spans several skill types, there's no single answer key to check against here. Use it as a diagnostic: note which sections were completed confidently and which needed help, and let that guide which single-skill worksheet to focus on next. A mixed result is completely normal — few children are equally strong across counting, addition, and recognition at the same time.
