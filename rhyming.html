🔤 Parent Tip: Build Phonics & Listening Skills

After playing, say a word like “cat” and ask your child to name other words that rhyme. This strengthens phonemic awareness, vocabulary, and reading readiness—perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Rhyming Words

  1. Read the word shown, like cat.
  2. Tap the word that rhymes with it, like hat.
  3. Keep going to match more rhyming pairs.

What your child practices

Matching written rhyming words helps children notice that words that sound alike often share the same ending letters, like -at in cat, hat and bat. This is an early step toward reading word families.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Cover the first letter of each word and see what stays the same.
  • Make a list of words in the same family: cat, hat, mat, sat.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a word family?

A word family is a group of words with the same ending, like cat, hat and bat, which all end in -at.

How is this different from the Rhyming and Listening game?

This game uses written words. The listening game plays words aloud and uses pictures.

What age is it for?

It suits children from about 4 to 7.
