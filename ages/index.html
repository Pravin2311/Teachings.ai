Why Age-Specific Guidance Matters

Kids of the same age can develop at different paces, but knowing what's typical helps parents and teachers pick activities that are challenging without being frustrating. Each age hub below gathers reading, math, science, and creative activities matched to what most children can do at that stage.

How should I use these age hubs?

Start with your child's age, but don't be afraid to look at the hub above or below it too — development isn't a strict ladder, and kids are often ahead in some areas and still developing in others.

What if my child isn't hitting a milestone yet?

Most milestones cover a wide age range, so a few months of difference is normal. If you have ongoing concerns, it's always worth mentioning them to your child's pediatrician or teacher.
