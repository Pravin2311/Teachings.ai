🎮 How to Play: Nouns & Verbs Sorting

  1. Read each word on the screen.
  2. Drag it into the Noun box if it is a person, place, animal or thing, or into the Verb box if it is an action.
  3. Tap Submit to check your answers, and Try Again for a new set of words.

What your child practices

Sorting words into nouns and verbs is one of the first grammar ideas children learn. Thinking "Can I touch it or see it?" for nouns and "Can I do it?" for verbs helps children understand how sentences are built.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Act out the verbs together: run, jump, eat, read.
  • Make a silly sentence from one noun and one verb, like "The cat jumps."

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a noun and a verb?

A noun names a person, place, animal or thing, like dog, school or apple. A verb is an action word, like run, jump or eat.

What age is this grammar game for?

It suits children from about 5 to 8, when they start reading simple sentences.

What if my child puts a word in the wrong box?

Ask them to try the word in a sentence. If you can put "I can" in front of it, like "I can run", it is a verb.
