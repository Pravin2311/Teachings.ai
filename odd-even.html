What are Odd and Even Numbers?

🔵 Even numbers end with 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 (like 2, 10, 34).
🔴 Odd numbers end with 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9 (like 1, 9, 27).
✨ Tip: Even numbers can be split into two equal parts.
