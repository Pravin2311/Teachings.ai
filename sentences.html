🔤 Parent Tip: Build Language Confidence

Support early reading skills by encouraging your child to say each word aloud as they tap it. This multisensory approach—seeing, touching, and speaking—boosts vocabulary, improves word order understanding, and strengthens sentence formation for preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Sentence Builder

  1. Look at the mixed-up words.
  2. Tap them in the right order to build a sentence, like "Birds can fly."
  3. Tap Submit to check, and Next for another sentence.

What your child practices

Putting words in order teaches children how sentences work: who or what the sentence is about, then what they do. It also practices reading short, common words.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Read the finished sentence aloud together.
  • Ask your child to make a new sentence using one of the words.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of sentences are in the game?

Short, simple sentences like "I see you", "Birds can fly" and "Dogs run very fast".

What age is the sentence builder for?

It is designed for children from about 5 to 7 who are reading simple words.

How can we practice sentence building offline?

Write words on cards, mix them up and let your child arrange them into a sentence.
