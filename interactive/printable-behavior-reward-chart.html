How Many Behaviors to Track

Two or three is usually the limit — tracking too many behaviors at once dilutes focus and makes the chart harder to fill out consistently, which undermines the whole point of visible, easy tracking. Pick the behaviors that matter most right now, not every habit you'd eventually like to see improve.

Daily or Weekly Rewards?

For younger children, small daily acknowledgment (a sticker, a checkmark) tends to work better than only a delayed end-of-week reward, since the connection between behavior and recognition feels more immediate. Older children can often work toward a single larger end-of-week reward without losing motivation along the way.

✅ Leave Blank, Don't Mark a Miss

On a day the behavior doesn't happen, leave the box blank rather than marking it as a failure — a reward chart works best when it highlights and reinforces the wins rather than creating a visible record of what went wrong.
