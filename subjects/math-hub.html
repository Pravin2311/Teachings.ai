✅ Fluency, Not Just Correctness, Is the Real Signal

A child who answers correctly but slowly and with visible effort usually needs more practice at the current level before adding a new layer of difficulty on top — see Homeschool Scope and Sequence by Grade for typical benchmarks by grade band.

Frequently Asked Questions

What order should math skills be introduced in?

Roughly: counting and number recognition, then addition and subtraction, then multiplication and division, then fractions, then pre-algebra concepts. Each stage depends on real fluency in the one before it.

How do I know if my child is ready to move to the next math skill?

Fluency, not just correctness — a child answering slowly and with visible effort usually needs more practice at the current level first.

What if I'm not confident teaching math myself?

See our guide on teaching math when you're not a math person — staying one lesson ahead covers most of what's needed at the elementary and middle school level.
