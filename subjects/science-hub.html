Frequently Asked Questions

What science topics should young kids start with?

Living things and everyday observations tend to work best first, since they connect to what a child can see and touch, before moving to more abstract topics like space or technology.

Do I need lab equipment to teach science at home?

No — most early science concepts explore well through observation and simple household experiments. See our STEM classroom projects guide for low-equipment ideas.

How does this hub relate to the science section of a scope and sequence?

This hub organizes resources by topic; a scope and sequence tells you which topics are typically covered at which grade level. Use one to decide, the other to find resources.
