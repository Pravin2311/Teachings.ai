Build Community Awareness

After playing, talk about real-life helpers in your neighborhood: “Who helps if there’s a fire?” or “Where do doctors work?” This builds safety knowledge, vocabulary, and a sense of community—perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Community Places Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the card to reveal a community place, such as a fire station or library.
  2. Listen to its name.
  3. Talk about who works there, then tap Next.

What your child practices

Children learn about the places and people that keep a community running. This builds social studies vocabulary and helps children understand where to go for help, a book, a parcel or a check-up.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask "Have we been to this place? What did we do there?"
  • Visit a library, park or post office together and look for the helpers you talked about.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which places are included?

It includes the police station, fire station, hospital, school, library, post office, park, museum, city hall and airport.

Why teach children about community places?

Knowing what each place is for helps children feel confident in their neighborhood and know where to go for help.

Where can we learn more?

Each place has a learning page about who works there and what you can do when you visit.
