Privacy Policy

At Teachings.AI, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information while keeping the site safe and kid-friendly.

All activities on Teachings.AI are designed for children aged 2–10 and are safe to use without collecting personal information. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, making the site compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and performance improvements. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Our site may use Google Analytics or other kid-safe tools for anonymous data collection. Ads or affiliate links included in parent-focused sections help support Teachings.AI but do not collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy or practices, please reach out to us at support@teachings.ai.
