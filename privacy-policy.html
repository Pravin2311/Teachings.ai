Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

At Teachings.AI, we respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you or your child use the site, who collects it, and what choices you have.

The Short Version

  • There are no accounts, logins or sign-up forms. You can use every game and worksheet without telling us who you are.
  • We do not ask children for names, email addresses, photos or any other personal information.
  • The site is free because it shows ads from Google AdSense. Ads appear on most pages, including the games and learning pages children use.
  • Google Analytics and Google AdSense use cookies and similar technologies, as described below.

Information We Collect Directly

We do not collect personal information through the site. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us, for example your email address and message if you write to us. We use it only to reply to you.

If a technical error happens on the home page, a short error report is sent to us so we can fix it. It contains the page address, the browser type (the "user agent") and the error message. It does not contain a name or email address.

Information Stored on Your Device

A few pages save simple settings in your browser's local storage, such as whether background music is switched on and whether you have already seen a site announcement. These settings stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited, what kind of device and browser are used, roughly which country visits come from, and how long people stay. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives your IP address to do this. We see this information only as totals and reports, and we use it to decide which games and worksheets to improve.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Teachings.AI is supported by ads served by Google AdSense. Ads appear on most pages of the site, including game and learning pages. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies and may receive information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the page being viewed.

Because many of our visitors are young children, we ask Google to treat this site as directed to children. For sites treated this way, Google says it does not show personalized (interest-based) ads and limits the data it uses for advertising. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not choose individual ads, but we use AdSense controls to block ad categories that are not suitable for a family audience. If you ever see an ad that seems inappropriate, please tell us at the address below and include the page you were on.

Files and Fonts From Other Services

Printable worksheet PDFs are delivered through jsDelivr, a free file delivery network, and a few pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when they send you a file.

Children's Privacy

Teachings.AI is made for children aged about 2 to 10 and for the parents and teachers who help them learn. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and nothing on the site asks a child to type in personal details. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The games and worksheets still work without them.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • You can manage Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Links to Other Websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as health or education organisations and our social media pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please write to us at support@teachings.ai.
