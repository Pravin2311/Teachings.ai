Learn Electronic Gadgets

💡 Parent Tip: Connect Learning to Daily Life

After playing, point out electronic gadgets at home like tablets, phones, or remotes. Ask your child: “What does a tablet do?” or “How do we use a laptop?” This builds tech awareness, vocabulary, and real-world connections—perfect for curious preschool and kindergarten learners.
