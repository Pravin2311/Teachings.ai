Learn Electronic Gadgets

💡 Parent Tip: Connect Learning to Daily Life

After playing, point out electronic gadgets at home like tablets, phones, or remotes. Ask your child: “What does a tablet do?” or “How do we use a laptop?” This builds tech awareness, vocabulary, and real-world connections—perfect for curious preschool and kindergarten learners.

🎮 How to Play: Electronic Gadgets Scratch Card

  1. Scratch away the grey card to reveal a gadget.
  2. Listen to its name.
  3. Tap Next to see more, such as a laptop, camera, charger or headphones.

What your child practices

Children see gadgets every day but often do not know their names or what they do. Naming them builds vocabulary and gives grown-ups an easy way to start conversations about how technology works and how to use it safely.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask "What do we use this for?" after each gadget.
  • Point out the same gadgets at home and talk about which ones need charging or batteries.
