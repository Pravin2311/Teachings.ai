🎮 How to Play: Rhyming & Listening

  1. Tap Play Word to hear a word.
  2. Pick the picture whose name rhymes with it.
  3. Earn a point for each correct rhyme.

What your child practices

Hearing that words rhyme, like cat and hat, is part of phonological awareness, one of the strongest early predictors of reading success. This game works on listening only, with no reading needed.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Say the two words slowly and listen for the matching ending.
  • Make up silly rhymes together at bath time or in the car.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why does rhyming help with reading?

Rhyming helps children hear the parts of words, which makes it easier to learn how letters and sounds work together.

How is this different from the Rhyming Words game?

This game is about listening: children hear a word and choose a rhyming picture. Rhyming Words uses written words.

What age is this for?

It suits children from about 3 to 6.
