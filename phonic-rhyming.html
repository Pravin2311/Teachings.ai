Rhyming & Listening Game

Parenting Tips

  • Encourage your child to say the word aloud before choosing an answer.
  • Let them listen multiple times if needed; repetition helps learning.
  • Use real objects at home to reinforce rhyming words (e.g., cat & hat).
  • Praise effort and curiosity, not just correct answers.
  • Keep sessions short (5–10 minutes) for maximum attention.
