🔢 Parent Tip: Build Number Sense Through Play

Reinforce number learning by counting everyday objects: stairs, toys, or snacks. Ask, “How many blocks did you stack?” or “Can you show me 5 fingers?” This builds number recognition, one-to-one correspondence, and early math confidence in preschool and kindergarten kids.
