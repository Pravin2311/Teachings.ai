🔢 Parent Tip: Build Number Sense Through Play

Reinforce number learning by counting everyday objects: stairs, toys, or snacks. Ask, “How many blocks did you stack?” or “Can you show me 5 fingers?” This builds number recognition, one-to-one correspondence, and early math confidence in preschool and kindergarten kids.

🎮 How to Play: Numbers Scratch Card

  1. Scratch the grey card to reveal a number.
  2. Listen to the number's name.
  3. Hold up that many fingers, then tap Next for the next number.

What your child practices

Children practice recognizing written numerals and matching them to their spoken names. Holding up fingers or counting objects for each number connects the symbol to an amount, which is the heart of early number sense.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Count real objects to match each number: 3 spoons, 5 blocks.
  • Ask "What number comes next?" before tapping Next.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What numbers does the game cover?

It covers the numbers 0 to 10, each shown large and spoken aloud.

What is the difference between counting and number recognition?

Counting is saying numbers in order and matching them to objects. Number recognition is knowing what the written numeral looks like. Children need both.

Where can we practice counting next?

Try the Counting with Objects game, where children count pictures and tap the right number.
