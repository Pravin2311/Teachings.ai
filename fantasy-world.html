🪄 Parent Tip: Encourage Imagination & Storytelling

After playing, ask your child to create a story using the fantasy characters they learned. “What does a dragon eat?” or “Where does a unicorn live?” This boosts creativity, language skills, and narrative thinking—perfect for preschool and kindergarten learners.
