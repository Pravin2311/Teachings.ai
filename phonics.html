🎯 Parenting Tips: How to Teach Phonics at Home

Support your child’s reading journey with these simple, research-backed tips.

  • 1. Say sounds, not letter names: Say "/s/" not "ess". This helps blending.
  • 2. Blend slowly: Stretch words: "ssssaaaat" → "sat".
  • 3. Use gestures: Rub arms for /s/ (snake), tap head for /t/.
  • 4. Keep it fun: Turn words into games: “Can you sip the magic potion?”
  • 5. Revisit daily: 5–10 minutes a day beats 30 minutes once a week.
  • 6. Praise effort: “You sounded that out perfectly!”
  • 7. Read together: Use simple books with CVC words (e.g., “Pat the Cat”).

Tip from teachings.ai: Consistency beats perfection. Celebrate every sound!
