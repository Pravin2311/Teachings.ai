Unplanned indoor days need a different kind of resource — something to grab quickly, not a season's worth of planned content.

1. How This Differs from the Seasonal Guides

Each seasonal guide plans ahead for a whole season of activities. This guide is a contingency plan for the specific, unplanned moment a child is suddenly stuck inside — quick to reach for, not something to schedule in advance.

2. What Makes an Activity a Good Fit

Activities that need minimal setup and materials already on hand work best — a sudden indoor day isn't the time to discover a project requires a supply run first. Keeping a small, dedicated bin of go-to supplies (paper, tape, a deck of cards) makes this even easier.

4. Keeping Screen-Free Easy to Reach For

✅ Make the Alternative as Easy as the Screen

It doesn't have to be — having a short list of no-prep, screen-free options ready in advance makes it just as easy to reach for one of those as it is to reach for a screen. See our screen-free games guide for more structured options that build specific skills.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from the four seasonal activity guides?

Seasonal guides plan ahead for a whole season; this is a contingency plan for a sudden unplanned indoor day.

What makes an activity a good fit for a sudden indoor day?

Minimal setup and materials already on hand — no supply run required.

Should screen time be the default for an unexpected indoor day?

It doesn't have to be — a ready short list of screen-free options makes them just as easy to reach for.
